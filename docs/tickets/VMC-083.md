# VMC-083 — M3 gate findings

Status: In progress
Milestone: M3
Parent: [VMC-078](VMC-078.md).

The October 30–31 gate run ([VMC-078](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026))
found these on the native mob core. Its five crashes are fixed (124d6349,
cd53a081, 5447d2a4, 2464001a, b323cdaf). Evidence files are in
`.local/client-tests/M3-gate-573bce09/`.

## Correctness

1. **Mobs vanish without a trace.** Two summoned husks underwater (after 20
   and 39 s) and endermen near grass (within 20 to 40 s, twice) left every
   entity table with no drop, no conversion and no log line.
   - Fixed: a death in a mob's own tick or timers (the sun, a fall, rain,
     drowning) never reached its owner's death queue. The mob finished its
     death animation and was removed without loot or orbs. Every body change
     now queues its death (`Store.put_body`), and the owner drops the queue at
     the end of its entity phase (`RegionData.drop_deaths`). A reproduction
     over a real connection lost five sun-burned zombies and a skeleton with no
     item; the region test is "a sheep that dies of its fall drops its loot".
   - Not found: what killed the gate's husks. A husk under water keeps its air
     and converts on time in a region test with no player near. Despawning
     cannot take a mob within 32 blocks of a non-spectator player. The client
     run rechecks with drops visible.
2. **Possible missing floor cells: not a defect.** The commands log shows the
   holes were creeper craters. "Replace air" refills after summoned creepers
   exploded filled 5, then 3, then 6 cells, and natural creepers (item 3)
   blew more. Light and block reads over a filled sky platform are correct
   in the live dataset.
3. **Monsters on a lit platform at noon: not reproduced.** A region test
   fills a 41 × 41 stone platform at y 149 and spawns 400 ticks at noon with
   the player on it. Monsters spawn in its shadow below and none on top
   ("at noon nothing spawns on a lit sky platform, only in its shadow"). The
   live dataset's light above the arena is 15. Vanilla does spawn monsters in
   daylight during a thunderstorm (`isThundering` darkens by 10), and the
   run's weather was not recorded. A spawn now reads a chunk whose light is
   not yet known as fully lit. Before, unknown light read as darkness, so
   monsters could spawn there.
4. **A husk's hit gives no Hunger: not reproduced.** A connection test has a
   summoned husk hit a survival player at normal difficulty, and the player
   gets Hunger ("a bare-handed husk's hit makes the player hungry").
   Rechecked in the client run.
5. **Endermen take no damage from rain.** Fixed: a water-sensitive type
   (blaze, enderman, snow golem, strider) is hurt in rain as in water
   (`isInWaterOrRain`). An enderman's hurt without a living attacker then
   teleports it.
6. **Endermen never pick up blocks: the test setup.**
   `EndermanTakeBlockGoal` picks cells from the enderman's feet up to three
   blocks above. On a flat grass field its feet stand above the grass, so
   vanilla's endermen take nothing there either. The client run uses grass at
   feet height (a step or wall); a region trial with a grass ring at its
   feet saw one taken within six seconds.
7. **Turtles fed seagrass do not breed: not reproduced.** Two turtles in
   love breed in a region test, and one carries an egg ("two turtles in love
   breed: one carries an egg"). The gate's first feeding was uncertain.
   Rechecked in the client run.
8. **An axolotl does not hunt: not reproduced.** In a region pool an axolotl
   two blocks from a cod targets and kills it within two seconds. The gate's
   cod may have been out of its eight-block, in-water sensing at the scans.
   Rechecked in the client run.
9. **The wandering trader does not drink milk at dawn: not reproduced.** An
   invisible trader drinks milk by day in a region test. Vanilla drinks only
   while `skyDarken < 4`, and a thunderstorm darkens the day to 5. The
   client run clears the weather first.
10. **A drowned's zombie nautilus is not mounted.** Riding is not modelled
    (VMC-046), so no jockey rides; the nautilus spawns as a companion. Out of
    scope here.

Known from the native port and not yet seen in play: despawn checks drawing
from a shared random rather than each mob's (a parity detail, not a
distribution change), an idle reset that leaves `no_action`, and
`Mob.restore` capping health at the base maximum.

## Commands the gate needed

- `/summon` takes entity data. It reads a mob's `equipment` (item `id` and
  `count`), `CustomName`, `NoAI`, `PersistenceRequired`, `Health` and a
  zombie's `IsBaby`, and refuses other keys by name. With data, a mob does
  not finish its spawn, as vanilla's `initialize` is false.
- `/effect` takes Strength and Weakness: their attack-damage modifiers
  (`HostileFacts.effect_modifiers`) apply to a player's melee hit.
- `/execute` runs its `in`, `positioned`, `positioned as`, `at`, `as`
  (players) and `run` branches (`Commands.Execute`). The tree keeps only
  those branches. `/execute in minecraft:the_nether run tp …` is the
  console's teleport into another dimension.
- `/item replace` remains missing; `/summon`'s `equipment` covers what the
  gate wanted it for.
