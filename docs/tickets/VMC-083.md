# VMC-083 — M3 gate findings

Status: Planned
Milestone: M3
Parent: [VMC-078](VMC-078.md).

The October 30–31 gate run ([VMC-078](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026))
found these on the native mob core. Its five crashes are fixed (124d6349,
cd53a081, 5447d2a4, 2464001a, b323cdaf); what follows is open. Evidence files
are in `.local/client-tests/M3-gate-573bce09/`.

## Correctness

1. **Mobs vanish without a trace.** Two summoned husks underwater (after 20
   and 39 s) and endermen near grass (within 20 to 40 s, twice) left every
   entity table with no drop, no conversion and no log line, with the player
   near. A third husk converted on schedule. Suspect: despawning, which draws
   from a shared random rather than each mob's own, or a native removal path.
2. **Possible missing floor cells.** The server read air at 300..301,
   149..150, 303..305 after the floor there was filled more than once; the
   player fell through to y 49, and other falls and a walk through a glass
   wall may be the same. Not isolated: reproduce with `/fill` and block reads
   before anything else.
3. **Monsters on a lit platform at noon.** Two creepers and two spiders
   appeared on an open sky platform at noon within 30 s; light was not
   checked. Vanilla's `isDarkEnoughToSpawn` refuses there.
4. **A husk's hit gives no Hunger.** The native husk emits the effect on a
   bare-handed hit; it never reached the player (or the summoned husk was
   holding an item — check first).
5. **Endermen take no damage from rain** (20 s in rain the server reports;
   a phantom in the same rain did not burn, so the rain is seen).
6. **Endermen never pick up blocks.**
7. **Turtles fed seagrass do not breed** or lay eggs (60 s).
8. **An axolotl does not hunt** a cod beside it (33 s).
9. **The wandering trader does not drink milk at dawn**; it stays invisible.
10. **A drowned's zombie nautilus is not mounted**: the nautilus spawns and
    stands alone.

Known from the native port and not yet seen in play: despawn checks drawing
from a shared random, an idle reset that leaves `no_action`, and
`Mob.restore` capping health at the base maximum.

## Commands the gate needed

`/summon` with entity data, `/effect` with Strength, `/item replace`,
console `/tp` into another dimension and `/execute in`. Without them baby
and armoured zombies, a trident drowned, sized slimes and loot pickup could
not be set up.

## Not yet observed

Ghast fireball return, guardian thorns, bat hanging, dolphin feeding, leap
and drying, fish and axolotl bucket capture, axolotl playing dead, shulker
closing, arrow glance and shell drop, sheep grazing, cats avoiding players
and taking cod, lectern trade loss, golem cracks and poppy. A later client
run covers them once the items above are fixed.

Acceptance: each item above fixed with its oracle or region test, or shown
to match vanilla; the not-yet-observed scenarios pass in one client run.
