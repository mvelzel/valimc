# VMC-043e9 — Aquatic spawn-pack state

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-042a](VMC-042a.md), [VMC-043e2](VMC-043e2.md),
[VMC-043e6](VMC-043e6.md), [VMC-043e7](VMC-043e7.md).

Keep a natural/generated spawn pack's identity through admission and apply
its shared finalization state. Axolotls share their two common variants and
make the later pack members babies; turtles use their pack's baby chance.
Salmon keep their individual size draws, and schooling fish retain their
initial leader and tropical fish's common variant. The natural-spawn kernel's existing school
decision must remain authoritative instead of being rolled independently
again when the body initializes.

Acceptance:

- Ordered pack finalization matches native 26.3 fixtures for axolotls,
  turtles, cod, salmon and tropical fish, including group resets, variants,
  ages, school links/counts and the applicable random transitions.
- Natural and generated spawn results retain pack boundaries across section
  lines. A block spawner's independent attempts never become one pack.
- Pack state is bounded to the owning group's admission callback; it is
  neither a global process nor a new durable permit/receipt system. The
  admitted live body keeps transient school state while ordinary body records
  retain the resulting age/variant and random state.
- Existing individual/bucket/command spawns keep their native behavior;
  Rust/NIF metadata changes have Elixir parity coverage and affected checks.
- Actual-client observations stay deferred at the user's request.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `NaturalSpawner`,
`AgeableMob`, `AbstractSchoolingFish`, `Salmon`, `TropicalFish`,
`Axolotl` and `Turtle`. A salmon draws its own size and has no ageable baby
state: `AbstractFish` extends `WaterAnimal`/`PathfinderMob` in this pin.
Squid/dolphin age handling and turtle-egg placement particles remain separate
aquatic gaps rather than being implied complete by this slice.

Implementation: the pure spawn kernel returns the first admitted member's
index as each pack's identity (no pack for block-spawner attempts). Natural
results keep their original order across sections, and generated results
scope their pack to the source chunk. The owning group finalizes modeled
aquatics together before publication, drawing from each section's existing
AI level random. A tropical school's decision comes from the kernel's
separate finalizer stream and is not drawn again. Shared state lives only
in this callback. The ordinary AI effect reducer applies leader increments;
live bodies retain their school links through admission, while durable
records keep only the normal age, variant and random state. Reloading drops
school links as vanilla does.

  returned group object: 640 cases (five types, 64 seeds, natural and
  generated reasons, two packs each). Another 256 tropical cases replace

  the live kernel's authority and the remaining native random transitions.
  All 896 cases pass, including each admission prefix and final leader counts.

  leader index; the NIF's pack identity matches it. Its 56,800 spawn ticks
  (12,674 mobs), 73,200 block-spawner ticks (343 mobs), 138 generated
  creatures and existing despawn/sky cases pass. Every block-spawner result
  carries no pack identity.

  retention, record round trips and the bucket socket regression.
- Both changed Rust crates pass their tests (124 core tests); workspace

- The affected run passes 108 of 109 stages in 376 seconds, with 105 cache

  Logs: `.local/m3-aquatic-pack-changed.log` and
