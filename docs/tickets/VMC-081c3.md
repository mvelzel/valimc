# VMC-081c3 — Unload what the view left, survive a lost snapshot

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3, at the user's request
Parent: [VMC-081c](VMC-081c.md).

Capture 13 (081c1 on the VPS): generation kept up with the player, but the
player's group loaded 333 sections and unloaded none. In 70 seconds of flying
over saved terrain it grew from 4,004 to 18,632 loaded chunks (five times the
view) and 3,047 entities, and ran at 7.5 TPS (entities 38 ms and blocks 16 ms
a sampled tick, collections 8 s a minute). Seconds after the capture the
kernel killed the server for its memory: 3.09 GB resident against the
container's 3 GiB, of which the VM's own heaps and binaries were about 460 MB;
the rest is the loaded chunks' native stores. Capture 12 had the same growth
(282 sections) with 203 unloads.

## Why sections stayed

The harness did not reproduce it: out-and-back flights over saved terrain
loaded and unloaded 440 sections each, on two cores. On the VPS the group ran
3 to 10 ticks a second while sections loaded and merged into it about five
times a second.

- **Tickets withdrawn from nobody.** The session remembers which group it told
  each section's view tickets. When the section's group could not be reached
  at a view update (admitting, or moving between groups), it recorded none,
  forgetting who held the tickets; once the view left, no withdrawal was sent
  and the section kept the player's ticket while the player stayed. It now
  keeps who was told last, and sends a withdrawal to the group the directory
  names now (the one told may have retired). This is the likely cause; the next
  capture confirms it.
- **Diagnostics.** Each profile's workload now counts a group's sections by
  what keeps them (`RegionData.unload_blocker/1`): the ticket types held, the
  first work in flight, or `idle` (waiting out the 10 s delay).

## A lost snapshot

The kill skipped the graceful stop, so the generation snapshot (the proto
chunks around the saved area) was lost. A decoration whose 3 × 3 held a chunk
handed over could not run, so every chunk beside the saved area was made alone
(over fresh terrain, with only its own decoration), and so was each next ring
outward, indefinitely, at about five times the work.

Chunks handed over no longer block decorations: a decoration runs over fresh
terrain in their place, whose writes it drops, as vanilla's features beside a
saved chunk are cut at its border. Only a chunk wanted again after it was
handed over is made alone; a failed chunk still blocks as before.

Acceptance:

- A capture where the player's group stays near its view's size and the
  workload's unload counts show what holds the rest.
- After a lost snapshot, generation beside saved chunks runs as usual.

Checks: frontier (new: beside a saved area, decorated as usual), scheduler
