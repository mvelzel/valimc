# VMC-071 — New terrain reaches a moving player sooner

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M2 real worlds (player-visible)
Depends on: [VMC-070](VMC-070.md) (view distance 10), [VMC-024](VMC-024.md) (live
terrain), [VMC-022](VMC-022.md) (light across region borders).

The VMC-070 client run found that, at view distance 10, the last hundred chunks
of the view took 12–15 s to arrive after a 294-block flight over new terrain.
Chunk sending was not the limit (vanilla's pacing allows 64 chunks per tick):
every chunk waited for its region, and every region waited for the one worldgen
state of the seed. That state compiles its samplers lazily and keeps bounded pure
caches, so it sits behind a lock; regions loading at once (16 for a fresh view)
generated their 64 chunks one region after another, in no particular order.

Scope: generate a view's regions in parallel and nearest first, and keep the
connection from being flooded while they load. Generating only the chunks a view
needs (a region still generates all 64) is a larger change, listed below.

Acceptance:

- A seed has one worldgen state per dirty CPU scheduler; a call checks a free one
  out (atomics, no process), waiting briefly while all are busy. A region generates
  its missing chunks in batches of 16, up to four at once. The chunks equal one
  state's, byte for byte.
- A view's new regions start loading at once, nearest to the player first.
- A region publishes border light changes (`light_update`) only for chunks a client
  has asked for since it loaded; later requests carry the current light. Loading
  fresh terrain no longer fills the connection's mailbox towards the 256-message
  overload limit with updates for chunks nobody has.
- A matched benchmark (`bench/fresh_terrain.exs`) shows the time to the first new
  chunk and to the whole view after a teleport into fresh terrain clearly shorter
  than before, with raw results kept.
- Player-visible: flying over new terrain at render distance 16 (view 10), the view
  fills in clearly faster than in the VMC-070 run.

Design:

- `Valimc.World.Generator`: the pool `{states, busy}` in `:persistent_term` per
  seed; `checked_out/2` takes a slot with `:atomics.compare_exchange/4`;
  `columns/2` splits chunks into batches with `Task.async_stream/3` (linked to the
  region owner, bounded at four). The worldgen compiler is unchanged.
- `Replication.update_view/1` admits new regions sorted by distance from the
  player's chunk.
- `RegionData` keeps `served` (requested chunks, at most the region's 64);
  `LightHalo.adopt/2` filters its published frames by it. Edits' own light updates
  are unchanged.
- Counters `worldgen_chunks`, `worldgen_microseconds`,
  `column_persist_microseconds`, `region_loads` and `region_load_microseconds`
  show where a load spends its time.

Limits:

- A region still generates all 64 chunks before serving any; a fresh view of 473
  chunks generates 1,024. Generating per chunk, nearest first, needs regions that
  hold ungenerated columns (blocks, light and ticks around them).
- The pool holds up to one state's caches per dirty scheduler (bounded at 4,096
  chunk biomes and 65,536 carver biomes each, roughly 15 MB when full).
- Generation cost itself (about 6 ms per chunk on one thread, VMC-024) is
  unchanged.

Progress (October 21, 2026):

- Before: of a fresh join at view distance 10 (16 regions, 1,024 chunks), the
  regions' generation calls added up to 60–730 s of waiting on the one state, while
  encoding took 0.6–2.8 s and persisting columns 0.1 s; generation was the whole
  delay.
- `bench/fresh_terrain.exs`, seed 20261020, three 512-block teleports per run,
  runs alternating between `04ca075` (counters and benchmark added) and this
  slice on the same host under other sessions' load (1-minute load 5–7):

  | | first new chunk | whole view (473) |
  |---|---|---|
  | one locked state, `04ca075` | 3.9–4.9 s (median 4.1) | 7.3–8.5 s (median 7.7) |
  | pool, batches, nearest first | 0.74–1.08 s (median 0.86) | 2.7–3.5 s (median 3.1) |

  Joins: 6.5–6.7 s → 2.1–2.7 s to the whole view. Under heavier load (25–45) the
  same comparison gave 3.7–7.0 s → 0.76–1.08 s and 7.3–14.1 s → 2.9–4.6 s. Raw
  results: `bench/results/fresh-terrain-*.json` (ignored).
- The connection's largest mailbox during a fresh view: 47–82 messages before,
  107–132 with parallel generation alone (almost all border `light_update`
  batches for chunks nobody had), 44–74 with light updates only for served
  chunks. The overload limit is 256.
- Tests: `test/generator_pool_test.exs` (two regions' 80 chunks from concurrent
  batched callers equal one state's), `test/region_light_test.exs` (a neighbour
  sends a border light update for a chunk a client requested and none for one
  nobody requested; fails without the filter). Fast suite 1,111 and socket suites
  571 pass; the official-codec TCP oracle passes plain and encrypted (now sending
  `client_information`, 117 chunks).
