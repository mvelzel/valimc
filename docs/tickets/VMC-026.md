# VMC-026 — Nether, End and portals

Status: In progress
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-024](VMC-024.md) (Nether and End terrain), [VMC-025](VMC-025.md)
(the decoration pipeline and live decoration).

ValiMC has one dimension, the overworld. Vanilla's normal world preset has
three: the overworld, the Nether (0 to 256, noise filling the lower 128,
coordinate scale 8, no sky light, a bedrock ceiling) and the End (0 to 256, the
main island with its spikes, outer islands past 1,000 blocks). This ticket
brings the other two, first their generation as parity-tested foundations,
then live worlds that players reach through portals.

Subtickets:

| Ticket | Slice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [VMC-026a](VMC-026a.md) | Nether and End generation beyond terrain: the dimensions' facts, decoration at their heights, every Nether and End feature type (with the End spikes' crystals and gateway exits), fire and chorus shape updates | Foundation |
| [VMC-026b](VMC-026b.md) | Live Nether and End worlds: dimension lifecycle and storage, generation and decoration per dimension, the client's dimension switch, Nether and End portals, player and entity transitions | In progress |

Acceptance (parent):

- Nether and End chunks equal vanilla's for several seeds and areas (terrain,
  features; structures with VMC-025e/f) when both decorate the same chunks in
  the same order.
- A player lights a Nether portal, travels to the Nether and back (portal
  search and creation at the scaled position), enters the End through an end
  portal and arrives on the obsidian platform, and leaves through the exit
  portal, as a vanilla client sees it.
- Generation throughput per dimension is benchmarked against vanilla and
  Pumpkin on matched chunks and stays in Pumpkin's range.

## What VMC-026b needs

Storage, owners, generation, light and the client's dimension switch are
VMC-026b1's. Still needed:

- The entities features add (the End spikes' crystals, `ProtoChunk.entities`)
  spawned through the entity model (VMC-067).
- Portals: nether portal frames and lighting (`PortalShape`), travel with the
  coordinate scale, the portal search and creation (`PortalForcer` and portal
  POIs), end portal frames and eyes (strongholds, VMC-025f), the arrival
  platform (`EndPlatformFeature.createEndPlatform`, already in VMC-026a),
  end gateways (their exits; gateways without one search for an exit on first
  use), the exit portal (`EndPodiumFeature`).
- The dragon fight (`EndDragonFight`): the dragon, respawning spikes with
  invulnerable crystals and beam targets (the spike feature's options VMC-026a
  refuses), new gateways after each kill, the exit portal. It may become its
  own subticket.
- Fire and fluids: decoration places fire without a tick (fire spreads and
  burns out only once ticked; netherrack keeps it burning) and marks lava
  springs for fluid ticks, which need fluid flow (VMC-031).
