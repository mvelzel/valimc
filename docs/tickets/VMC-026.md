# VMC-026 — Nether, End and portals

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-024](VMC-024.md) (Nether and End terrain), [VMC-025](VMC-025.md)
(the decoration pipeline and live decoration).

ValiMC has one dimension, the overworld. Vanilla's normal world preset has
three: the overworld, the Nether (0 to 256, noise filling the lower 128,
coordinate scale 8, no sky light, a bedrock ceiling) and the End (0 to 256, the
main island with its spikes, outer islands past 1,000 blocks). This ticket
brings the other two, first their generation as parity-tested foundations,
then live worlds that players reach through portals.

Subtickets:

| Ticket | Slice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [VMC-026a](VMC-026a.md) | Nether and End generation beyond terrain: the dimensions' facts, decoration at their heights, every Nether and End feature type (with the End spikes' crystals and gateway exits), fire and chorus shape updates | Done |
| [VMC-026b](VMC-026b.md) | Live Nether and End worlds: dimension lifecycle and storage, generation and decoration per dimension, the client's dimension switch, Nether and End portals, player and entity transitions | Done |
| [VMC-026c](VMC-026c.md) | Items through nether portals, end portals and gateways; cross-dimension item handoffs; vanilla's velocity turn on arrival | Done |

Acceptance (parent):

- Nether and End chunks equal vanilla's for several seeds and areas (terrain,
  features; structures with VMC-025e/f) when both decorate the same chunks in
  the same order.
- A player lights a Nether portal, travels to the Nether and back (portal
  search and creation at the scaled position), enters the End through an end
  portal and arrives on the obsidian platform, and leaves through the exit
  portal, as a vanilla client sees it.
- Generation throughput per dimension is benchmarked against vanilla and
  Pumpkin on matched chunks and stays in Pumpkin's range.

## Remaining

The Nether and the End are live, with nether portals, end portals (a
generated stronghold's portal room too, VMC-026b3) and gateways, and items
travel through them (VMC-026c). Generation is benchmarked per dimension
(below). What remains of M2 is the client check of a generated stronghold's
portal room.

Moved to M3, as they need its systems:

- The dragon fight (`EndDragonFight`): the dragon, the spikes' End crystals
  (`ProtoChunk.entities`, kept in proto logs) and their respawn, the exit
  portal (`EndPodiumFeature`) and its activation (the End's way out after the
  credits), and new gateways after each kill. Mobs, combat and explosions.
- Eyes of ender thrown into the air towards the nearest stronghold
  (`EnderEyeItem.use`, the `EyeOfEnder` entity): projectiles.
- Experience orbs through portals, once orbs move ([VMC-034n4c](VMC-034n4c.md));
  mobs through portals.

## Benchmark

Decoration per dimension is VMC-026a's: the Nether level with Pumpkin, the
End's outer islands below it.

Terrain, October 21, 2026: the matched terrain bench of VMC-024, now per
dimension. `TerrainOracle bench OUT nether|end X0 Z0`,
`bench/worldgen_terrain.exs`, and a local Pumpkin harness kept out of the tree
(`valimc_dimension_terrain`, like VMC-024's: Pumpkin b0c48b2, release profile,
`RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1`). Seed 20261020, the 64 chunks of an 8 × 8 area (the
Nether's from chunk (0, 0), the End's outer islands from (100, 0)), one
thread, rounds alternating vanilla, Pumpkin and ValiMC at load 15 to 32. The
raw results and the run logs are ignored in `bench/results/026-terrain-*`.
Pumpkin's carved blocks equal ValiMC's (0 of 2,097,152 differ, in every
round), so both do the same work. Median ms per chunk, round by round:

| Area | Step | ValiMC | Pumpkin | Vanilla |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nether | noise fill | 0.38, 0.30, 0.57 | 0.52, 0.51, 0.33 | 1.83, 1.13, 1.41 |
| Nether | through carvers | 2.76, 2.03, 1.90 | 4.00, 3.78, 2.62 | |
| End | noise fill | 0.52, 0.37, 0.36 | 0.38, 0.37, 0.40 | 0.59, 0.39, 1.23 |
| End | through carvers | 0.85, 0.67, 0.42 | 1.83, 1.78, 1.94 | |

The End's rows are the three rounds at load 20 to 23; three earlier ones at
load 16 to 31 swung from 0.47 to 1.74 ms in ValiMC's fill alone (in two of
them more than its whole step through carvers), so they are left out as load
noise. Vanilla through carvers runs without a world region, so it looks
carver biomes up for every chunk and is not matched, as for VMC-024. ValiMC
is in Pumpkin's range in both dimensions: level on the noise fill, faster
through carvers.

Elsewhere: respawn points in other dimensions (beds, anchors) are VMC-040's;
fire spread and burning out, and the lava springs decoration marks for fluid
ticks, are VMC-031's.
