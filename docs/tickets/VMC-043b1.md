# VMC-043b1 — What hostiles share, and the zombie family

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-041b](VMC-041b.md), [VMC-037n](VMC-037n.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md). Zombies, husks and zombie villagers, and
what every overworld hostile shares with them:

- **Spawning** (`finalizeSpawn`): the follow range bonus and handedness,
  loot pickup, babies, chicken jockeys' draws, door breaking, armour and
  weapons with their enchantments, Halloween heads, the zombie's
  reinforcement, knockback and leader bonuses; a husk's second loot pickup
  draw and camel husk; a zombie villager's type by biome and profession.
  Every way a mob appears runs it on the mob's first tick.
- **Local difficulty** (`DifficultyInstance`) from the overworld clock and
  moon. Chunks do not count inhabited time yet: it reads as 0.
- **Babies**: smaller (their own dimensions everywhere a body's size
  counts), faster, two and a half times the experience.
- **Burning in daylight** with helmet wear, from the light where the mob's
  eyes are.
- **Loot pickup** and equipment drops on death with their drop chances.
- **Hits**: burning zombies set their target alight, husks make it hungry;
  reinforcements on hard.
- **Drowning**: zombies turn into drowned, husks into zombies.

Acceptance:

- `finalizeSpawn` of each family type matches the oracle's fixtures
  (`hostile_spawns`) over difficulties, local difficulties, reasons and
  biomes.
- Each type's ticks in the oracle's scenes match (`hostile_ai`): a baby
  chasing, a zombie burning by day with and without a helmet, a husk's
  hungry hit, a zombie converting in water, a zombie picking up a sword.
- Loot of the husk and zombie villager tables matches (`loot`).

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

**Spawning.** `Valimc.AI.Spawn` runs `Mob.finalizeSpawn` for a mob whose
`Mob.finalize` holds its spawn reason (natural, spawner, chunk generation,
`/summon`; `Body.spawned/2`), on its first tick before its goals, drawing
from the section's level random and the mob's own; families give theirs
(`Mobs.finalizer/1`). Armour (`populateDefaultEquipmentSlots`) and spawn
enchantments (`EnchantmentsByCostWithDifficulty`, NIF `loot_enchant` over
the loot engine's `selectEnchantment`) are shared. `Valimc.AI.Mobs.Zombie`
holds the family: goals, finalize, own data (baby, conversion, villager
data), baby dimensions and voice, hits and the drowning tracker. A new
jockey chicken is finalized from the zombie's draws (its variant itself is
VMC-043c's).

**What hostiles share.** `Living.LocalDifficulty` (inhabited time reads 0);
`Body.dimensions/1` everywhere a body's size counts (the kernel, the AI,
explosions, projectile targets); travel reads movement speed with its
modifiers. `Valimc.AI.Daylight` burns undead mobs after the move from the
light at their eyes (NIF `region_light`, the AI world's `light`, sky
darkening and the day timeline's `monsters_burn` from the facts), wears
helmets instead, and lets rain put fires out. `baseTick`'s burning now
runs before a mob thinks, and a hit its damage cooldown turns away still
restarts its idle time, as vanilla's. `Valimc.AI.Pickup` is `Mob`'s
looting with `equipItemIfPossible` (equip sounds from the facts);
`Combat` drops equipment on death by drop chance, Looting and wear, and
baby zombies give two and a half times the experience. A melee hit's
family extras (`Mobs.hit/2`) carry a burning zombie's fire and a husk's
hunger into `Player.Hurt`. A drowning conversion replaces the mob with a
new one keeping what `ConversionType.SINGLE` keeps.

Facts: `HostileOracle facts` (`priv/registry/hostile_facts.json`, stage
`hostile_facts`): armour by slot and material, the default drop chance, the
spawn enchanting provider, baby dimensions, equippable items' slots and
sounds, when monsters burn, villager types by biome and the professions.
`EntityOracle` gained the hostiles' entity data fields; `MobAiOracle`,
`CombatOracle` and `LootOracle` the family's types. `MobAiOracle`'s probe
lets a family oracle prepare mobs and record more per tick, and its
fighters record what a hit adds.

Checks: stage `hostile_spawns` (`HostileOracle spawns`,
`scripts/check_hostile_spawns.exs`): 3240 `finalizeSpawn` results of
zombies, husks and zombie villagers equal vanilla's (equipment,
enchantments, drop chances, flags, attributes, health, villager data,
jockeys, both randoms), and each one's equipment dropped on its death.
Stage `hostile_ai` (`HostileOracle cases`, `check_mob_ai.exs`): 2901 mob
ticks of 7 scenes equal bit for bit: a baby zombie chasing, zombies by day
(burning, a helmet wearing down, one in the shade) beside a husk, a husk's
hungry hits, a burning zombie's hits, a zombie villager chasing, a zombie
picking up a sword and a helmet, and a husk drowning into a zombie.
`loot`, `mob_ai`, `nether_mobs`, `mob_travel`, `combat`, `projectiles` and
the facts stages pass. `test/hostile_region_test.exs`: in an owner a
summoned zombie finishes its spawn and burns at noon in the open, not under
a roof.

Not modelled yet: reinforcements on hard, breaking doors (its chance is
drawn), curing zombie villagers (with VMC-044a's villagers), camel husks'
own spawn draws, chunks' inhabited time, riding (VMC-046), the rain's
hiss as it puts a fire out.

Client scenario (phase-end handoff, with VMC-043b's): survival, normal
difficulty, at noon on open ground. `/summon zombie ~5 ~ ~`: it catches
fire within seconds and burns; `/summon zombie ~5 ~ ~` again and give it an
iron helmet (drop one beside a zombie that picks up loot, or summon one
with `/summon` until one spawns with a helmet): it does not burn. `/summon
husk`: it never burns, and its hits leave the Hunger effect. A baby zombie
(summon until one is small) runs faster than an adult. Drop an iron sword
beside a zombie that picks up loot: it takes it and holds it. Put a husk
in a pit of water two deep: after about 45 seconds it turns into a zombie.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
