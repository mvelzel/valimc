# VMC-034n3 — Owned XP orb materialization

Status: Planned. Depends on [pending awards](VMC-034n2.md); part of [earned output](VMC-034n.md).

Turn a durable pending XP obligation into bounded region-owned orb records exactly
once. Preserve the recipe-grant obligation until the player durably acknowledges
it. Live earned-output admission still waits for complete orb pickup/player XP and
recipe delivery; emitting packets alone cannot settle an award.

Acceptance:

- Native differential splitting, entity metadata and spawn/update/removal codecs
  for Java 26.3. Use persisted UUIDs with replaceable network IDs. Represent merged
  value/count explicitly; do not allocate one task/process per orb or XP point.
- Atomically install orb records and advance the matching pending obligation in
  the leased region snapshot/journal. Replay, capacity refusal, owner replacement
  and interrupted writes must preserve `pending XP + materialized XP` exactly.
  Zero-XP awards still retain recipe delivery. No unacknowledged obligation removal.
- Bound record count/bytes, pure worker work and replication batches. Cross-region
  spawn poses require fenced ownership handoff before effects are exposed. ETS
  observations cannot authorize transfers or pickup.
- Actual client: dedicated fresh fixture shows native orb types/icons/positions;
  cold owner/restart preserves records and no duplicate materialization. Keep
  pickup explicitly inactive until its player/durability prerequisites are complete.
