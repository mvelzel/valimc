# VMC-043e3 — Guardians and elder guardians

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e2](VMC-043e2.md).

An ocean monument's guards (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- **Guardians** swim to random spots in water, bobbing as they go and
  looking a little ahead, sink slowly while still, and flop on land. They
  beam players, squid and axolotls they see more than three blocks away:
  ten ticks to show the beam, then after 80 ticks magic damage and their
  bite. One hit while still pricks a living attacker with thorns.
- **Elder guardians** never despawn, beam harder and sooner, keep to a
  home of 16 blocks around where they first ticked, and every minute curse
  survival players within 50 blocks with Mining Fatigue III, which slows
  mining to 0.3 cubed and attacks by a tenth a level.

Spawning (the monument's overrides, the elders its generation places) is
VMC-042a/b's.

Acceptance:

- Guardian behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) equal vanilla's tick
  for tick: swimming, beaming, thorns, flopping, an elder's beam and home.
- Their combat facts, loot and entity data match the oracle.
- A guardian's beam hits a squid (the region's mob-on-mob hurts).

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- **Family.** `Mobs.Guardian` with its `Attack` goal: the
  `GuardianMoveControl` (`move_control/1`; `Math.sin`/`cos` as
  `StrictMath`), the guardian travel, the land flop, in-water and land
  sounds, the beam's data (`guardian_moving`, `guardian_attack_target`) and
  entity event 21, thorns (owed on its next tick), and the elder's home and
  curse.
- **Shared AI.** `Goals.MoveTowardsRestriction` over VMC-043c3's homes
  (`Mob.home`); a family's
  `walk_value/2`; `RandomStroll.trigger/1` through `Selector.change/4`;
  `LookAtPlayer` at mobs of given types; `NearestAttackableTarget` over
  players and mobs (`:living`).
- **Region.** One `{:hurt, target, type, amount, actions}` effect for a
  mob's non-melee hurts (a pufferfish's sting, a beam, thorns), melee on
  mob targets, and the elder's curse (`{:curse, ...}` splash action: the
  player keeps a stronger or longer effect, else takes it with game event
  10).
- **Mining Fatigue** is a live effect: mining speed (`MiningSpeed`) and
  attack speed (`Player.Attack`).

Checks: stage `mob_ai`: 33 scenes, 12375 ticks equal (six guardian scenes:
swim, beam, thorns, land, an elder's beam with its double hit, an elder
back to its home); `ai_facts`, `combat_facts`, `loot`, `nether_mobs`,
`animals`, `paths`, `mob_travel`, `combat`, `player_mining`,
`status_effect` and `projectiles` pass; the mining, mob travel, combat,
animal, brain, path and projectile suites pass.

Not modelled yet: the attack speed attribute the client draws its cooldown
from (the server's own attack strength is right), a thorns hit while the
guardian's hurt cooldown refuses the damage.

Client scenario (milestone handoff): in water, `/summon guardian` five
blocks away: it turns, the beam shows and after four seconds hurts. Hit
it while it is still: you take thorns. `/summon elder_guardian` in
survival: within a minute the ghost appears and mining slows to a crawl.
