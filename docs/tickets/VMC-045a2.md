# VMC-045a2 — Dragon flight geometry and paths

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-045a1](VMC-045a1.md), [VMC-041b](VMC-041b.md).

A bounded, pure flight model supplies the dragon's steering, 64-sample body
history, eight multipart boxes and arena paths. The live fight actor uses
this model through the owning section's ordinary entity store.
One owner keeps the small state; no process, NIF or independent part entity is
needed. Graph coordinates, adjacency, part dimensions and phase profiles come
from one generated Mojang fact source.

Acceptance:

- Native flight traces match position, velocity, yaw, turn and every part box,
  including landing turns, sitting geometry, wall slowdown and history wrap.
- Paths and closest nodes match across terrain heights, crystal counts and
  optional final nodes; searches visit at most 24 arena nodes.
- The next actor slice uses this state in its existing section store and the
  final batched client gate checks visible flight and multipart hits.

Reference: pinned Mojang 26.3 jar
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `EnderDragon.aiStep`,
`DragonFlightHistory`, `findClosestNode`, `findPath`, `EnderDragonPhase` and
phase movement profiles. Original probes call those native methods; reference
sources and fixture output stay ignored.

Checks: `dragon_flight --regen` passes 5,280 native ticks over all eleven
phase profiles, changing and absent targets, vertical flight, high speed,
NoAI, wall slowdown and an 80-tick history wrap. Position, velocity, yaw,
turn and all eight boxes agree within 1e-9. All 10,368 native paths and 45
closest-node cases pass over three height scenes with no fight, zero or ten
crystals, including unreachable outer nodes and optional final positions.
Private logs: `.local/dragon-flight-check.log` and
`.local/vanilla-checks/dragon_flight.log`. No graphical gate applies until
the actor uses the model. Next: phase decisions and their owner integration.


Live integration: [VMC-045a7](VMC-045a7.md) and [VMC-045a8](VMC-045a8.md)
now use this model in the reachable, durable fight. The remaining gate is
the parent's batched actual-client finale.
