# VMC-032c3 — Ender chests

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md). Depends on [032c1](VMC-032c1.md).

Each player has 27 ender slots (`PlayerEnderChestContainer`), saved with
the player as `EnderItems`. Any ender chest opens them in a `generic_9x3`
menu titled `container.enderchest`.

- The block holds no items; its entry counts viewers for the lid and the
  `block.ender_chest.open`/`close` sounds, as a chest's does.
- A solid block above keeps it shut, as for chests.
- The menu's transaction is the player's own: the ender slots and the
  inventory live with the player, so a click commits in the player's
  frame and a crash keeps both or neither.
- Breaking drops eight obsidian (silk touch: the chest); its loot exists.

Acceptance: two ender chests far apart show the same items; contents
survive a restart, a handoff and the player moving between groups; the
oracle's menu traces. Client scenario (M4 batch): store items in one
ender chest, open another elsewhere and take them out; relog and check.

## Result (October 31, 2026)

Ender chests are owned plain block entities: `minecraft:ender_chest` joins
`BlockEntityTypes`' plain types, so the block places (facing and
waterlogging from the generated placement facts), is listed in chunk
packets without a tag, keeps no container entry and breaks through the
generated block loot (eight obsidian, the chest with silk touch). An
imported ender chest's preserved compound no longer blocks its use.

Each player owns 27 ender slots (`Valimc.Player.EnderChest`): the live
player's `ender_items` (occupied slots by index) and, in the player's file,
one `{:ender, n}` record per occupied slot (vanilla's `EnderItems`),
validated like the inventory's stacks. A file without such records loads
with an empty chest. The slots ride the session through relogs, deaths and
moves between groups like every other player record.

`MenuAccess` has an `:ender_chest` menu among the viewed containers and
openers: opening one angers piglins, a redstone conductor on it refuses the
open as `ChestBlock`'s cover bits do (no cat check), and a spectator cannot
open it (its block entity is no menu provider). The owner answers grants
with a constant view (`EnderChest.view/0`, revision 1) and counts viewers in
the existing viewer index: the first opener and the last to leave publish
block event 1 for the ender chest's own block and
`block.ender_chest.open`/`close` at the centre (volume 0.5, the chest pitch
draw); removing the block retires the views and closes the menus. The
session opens `generic_9x3` titled `container.enderchest` over its own
slots. A click runs the shared click engine on the slots and the inventory
inside the session after the owner confirms the chest and the view (a
`:check` grant), so both change in one player state and reach the player's
next frame together; thrown stacks drop in the player's group as other
menu throws do.

Not done: the intermediate opener-count block events between the first and
last viewer are not sent, as for chests and shulker boxes; `/item ...
enderchest.N` does not address the ender slots yet.

Oracles: `ChestSoundOracle` records 8 ender chest states through a real
`EnderChestBlockEntity` (block events, sounds and draws for three viewers
with a spectator) and 36 ender chest sound codec bodies;
`ChestMenuOracle` records `ChestMenu.threeRows` over a
`PlayerEnderChestContainer` (63 slot identities, the titled open body);
`ChestOpeningOracle` runs `EnderChestBlock.useWithoutItem` for every cover
state, which matches the chest's cover bits, and with a sitting cat, which
does not keep it shut; the check runs each cover through the ender chest's
menu grant. `priv/protocol/chest_sounds.json` adds the two sound ids.

Tests: `ender_chest_test.exs` (placement facing and waterlogging, the plain
entity; lid events and sounds for first and last of three viewers with a
spectator, removal closing a view; the conductor, glass, a secondary use and
a spectator; the records' round trip and refusals; a click's frame carrying
the ender slot and the inventory slot together, then reloaded; over real
connections two chests showing one player's slots, a second player's own
slots, removal closing the menu, a relog, and a move between groups before
a click and a save). The connection test for refused block entities now
uses a crafter.

Checks: the ender chest tests and the affected chest, barrel, shulker,
container, viewer, lifecycle, menu, placement, player state and storage,
import, table, cooking, merchant and inventory persistence suites pass (106
tests, socket ones included). Twelve vanilla stages pass: chest_sound,
chest_menu, chest_opening, chest_access, chest_click, chest_view,
chest_placement, chest_block_entity, block_entities, placement, block_loot
(every ender chest state, silk touch included) and anvil_content. The
client scenario is pending.
