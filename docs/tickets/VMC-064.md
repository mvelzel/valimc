# VMC-064 — One owner-transaction engine and section fencing

Status: In progress
Milestone: M1 foundations
Depends on: [VMC-063](VMC-063.md) (steps 6–8 of its design).

Cross-owner effects are idempotent transfers, but each kind grew its own copy of
the same machinery: 40 modules (4,265 lines) plus orchestration in `RegionData`,
`PlayerStore` and `Connection`. Five permit tables (pickup, mining, placement,
container, item merge), four receipt tables with separate classification code,
three retrying delivery loops (player, handoff/merge, cooking orbs), a dozen
digest prefixes, and per-kind source prepare/resolve modules. Fences compare one
region-wide revision, so any edit anywhere in a region invalidates a placement
plan.

Scope: replace the duplicated machinery with shared primitives while keeping
every wire, storage and digest format byte-identical (stored receipts and
outboxes must keep verifying). Sequence spaces stay separate: player transfers,
region-to-region transfers and orb routing keep their own tables.

Acceptance:

- One permit table per owner (`Valimc.Txn.Permits`): per-kind limit, lifetime,
  one-per-source rule, error atoms and position invalidation; kind modules keep
  only their consume-time validation.
- One receipt classifier (`Valimc.Txn.Receipts`) for every table: new, duplicate
  with its recorded result, stale, conflict; slot reservation and limits.
- One delivery driver (`Valimc.Txn.Delivery`) with bounded attempts, backoff and
  deadline for player, handoff/merge and orb deliveries.
- Result digests come from one place (`Txn.Receipts.stored/3`); request digests
  stay per codec (each is one tagged SHA-256 over its canonical bytes, and their
  formats are pinned by stored outboxes).
- Player-sourced kinds (items, pickup, mining, placement, container/experience)
  implement one `Valimc.Txn.Operation` behaviour; delivery, completion,
  destination proof and resolution each run one generic path. The destination
  keeps its three commit pipelines (item, block and container writes), which
  differ in what they persist. The unused `recover_container_transfer` is gone.
- Per-section fencing: block observations return a fence over the sections they
  read; placement, destruction, placement reservations and batch edits check
  only those sections, so an edit elsewhere in the region no longer stales them.
  (Column observations for collision frames stay region-wide, like the
  published snapshots they verify.)
- Client check: drops, pickups, mining, placement, chest and furnace exchanges,
  reconnect and restart unchanged.

Steps (one commit each, affected suites green, fast suite per step):

1. `Txn.Permits` replaces the five permit modules.
2. `Txn.Receipts` behind every receipt table.
3. `Txn.Delivery` behind the three delivery loops.
4. Remove dead recovery paths.
5. `Txn.Operation` for player-sourced kinds; generic delivery, proof, resolve.
6. Per-section fencing.

Reference: transfer survey of October 20, 2026 (module table and flows in this
ticket's progress notes).

Progress:

- Step 1 (October 20, 2026): `Valimc.Txn.Permits` is the owner's single permit
  table (one `permits` field instead of five). Kind rules (limit, lifetime,
  one-per-source error, positions that revoke) live in one table; the kind
  modules shrank to their grant binding and consume-time validation. Container
  permits are now taken in the owner before planning and the worker only judges
  the grant, so adopting a decision can no longer overwrite permits issued
  meanwhile. Lit-only cooking edits keep container grants, as before.
- Step 2 (October 20, 2026): `Valimc.Txn.Receipts` holds the idempotency rules
  (stale, duplicate with its recorded outcome, conflict, source limit, slot
  reservation, destination proof) for every table: player items, pickup, mining,
  placement, container and experience receipts, region handoff and merge
  receipts. Stored digests keep their three historical schemes (`:plain`,
  `{:prefix, tag}`, `{:suffix, tag}`), so existing receipts still verify.
  `ItemReceipts` is gone, and the per-kind `outcome`/`result_digest` copies too.
  Fast suite 1,018 and 46 transfer-related socket suites (510 tests) pass.
- Step 3 (October 20, 2026): `Valimc.Txn.Delivery` owns the attempt lifecycle
  (bounded worker, token-matched report, optional link and deadline, finish and
  abort) for player-to-region transfers, region handoffs and merges, and cooking
  orb routing; each kind keeps only its delivery function and its reaction to
  the result. The retryable set now includes `:storage_backpressure` and
  `:mining_commit_uncertain` (VMC-063), which would otherwise end a player's
  recovery instead of redelivering the unchanged, idempotent intent.
- Step 4 (October 20, 2026): removed `Region.recover_container_transfer` and its
  owner path; production recovery redelivers through `container_transfer`, whose
  missing permit already yields a rejection. Tests use that path now.
- Step 5 (October 20, 2026): `Valimc.Txn.Operation` (`request/apply/receipt/
  proof/resolve`, `rejectable?`) replaces the five-way switches in
  `TransferDelivery` and `PlayerStore`. A reserved receipt slot now proves "not
  applied yet" (retry) for every kind, where pickups and items used to report a
  stale or conflicting transfer.
- Step 6 (October 20, 2026): each 16x16x16 section records the region revision
  of its last edit (raw or durable). `observe_blocks` adds `fence`, the values
  for the sections it read; `place_block`, `destroy_block`, `reserve_placement`,
  `set_blocks` and fenced re-observations accept a fence (or, as before, a region
  revision) and check only those sections. Creative placement and destruction
  now pass the fence, so builders working elsewhere in the same region no longer
  turn each other's clicks into stale-revision corrections. A new placement
  test edits another chunk and another section between plan and commit.
  Checks: targeted region suites; the fast suite ran under heavy CPU load from a
  parallel session (deadline failures in other modules), and every failing
  module passes on its own at low concurrency.
