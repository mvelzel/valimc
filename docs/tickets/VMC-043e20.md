# VMC-043e20 — Endermite lifetime without AI

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e19](VMC-043e19.md).

An endermite's own lifetime still advances with NoAI. Run its family step
through the existing still-mob path, so it disappears at 2400 ticks without
enabling goals or travel. Persistence stops the increment but does not save
an endermite whose stored lifetime already reached the limit.

Acceptance: the pinned native `Endermite.aiStep` and live owner agree at and
around the lifetime boundary, with NoAI and either persistence flag. The
saved lifetime survives reload. Actual-client testing remains deferred.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `Endermite.aiStep` and its
`life` field. Its subclass step follows the superclass even with NoAI.

Deferred client scenario: a nonpersistent NoAI endermite disappears after
two minutes; a newly created persistent one remains.

The shared still-mob path now runs the existing endermite family step. No
new timer, process or persistent field is needed. The native oracle records
ten traces (39 snapshots), including nonpersistent expiry, persistent counters
below the boundary and persistent counters already at or above it. Before the
fix, the live mob ticker's first step remains at zero instead of native's one;
all traces match after the fix.

All 30 affected owner, entity, ocean-age and group tests pass. The owner
regression also reloads a saved NoAI lifetime of 2399, discards it on the next
tick, preserves a persistent 2398 counter and immediately discards a persistent
2400 counter. The endermite, general mob AI, water-age and puffer-contact native
stages pass. Evidence: `.local/m3-endermite-life-{native,before,tests,stages}.log`.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
