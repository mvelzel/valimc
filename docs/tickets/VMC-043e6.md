# VMC-043e6 — Turtles and their eggs

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e5](VMC-043e5.md), [VMC-043c](VMC-043c.md).

Beach turtles (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- A turtle has a home beach where it spawned or hatched. It wanders the
  sand, takes to the water and travels far in it, and heads home now and
  then; on land its own move control keeps it slow, in water it swims at a
  tenth of its input. Hurt, it runs for water within seven blocks; seagrass
  tempts it.
- Bred, the mother carries an egg instead of a baby, goes home and lays one
  to four eggs on sand there (the sand crumbles as she digs, with her
  laying sound), then falls in love again.
- Eggs on sand crack and hatch (the overworld's dawn hatches them every
  random tick, else one in 500): one baby turtle per egg, at home there,
  which drops a scute as it grows up. Mobs (but turtles and bats) and
  players may crush eggs they stand on (one chance in 100 each move) or
  land on (one in three; zombies never do).
- A turtle struck by lightning drops a bowl (`damage_source_properties` in
  the loot engine).

Acceptance:

- Turtle behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) equal vanilla's tick for
  tick: wandering the beach, travelling at sea, panicking to water, going
  home and laying eggs.
- Their combat, loot, animal and entity facts match the oracle.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- `Entity.Animals.Turtle` (an animal of VMC-043c: its data `has_egg`,
  `laying_egg` and `home`, saved; its going home, travel and laying count
  in `Mind.own`), its goals (`GoToWater`, `LayEgg`, `GoHome`, `Travel`) and
  its move control; shared goals took options (`Breed` guard and own
  breeding, `Panic` toward water, `MoveToBlock` vertical start and
  recalculation), `World.manhattan/4`, `World.collides?/2`, `World.chunk?/3`
  (`hasChunk`), a family's `stable_destination?/2`.
- Kernel: a family's step gap and its step (`step_sound/1`, a turtle's
  shamble), ordered move-control impulses, the block a mob stands on.
- `Blocks.TurtleEgg` (random ticks: crack, hatch; `crush/2`), the hatch
  chance from the dimension's timeline (`spawn_facts`), the region's tick
  effects (sounds, level events, mobs a block makes), crushing by mobs and
  players (`Region.crush_egg/3`), a gift loot table (`{:gift, table, ...}`).

Checks: stage `mob_ai`: 46 scenes, 16045 ticks equal (four turtle scenes);
`turtle_egg_test.exs`; see the commit for the other stages.

Not modelled yet: a fed baby's scute comes on its next tick (vanilla's
as it is fed).
Pack baby finalization is implemented in [VMC-043e9](VMC-043e9.md).
Egg placement events are implemented in [VMC-043e11](VMC-043e11.md).

Client scenario (milestone handoff): on a beach, `/summon turtle` twice
and feed both seagrass: one goes home and lays eggs. Run `/time set 21500`:
the eggs crack and hatch babies. Jump on eggs: they break now and then.
