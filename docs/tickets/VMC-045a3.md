# VMC-045a3 — Dragon fight phases

Status: Foundation
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-045a2](VMC-045a2.md).

The dragon chooses holding paths, strafes and fires, approaches the podium,
lands, scans, breathes, charges and takes off. Phase decisions share the
flight history and Java random stream. One tick runs the current phase,
then at most one newly entered phase. Damage and arena ownership follow in
the parent; this slice first establishes the phase model against native runs.

Acceptance:

- Native traces agree on phases, targets, motion, random draws and counters,
  including zero crystals, missing players, line of sight and collisions.
- Fireball and breath-cloud effects carry their native positions and timing;
  leaving a breath phase removes its cloud.
- No work exceeds the fixed arena graph and the owning group's sensed players.
  The final fight client batch validates the model when the actor is live.

Reference: pinned Mojang 26.3 jar
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, all eleven
`world.entity.boss.enderdragon.phases` classes, `EnderDragon.aiStep` and
`getHeadLookVector`. Reference sources and native traces stay ignored.

The model keeps one phase state and the flight history; body yaw and head yaw
remain distinct inputs. The native probes hold head yaw fixed while calling
`aiStep`, isolate contact damage, and retain native steering, phase decisions,
random draws and phase-created entities. Crystal counting/healing, damage,
ordinary head control and applying the returned effects belong to the actor.
An above-world breath scan skips known air arithmetically, then scans at most
the supplied dimension height; its empty-column fallback is unchanged.

Checks: `dragon_phases` passes 132 native sequences and 56,184 ticks over all
eleven start phases with absent, close, moving, departing and hidden players, no fight,
zero or ten crystals, periodic collisions and repeated breath cycles. Phase,
target, remaining path, random state, timers, flame count, motion, all part
boxes, fireball origin/direction, breath creation/removal and dying health
agree (numeric tolerance 1e-9). The cases include breath at Y=10,000 to check
the bounded empty-column scan. Logs: `.local/dragon-phases-moving.log` and
`.local/dragon-phases-changed-final.log`. Next: the multipart actor, owner effects
and arena lifecycle; the graphical fight gate remains pending.
