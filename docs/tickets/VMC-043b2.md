# VMC-043b2 — Drowned

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Drowned as vanilla's `Drowned`: amphibious navigation, their own move
control and swimming, the trident attack (a thrown trident through
VMC-037o) and melee only at night or at targets in water, going into water
by day and ashore at night (`MoveToBlockGoal`), swimming up, their spawn
equipment (tridents, fishing rods, nautilus shells) and loot. The zombie
family's turtle egg goal walks to and tramples eggs.

Acceptance:

- A drowned's finalizeSpawn and its ticks in water and on land match the
  oracle's fixtures.
- A zombie walks to a turtle egg and tramples it as the oracle does.
- On the real client, drowned swim after a player in water, throw
  tridents, and come ashore at night.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Drowned` (a zombie: `Zombie` keeps its data, babies, hits
and attributes): `AmphibiousPathNavigation`, its own goals (heading for
water by day, the trident goal through the shared `RangedAttack`, melee
only at night or at targets in water, going ashore at night through
`MoveToBlock`, swimming up from deep water, wandering) and targets, its
move control (`DrownedMoveControl`: swimming toward where it goes with its
lift as its own step, `MobMotion.nudge`; heavier in the air out of it) and
the fish travel underwater while it wants to swim, unpushed by currents
(`swimming`, also the clients' shared flag). Spawning: a trident or a
fishing rod, a nautilus shell, and the zombie nautilus a natural trident
thrower rides (its variant and spawn draws; the nautilus and riding are
VMC-046's, so none appears). Its sounds are watery in water.

Thrown tridents (`minecraft:trident`, an arrow of their own): eight
damage, a bounce back off what they hit, passing through entities once
they dealt damage, water inertia 0.99 (the kernel's arrow kind takes it),
the trident's sounds and entity data.

The zombie family's `ZombieAttackTurtleEggGoal` (`Goals.RemoveBlock`) now
walks to the nearest egg in vanilla's order and stamps it out; any mob
that may grief cracks eggs it falls (not a zombie) or steps on, from the
level's random (the kernel reports the block it stands on, `on_pos`).

Shared: mobs splash into water (`doWaterSplashEffect`: in their base tick
and, through the kernel's `splash`, during a move) and hiss when put out;
a mob's swim sound follows its water state after the move; every `Monster`
values random walk targets by darkness (`RandomPos`, from the AI facts'
monster flag); a family may skip the look control; the mob AI oracle gives
sounds at blocks to the ticking mob.

Checks: `hostile_ai` (38 scenes, 10182 mob ticks bit for bit) adds a
drowned chasing at night, heading for water by day (splashing, put out),
swimming up, near the shore, throwing tridents, and a zombie stamping out
a turtle egg; `hostile_spawns` (19440 spawns) and `loot` add drowned;
`projectile_test` covers a trident's hit.

Not modelled yet: impaling and loyalty on thrown tridents, a player
throwing one, drowned conversion from zombies riding, the nautilus.

Client scenario (with VMC-043b's handoff): survival, normal difficulty, a
river or ocean at night. `/summon drowned ~3 ~ ~` on land: it walks at
you and hits. Swim into deep water: it follows you swimming. `/summon
drowned ~8 ~ ~ {HandItems:[{id:"trident",count:1}]}`: it throws tridents
that hit for 4 hearts and bounce off. By day a drowned on land walks into
nearby water. Place turtle eggs and `/summon zombie` nearby at night: it
walks over and stamps them out.
