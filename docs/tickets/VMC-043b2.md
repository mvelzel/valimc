# VMC-043b2 — Drowned

Status: Planned
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Drowned as vanilla's `Drowned`: amphibious navigation, their own move
control and swimming, the trident attack (a thrown trident through
VMC-037o) and melee only at night or at targets in water, going into water
by day and ashore at night (`MoveToBlockGoal`), swimming up, their spawn
equipment (tridents, fishing rods, nautilus shells) and loot. The zombie
family's turtle egg goal walks to and tramples eggs.

Acceptance:

- A drowned's finalizeSpawn and its ticks in water and on land match the
  oracle's fixtures.
- A zombie walks to a turtle egg and tramples it as the oracle does.
