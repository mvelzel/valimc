# VMC-026b3 — End portals

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-026b2](VMC-026b2.md); stronghold portal rooms with
[VMC-025](VMC-025.md) (structure placement, VMC-025f).

Players fill an end portal frame with eyes of ender, travel to the End's
arrival platform and leave the End through an end portal after the credits.

Acceptance:

- An eye of ender used on an end portal frame without one fills it (level
  event 1503); twelve frames with eyes facing a 3 × 3 opening from its four
  sides (corners free) open the end portal (level event 1038 for every
  player). The frame search is vanilla's `BlockPattern.find`. Survival uses
  the eye up.
- Entering an end portal outside the End leads to the End: the arrival
  platform (`EndPlatformFeature.createEndPlatform` at (100, 49, 0): a 5 × 5
  obsidian floor at y 48 with air above) and the player on it at (100.5, 49,
  0.5), facing west, keeping its pitch and motion.
- In the End, a player who has not seen the credits sees them (game event 4)
  and leaves the End's view; when its client asks to respawn, it respawns at
  the world spawn keeping everything (respawn keeping attribute modifiers, as
  `PlayerList.respawn` after the credits). One who has seen them returns
  through the portal like any travel. Whether it has seen them persists.
- Frames and platforms equal vanilla's (`portals` stage).

## Design

- `Valimc.Portal.EndPortal`: the frame pattern (the one orientation that
  matches: its front top left at the ring's largest X and Z corner, searched
  over the 5 × 5 × 5 corners at and above the clicked frame as
  `BlockPattern.find` does), the platform's changes in vanilla's order, and
  the destinations.
- End portals carry a block entity without data; `BlockEntities.plain?/1`
  lets region owners place them as plain blocks and chunk packets list them,
  so clients draw the portal.
- `Valimc.Portal.Items` fills frames through the placement job.
- Credits: the player's `won_game` hides it from others until its respawn
  request; `seen_credits` persists in snapshot v23 (a wrapper over v22).

## Evidence

- `portals` stage, October 21, 2026: 300 end portal frames (1,200 queries,
  780 opening a portal; incomplete, turned and eyeless frames) and 60 End
  platforms over varied island terrain (2,926 block sets in order) equal
  vanilla's.
- `test/portal_connection_test.exs`: the twelfth eye opens the portal;
  a creative player in it arrives at (100.5, 49, 0.5) facing west on the
  platform's obsidian floor with air above; an end portal in the End rolls
  the credits once; the respawn after them returns to the overworld keeping
  attributes, and `seen_credits` is saved.

## Limits

- Strongholds and their portal rooms arrive with structure placement
  (VMC-025f); until then frames are placed by commands or tests.
- Blocks a portal or the platform replaces drop nothing (vanilla's
  `destroyBlock` drops their loot); an eye pushes no entities up.
- Eyes of ender thrown into the air (towards a stronghold) are not handled.
- The dragon fight (the dragon, its crystals' respawn, the exit portal's
  activation, new gateways after each kill) needs mobs (M3).
