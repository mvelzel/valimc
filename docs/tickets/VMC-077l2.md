# VMC-077l2 — Player travel rules in the Rust kernel

Status: Done
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077l](VMC-077l.md) (the physics kernel).

Before: VMC-077l moved the collision geometry into `valimc_core::physics`, but
a move packet still ran the player's travel rules in Elixir around about a
dozen kernel calls: `PlayerMotionQuery.resolve_frame` with
`PlayerKinetics.packet` and `tick`, `PoseFit`, `PlayerSwimming`,
`PlayerLanding`, `BlockContacts`, `StuckContact`, `SneakEdges`,
`FluidControls`, `FluidMotion`, `ClimbMotion`, `SwimSteering`, `AirMotion`,
`MovementPaths` and their helpers. Of an 85 µs move the kernel's calls took
about 10 µs; the rest was Elixir arithmetic, validation and term building.

Scope: the player's move validation and resting tick, the travel rules they
run, the oracle checks that cover those rules and the Elixir modules the port
replaces. Not here: the living rules the results feed (burning, landing
damage, air, freezing; VMC-077k), rain's climate lookup, the entity collision
policy (`EntityCollisions`, entity boxes are not observed yet), relative
input motion (`RelativeMotion`, not live).

## Acceptance

- `valimc_core::physics::player`: the travel rules in Rust, each checked bit
  for bit against Mojang 26.3 by its oracle stage —
  - `rules`: jump power and the sprint push, gravity with Slow Falling, dry
    air drag and Levitation, the dead zone, fluid controls, water and lava
    travel with the surface escape, climbing clamps and boost, swimming
    steering and transitions, slowing contacts and their one-move
    consumption, sneaking back from edges (`maybeBackOffFromEdge`), travel
    and jump exhaustion, "moved wrongly" and the collision decision, the
    fall step;
  - `pose`: the catalog's boxes, fit boxes and eyes, the ordered pose
    choice and the occupancy queries it makes;
  - `paths`: the movement history (at most 100 moves) and the ordered block
    visits along it;
  - `contacts`: climbing contact, slowing blocks (cobweb, berry bush, powder
    snow), fire, lava and water contacts with the immediate hazards (magma,
    campfires, cactus, berry bushes) grouped by crossing step, and powder
    snow occupancy;
  - `kinetics`: the query box, a packet's jump, collision and restitution,
    and the server's tick (currents, fluid controls, steering, climbing,
    edges, collision, restitution, dry or fluid travel, slowing contacts);
  - `frame`: one move packet's validation and one resting tick.
- One call per move packet (`physics_player_move`) and one per resting tick
  (`physics_player_rest`), after a query-box call that picks the chunk rows
  to hand over (`physics_player_query`; the move recomputes and bounds it).
  The move returns the acceptance, pose, swimming, fluid, powder snow and eye
  contact, both kinetics, the landing block and distance, the exhaustion, and
  the player's motion after the packet and after the tick; the connection
  applies it as before. The fact table now also carries climbing, inside
  behaviour, hazards and the pose catalog (one install, VMC-077d).
- The living rules stay in Elixir and read the kernel's observations: the
  burning plan from the tick's contacts (`Living.Burning`), landing damage
  from the landing block (`PlayerLanding.call/3`), submersion from the eye's
  block (`Living.Air.submerged?/2`, bubble columns), rain from the column.
- `PlayerMotion.paths` is a list of `{from, to, axis}` tuples.
- Deleted: `AirMotion`, `FluidControls`, `FluidMotion`, `ClimbContact`,
  `ClimbMotion`, `SneakEdges`, `SwimSteering`, `StuckContact`,
  `StuckMotion`, `MovementPaths`, `BlockContacts`, `BlockHazards`,
  `BodyPaths`, `Swimming`, `MovementFood`, `MovementValidation`, `PoseFit`,
  `FluidContact`, `FluidCurrents`, `PlayerSwimming`; the travel half of
  `PlayerKinetics` and `PlayerLanding`; `Living.Freezing.sample/2`,
  `Living.Air.contact/2`, `PlayerPose.select/3` and its intent struct.
  `lib/valimc/physics/` holds 17 modules (about 64 before VMC-077l).
- The kernel's one-by-one entry points (move, colliding, fluids, flow,
  currents, sweeps, footing, inside shapes, restitution) are only for the
  oracle checks now: their NIFs moved to `physics_checks`, and the player
  rules' to one dispatcher in `player_parity`, both built with the `parity`
  feature only.

## Measurements

`bench/physics_kernel.exs` (`MIX_ENV=bench`, full Rust profile) on f952b6ca
(VMC-077l: kernel geometry, Elixir travel rules) and on this ticket, three
pairs back to back in one heavy slot at load 5–17 on the shared 10-core host;
raw results in the ignored `bench/results/` (`vmc-077l2-{before,after}-{1,2,3}.json`).

| | Before (VMC-077l) | After |
|---|---|---|
| CPU per move | 59.0–60.0 µs | 15.0–16.0 µs |
| Median move latency, one at a time | 59–61 µs | 15 µs |
| p95 move latency | 66–72 µs | 18–19 µs |
| Wall per move, 16 in flight | 27.3–28.5 µs | 14.4–15.7 µs |
| Item tick (floor, water; unchanged) | 1.20–1.25, 1.54–1.55 µs | 1.19–1.21, 1.54–1.57 µs |

A move costs about 3.9 times less CPU than with VMC-077l, and 10–12 times less
than the Elixir physics before VMC-077l (156–192 µs, VMC-077l's measurements)
— the audit's expected range.

Checks: the 33 stages the port touches (the 30 player-rule stages, item
travel, placement, block facts and TCP) pass; `mix test.fast` (463 tests); the
movement, pose, air, fire, landing, lifecycle, periodic, mining, pickup,
placement, void, portal and item socket suites, with the two fire tests that
fail on main in setup (VMC-077l's note) still failing.

## Deferred

- Entity boxes (boats, shulkers) are not observed for players yet; the kernel
  takes them (`EntityCollisions` keeps the policy) when entities arrive.
- Orbs and mobs use the same kernel when they move (M3).
- Gliding, riding and the Dolphin's Grace and Depth Strider attributes are
  not live; their inputs stay at vanilla's defaults.

Client: interim VMC-077 run on the frozen release of b8646a33 (October 22, 2026; evidence in ignored `.local/client-tests/VMC-077-interim/`): movement in survival with travel rules in Rust; no corrections at walls or chests. PASS.
