# VMC-084 — M4 gate

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Depends on: every M4 slice in the [backlog](../backlog.md#m4--survival-progression).

M3 made a world that can be survived to the dragon. M4 makes it worth
playing on: experience spent on enchantments, gear upgraded and repaired,
potions brewed, villages traded with and defended. M4 is done when, on the
real client, with vanilla play:

1. **Progression run.** A fresh vanilla world runs from spawn to a
   defended village and full netherite gear:
   - **Enchanting.** Earn experience, build an enchanting table with 15
     bookshelves, and enchant at level 30. The enchantments then work in
     play: Sharpness, Looting and Fire Aspect on a sword; Efficiency, Fortune
     and Silk Touch on tools; Protection, Feather Falling and Respiration on
     armour; Power and Infinity on a bow; Unbreaking and Mending everywhere.
   - **Repair and upgrade.** On an anvil, combine two enchanted books, repair
     a tool and rename an item, until "Too Expensive!". On a grindstone, take
     the enchantments off an item and get experience back. Mine ancient
     debris, smelt netherite scrap and upgrade diamond gear on a smithing
     table; apply an armour trim.
   - **Brewing.** Fuel a brewing stand with blaze powder and brew from
     Nether wart: Strength, Fire Resistance, Healing and Weakness, plus their
     splash and lingering forms. Each potion's effect works on players and on
     mobs.
   - **Villagers.** Workstations give villagers professions. Trade for
     emeralds and a librarian's Mending book; villagers level up and
     restock. Two villagers breed with beds and food. A zombie villager is
     cured with splash Weakness and a golden apple, and it gives a discount.
   - **A raid.** Take Bad Omen from a raid captain at a pillager outpost or
     patrol, carry it into a village and win every wave: pillagers,
     vindicators, evokers with their vexes and fangs, ravagers and witches.
     The reward is Hero of the Village.
   - **Containers.** A shulker box keeps its contents when broken and
     carried. An ender chest shows the same items in two places. A barrel
     works as a chest.

   Creative and commands are allowed only to shorten travel and gathering
   where the run's script notes it. The tester may buff itself freely; the
   behaviour under test must be genuine.
2. **Oracle fixtures.** Every M4 slice's fixtures pass in `check_vanilla.py`,
   together with all M3 stages.
3. **Persistence.** Enchantments, repair costs, potion contents, villager
   trades and levels, a raid in progress and the containers' contents all
   survive a restart and a group handoff.
4. **Tick budget.** A village under raid with one player holds the 50 ms
   p99 in the group bench; it never uses the flight bench.

Run 1 is one batched client handoff to the `minecraft-tester` agent, after the
slices are done.
