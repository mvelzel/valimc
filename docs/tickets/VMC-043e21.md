# VMC-043e21 — Shulker bullet break feedback

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e20](VMC-043e20.md).

A hit that breaks a shulker bullet must play its hurt sound and 15 critical-hit
particles before the bullet disappears. A bullet hitting a block emits two
explosion particles and its hit sound. Deliver terminal feedback once through
the owning group's existing world-event path; a removed entry cannot repeat it.

Acceptance: actual native callbacks and packet codecs match the feedback;
socket attacks receive the sound and particles and remove the bullet once.
The existing bullet flight and combat stages pass. Actual-client testing remains
deferred at the user's request.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `ShulkerBullet.hurtServer`,
`onHitBlock`, `onHit` and `destroy`. Hurt always returns true and discards the
bullet; the particle spread is 0.2 on each axis and speed is zero.

Deferred client scenario: punch a flying bullet and observe the break sound
and critical particles; let another hit a wall and observe its impact particles.

The bullet family supplies terminal feedback descriptors. The common owner
hurt path publishes them after store admission, including terminal feedback
from group projectile damage. A removed body no longer needs a tracker row to
carry its last packets. Block impacts use the same descriptors through the
existing projectile effect path. All event and particle ids come from generated
facts; the combat fact extractor now includes the critical particle.

Successful nonliving bullet hits now include zero previous/current health in
the attack outcome, as the player attack handler requires. Previously the
bullet reported only `hurt: true`, leaving those fields absent. The owner strips
feedback descriptors from the attack reply after publishing them.

Validation: before the change, the native comparison receives no hurt feedback
where the actual callback emits two packets. All 18 native cases now match the
full official-codec sound and particle packets (normalizing only the sound's
random seed). They cover zero, ordinary and large hurts at positive/negative
coordinates, direct block callbacks and actual block-impact ticks. The latter
confirm that impact feedback uses the tick's advanced projectile position.

All 50 affected combat, projectile, group, owner and socket tests pass. A real
socket attack receives the native hurt sound and 15 critical particles, removes
the bullet and keeps the connection alive. A second store/owner hit finds no
entry and cannot repeat terminal feedback. Seven affected native stages pass,
including regenerated combat facts, packet codecs, general combat, projectiles,
bullet flights and TCP; the final bullet-feedback stage passes after the impact
position comparison was corrected. Evidence:
`.local/m3-shulker-bullet-{hurt-native,hurt-before,hurt-check,hurt-tests,hurt-stages,hurt-final-stage,hurt-audit,facts}.log`.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
