# VMC-044c2 — Food and breeding

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044c1](VMC-044c1.md).

Part of [VMC-044c](VMC-044c.md).

- A villager's eight-slot inventory (saved) and the items it picks up
  (`wantsToPickUp`: its profession's requested items and food), food
  points (`VILLAGER_FOOD`), and the food and seeds it throws to villagers
  it meets (`TradeWithVillager`).
- `VillagerMakeLove` in the meet activity: two adults that can breed walk
  together, and after their time, with a free bed near, a baby villager
  (no profession) claims it; each eats and digests 12 points.

Acceptance: village scenes of two fed villagers and a bed breeding (and
not without the bed) equal vanilla's tick by tick.

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**Food** (`valimc_core::mobs::village::food`): the eight-slot inventory
(`SimpleContainer.addItem` and `canAddItem`, 99 a slot at most) and food
level, saved; `VILLAGER_FOOD`'s nutrition and `#villager_picks_up` from the
village facts (`VillageOracle facts`); `wantsToPickUp` (food, the tag, its
profession's requested items, with room) for its looting after the move
(mob griefing on) and the wanted-item sensor; `canBreed` (12 points between
level and inventory, awake, age 0); `eatAndDigestFood`; `throwHalfStack`.

**Brain:** the idle package's breed `InteractWith` reads `canBreed` of
itself and the villager it picks; `VillagerMakeLove` (350 ticks): gazes
locked and walking together, events 18, a birth 275 to 324 ticks on,
hearts one tick in 35 near; at the birth both eat and digest, and the
first home bed with room within 48 blocks its path reaches is taken for
the baby (its type by the biome half the time, else a parent's; age
-24000; no profession; the bed its home on its first think, counted as
taken from its birth), both parents 6000 ticks from breeding again;
without a bed, angry particles. `TradeWithVillager` throws half stacks:
food to a hungry villager (a farmer's always), a farmer's wheat past 32,
and items the other's profession asks for. Changes to the partner (its
events, meal and age) go through the owner after the tick.

Checks: stage `village` adds two scenes (fed villagers breeding with a
free bed; without one): 22 scenes, 18309 ticks, now with each villager's
food level, inventory and age, the baby's type, age, bed and place, and
the bed's ticket at the birth.

Not yet: farming (food reaches villagers from players); the oracle's baby
does not tick on (vanilla's brain gates are unseeded for it). The
breeding scene's parents start with their beds claimed: when an
`AcquirePoi` path fails, vanilla gives each tried POI its retry delay in
the order of a `HashSet<Pair<Holder, BlockPos>>`, which follows the
holder's identity hash and so differs between runs; no server can match
which bed is retried first.
