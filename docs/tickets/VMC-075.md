# VMC-075 — Fresh terrain generates per chunk, nearest first

Status: Done
Note: Live in play; client runs passed on b186641 (7 of 7) and, rebased onto VMC-025d4, on 7aea186 (6 of 6).
Milestone: M2 real worlds (player-visible)
Depends on: [VMC-071](VMC-071.md) (worldgen pool, nearest regions first, light
updates only for served chunks), [VMC-025](VMC-025.md) (VMC-025d: decorated
worlds live through proto owners), [VMC-022](VMC-022.md) (light across region
borders).

A region of a generated world generates every chunk its log lacks before it serves
any: a terrain world through `Generator.columns/2`, a decorated world through its
proto owner's finalize transaction (the 12 × 12 terrain around the region and its
10 × 10 decorated). A fresh view at distance 10 (473 chunks) touches 16 regions,
so it generates 1,024 final chunks (1,296 terrain chunks in a decorated world),
and the farthest regions bound the time to the whole view.

After this slice a region loads without generating. Its ungenerated chunks are
absent: they generate when a view or a reader wants them, nearest to the player
first, off the owner, and the owner installs each batch once it is durable.

Acceptance:

- Regions of generated worlds load without generating; their chunks without a
  column are absent. A player's view tells each region which of its chunks it
  wants and how far they are from the player; the region generates its wanted
  absent chunks nearest first, in bounded batches with bounded concurrency, on
  the worldgen workers (terrain worlds) or its proto owner (decorated worlds),
  never on the owner.
- A decorated world finalizes a set of chunks `T` of one region: the non-final
  chunks within 2 of `T` are locked (terrain generated where missing), the
  terrain-status chunks within 1 of `T` are decorated Z outer, X inner, and `T`
  becomes final. `T` = the whole region is the VMC-025d transaction, unchanged.
- An absent chunk is like a chunk of an unloaded neighbour region: block reads in
  the owner see nothing there, edits and observations of it are refused (and ask
  for it), random ticks skip it, collision and spawn readers fail closed on it,
  the chunk stream skips it, and nothing persists it. Light treats it as the halo
  treats an unknown ring chunk; installing it relights and sends `light_update`
  for served chunks whose light changed, and neighbours get its column for their
  ring.
- Generated terrain equals whole-region generation byte for byte (terrain is a
  pure function of seed and position; `test/generator_pool_test.exs`, the
  worldgen stages of `scripts/check_vanilla.py`). Decoration keeps VMC-025d's
  invariants (each chunk decorated once over terrain, a chunk final once its 3 × 3
  is decorated); its order now follows the batches.
- `bench/fresh_terrain.exs` shows a clearly shorter time to the whole view and
  fewer chunks generated per fresh view, raw results kept.
- Player-visible: flying over new terrain at render distance 16 (view 10), the
  view fills nearest first and faster than in the VMC-071 run, with no holes,
  seams or dark patches at chunk or region borders.

Design (October 21, 2026):

- Wants. `Replication.update_view/1` sends every region of the view
  `{:want, connection, center, chunks}` (its chunks in the view; an empty list for
  regions that left the view) and remembers which owner it told; a stream tick
  that finds another owner there (a restart) tells it again. The owner keeps per
  requester the wanted absent chunks and their center; a chunk's priority is its
  smallest squared distance to a requester's center. Entries empty out as chunks
  generate, so a dead connection's leftover wants cost at most its region's 64
  chunks once. Forced chunks (`/forceload`) are wanted by `ForcedChunks`, as
  vanilla generates them. Calls that need an absent chunk want it before
  anything else: raw block calls and chunk requests wait for it (`defer`),
  observations and destroys are refused with `:chunk_generating`, and published
  snapshot reads fail closed (`PlayerSpawn` asks for its columns and retries).
- Generation. The owner keeps `absent` (its chunks without a column) and a
  generation state (`Valimc.World.RegionGeneration`): wanted chunks are taken
  nearest first into batches (terrain: 4 chunks per worldgen job, up to 4 jobs
  per region; decorated: up to 16 chunks per proto request, two requests in
  flight). Terrain jobs run under `Valimc.WorldgenWorkers` (bounded by the dirty
  CPU schedulers) and report back as messages; proto requests are
  `gen_server` requests whose answers arrive as messages. A failed batch leaves
  its chunks wanted and retries after a backoff (50 ms × failures, at most 5 s).
- Durable, then installed. A batch's result goes to the region store as one
  merged frame (`Store.commit_generated/5`: columns, post-processing, block
  entities added, block ticks appended); on its acknowledgement the owner
  installs the columns (one NIF relights them once), adds block entities and ticks
  to its state, publishes the chunks' snapshots, sends neighbours the columns in
  their rings, publishes `light_update` for served chunks whose light changed,
  and answers calls that waited for the chunks. A crash in between loses nothing:
  a durable column is loaded at the next start, an undurable one is absent again.
- The chunk stream (`World.ready_chunks/1`) skips chunks its region publishes as
  absent (an ETS row, no call), so a connection never blocks on generation and
  sends the nearest chunks that are ready, as vanilla's `PlayerChunkSender` sends
  from the chunks that are loaded.
- Proto owners: `request_full_chunks/2` (and `full_chunks/3`) asks for a subset
  of the region's chunks; the owner finalizes what its waiters still lack as one
  transaction and answers each waiter with its full chunks; `handed_over/2`
  drops the images of handed-over chunks. Participants' locks, durable prepare,
  delivery and recovery are VMC-025d4's (rebased onto): terrain first, then all
  participants' locks at once or a busy back-off, prepare during decoration.
  Before that rebase this slice had its own fixes for what VMC-025d3's trace
  found (terrain started at every participant when a request arrived, and a
  decoration waiting for a worker instead of aborting); VMC-025d4's terrain
  board and decoration pool replace them.

Limits:

- Decoration order differs from whole-region transactions, so a decorated chunk
  may differ from the one an earlier ValiMC would have made where features of
  neighbouring chunks overlap; both are valid vanilla orders. A new world's spawn
  search (VMC-027d) still reads the chunks of whole-region first transactions, so
  its spawn may differ from the live chunks there by such overlaps (PlayerSpawn
  checks the live blocks).
- Light of a served chunk next to an absent one is provisional (the absent column
  reads as ValiMC's flat profile, solid up to y 63, as an unknown ring chunk
  does) and is corrected by a `light_update` when the neighbour generates.
- Nearest first holds within a region and in each view's wants. Across regions
  terrain goes by when each transaction started (VMC-025d4's terrain board),
  which follows the views' order only roughly; a fresh view's regions start
  together, so the player's own chunk takes about a second after a long hop.
- Smaller transactions overlap more: a fresh view's transactions answer busy
  120–200 times (back off and retry) where whole regions answered 60–200.
- On the build before VMC-025d4's parallel locks, a player flying at 10 blocks
  per second over new decorated terrain outran generation (client run).
