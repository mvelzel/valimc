# VMC-043e17 — Shulkers block mob movement

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e16](VMC-043e16.md), [VMC-043e1](VMC-043e1.md).

Feed the same physical shulker collider into the owner's interleaved mob
travel. Each moving mob sees nearby solid boxes where earlier mobs have
left them this tick. Refresh a shulker's collider after its lid or position
changes, and remove it immediately on a killing hit within the tick.

Acceptance:

- Native movement clipping matches for several mob sizes against closed,
  partial and full lids, every attachment face, and dead shulkers.
- Mobs collide across sections of their group; a dead shulker no longer
  blocks the next mover. The existing spatial grid bounds nearby discovery.
- Solid boxes follow the owner's interleaved state without a new process,
  permit or authority. Candidate overflow refuses a tick instead of omitting
  collisions. Existing block-only kernel callers remain supported.
- Actual-client observations remain deferred. Opening-lid push is separate.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `Entity.collide`,
`EntityGetter.getEntityCollisions`, `Shulker.canBeCollidedWith` and
`Shulker.onPeekAmountChange`.

Implementation: the existing resource-owned mob ticker retains solid boxes
by mob index and uses its spatial grid to discover nearby candidates,
refusing more than 256. A world with no solid families keeps the empty
fast path. `MobTicks` refreshes supported families before travel, after
settlement and after immediate mob-on-mob hurts, including box removal on
death. Families in other sections join the grid as fixed observations of
the same group's state; they neither tick nor receive pushes in this
section. A 66-block halo bounds those extra snapshot rows around the real
movers (64 of admitted movement plus shape extent and step-up).

Validation: 1512 actual native displacements match for husks, spiders and
small slimes against six attachment faces, three peek amounts and living/
dead shulkers. Twenty focused tests pass, including neighboring-section
collision and death, box animation, teleport across grid cells, removal,
and candidate overflow. The final group/ticker retry passes seven tests.
Both Rust crates pass (124 core tests), workspace Clippy and the production
NIF feature check pass, and all nine affected mob/path/socket stages pass
(133 seconds). A final physical-collision and TCP run after the section
observation integration also passes. Evidence:
`.local/m3-shulker-mob-{native,check,tests,final-tests,rust,stages,final-stages}.log`.

The crossing test also exposed a destination section simulating an adopted
mob again in the same group tick. [VMC-043e18](VMC-043e18.md) tracks that
separate simulation fence, now completed there. This slice's evidence
covers collision and passage after death.

Deferred actual-client scenario: another mob stops at an open shulker lid,
including at a section boundary, then passes through the freed space after
its death. Opening-lid displacement remains separate.
