# VMC-043e12 — Pufferfish player contact

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e2](VMC-043e2.md).

A pufferfish's poison and player-only sting event belong to a successful
contact hit. Route those effects through the player's damage decision so
a blocked, immune or refused hit cannot poison the player or play the sting.
The current separate hurt/splash messages incorrectly grant poison even
when the hurt is refused.

Acceptance:

- Native `Pufferfish.playerTouch` confirms damage, poison duration and the
  sting packet for unpuffed, partly puffed and fully puffed contact, with
  admitted and refused hits.
- The ordinary owner-to-player damage path applies post-hit actions only
  when its damage is admitted. Shields, hurt cooldown and creative immunity
  retain their existing authority.
- Headless socket tests cover an admitted sting and refused repeats; native
  packet and affected gameplay checks pass. Actual-client testing remains
  deferred by the user.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `Pufferfish.playerTouch`,
`Player.hurtServer` and `ClientboundGameEventPacket.PUFFER_FISH_STING`.
The sting is a game-event packet to the stung player, not a broadcast
entity sound. Generic silent-entity commands are outside this slice.

Implementation: contact actions travel in the same hurt message, and the
player applies them only after its shield, immunity and hurt-window
decision admits damage. The sting event precedes the poison and still
reaches the player on lethal contact. Ordinary damage-free gifts and potion
splashes retain their own paths.

The live owner regression exposed a second gap: goal-disabled settlement
ran only for `Animal` subclasses. It now also runs the ocean ageables' and
pufferfish's post-move hooks. This keeps actual NoAI fish contact, ocean-mob
growth and the dolphin's full-air hook live. This completes the NoAI owner
path of [VMC-043e10](VMC-043e10.md), beyond its pure ageing checks.

Evidence (October 24, 2026):

- `PufferTouchOracle` invokes native `playerTouch` with an explicit damage
  result and captures the player packets/effects: all six puff/admission
  combinations match the contact damage, poison and sting payload. The
  player-life stage also verifies the complete official packet codec.
- A compound socket test verifies one admitted sting, no repeat poison or
  event in the hurt window, full shield refusal, immunity and one lethal
  sting. Another test spawns a real puffed NoAI fish in a live owner and
  verifies three further owner ticks cannot reapply removed poison while
  the player still refuses the same damage.
- Nineteen affected ExUnit tests pass (combat, both socket flows and ocean
  age compatibility). The new NoAI body checks exercise `AI.settle` itself.
- All 27 final affected vanilla stages pass in 188 seconds, with three
  cache hits and no unverified extraction. This includes 18,063 AI ticks in
  54 scenes, adding native NoAI baby dolphin/squid traces, plus packets,
  ocean growth, spawning, combat and ordinary TCP.
- Formatting, registration, public-tree and layering audits pass. Logs:
  `.local/m3-puffer-contact-final-changed.log`,
  `.local/m3-puffer-final-tests.log` and `.local/m3-puffer-live-contact.log`.
