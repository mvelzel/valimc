region checkpoint coalesces moving items, with at most one ordinary write in

crash rollback, storage failure and writer-lease fencing; preserve retry receipts.

inventory/source intents and critical receipts retain their existing guarantees.

Client scenario: controlled accepted motion publishes through this path while a writer
is paused, stays responsive, then checkpoints/reconnects to the same latest item
without duplication. A dedicated tester owns the graphical gate.

`Region.publish_item_motion` accepts bounded revision/epoch/deadline-fenced batches
and publishes immediately. Region-local generations track saved progress; a1s
timer coalesces ordinary motion with one write in flight. Critical work pauses new
motion and writes its candidate after any earlier snapshot on the same leased
writer. Matching acknowledgments advance saved generations monotonically.

Flush serializes latest motion behind earlier writes and refuses pending critical
work. Normal shutdown saves dirty motion unless a newer critical write is already
queued; abnormal shutdown may roll back ordinary motion. Failed/timed-out saves
fence the owner. Receipt-backed retries cannot recreate moved/removed items.
