# VMC-043e24 — Dolphins read each item's water state

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e5](VMC-043e5.md).

centre block can contain water while the item is above the surface, or be air
while the item's box still touches water in a neighboring cell.

Acceptance: item water flags and dolphin eligibility match the native travel
fixtures; the owner retains the observation and publishes it to mob sensing.
Pickup delay still excludes an item. Vanilla NBT does not persist the flag.
Actual-client testing remains deferred.
