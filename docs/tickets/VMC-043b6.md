# VMC-043b6 — Witches, slimes, phantoms and silverfish

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Witches drinking and throwing potions, slimes splitting by size and
spawning in swamps and slime chunks, phantoms circling and swooping and
spawning from `PhantomSpawner` by each player's time since rest
(`time_since_rest`, VMC-040a), and silverfish calling others out of
infested blocks.

Acceptance:

- Each mob's ticks and spawns match the oracle's fixtures.
- On the real client, a witch throws potions, a slime splits, phantoms
  swoop at a player who has not slept, and silverfish pour out of
  infested stone.

## Silverfish and slimes (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Silverfish`: vanilla's goals with `WakeFriends` (hurt by
something, a second later it wakes the infested blocks within ten blocks,
nearest layers first, each stopping the search on a coin flip: broken into
a silverfish while mobs may grief, else turned back to stone) and `Merge`
(idle, it slips into a stone beside it, which becomes infested, and is
gone); it prefers walking over stone. A player's harvest of an infested
block lets a silverfish out unless the tool has Silk Touch
(`Breaking.plan`'s `infestation`, `Body.infestation/1`).

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Slime` builds on VMC-043d's `Cube`: attack damage and
experience its size, touch damage from all but the smallest, its own
sounds, a plain cube jump.

Checks: `hostile_ai` adds a silverfish chasing, merging and waking its
friends and two slimes at a player; `hostile_spawns` (16200 in all) and
`loot` add both.

Not modelled yet: explosions letting silverfish out of infested blocks.

## Witches (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Witch`: a raider's goals (idle without raids), floating,
the shared `RangedAttack` goal (`RangedAttackGoal`, its interval by
distance) throwing splash potions (`{:throw_potion, ...}`: slowness at a far
target, poison at a healthy one, weakness now and then close by, else
harming; the owner shoots it from the witch's eyes), wandering and looking
around. Its `aiStep` drinks water breathing, fire resistance, healing or
swiftness by chance (the potion in its hand for 32 ticks, slowed), and
shows its particles now and then; magic it resists takes 15%. Spawning
draws the patrol leader chance first. Mobs' eyes in water are kept after
their move (`MobMotion.eye_water`); instant health heals a mob as its
effects tick; players as mobs sense them carry their motion, health and
effects.

Checks: `hostile_ai` adds a witch drinking swiftness and throwing at a
far player, and one drinking fire resistance and healing; `hostile_spawns`
and `loot` add witches.

Not modelled yet: a witch's own potions sparing it, mob effect periodic
actions and expiry side effects (attribute modifiers stay until replaced).

## Phantoms (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Phantom`: it flies with the kernel's `travelFlying` and
no input, steered by its own move control (`move_control/1` as an impulse
after `aiStep`'s dead zone: four degrees a turn, speeding up flying
straight, its pitch toward its move target); no look control (the new
`looks?/0`), its body facing its yaw. Its goals: the attack strategy
(circling above its target, then swooping, with the swoop sound), the
swoop (biting on touch, level event 1039; a wall or a hurt breaks it off;
creative players and cats end it) and circling its anchor; its target the
highest player it may attack, looked for every 30 updates. Its size scales
its box. `Valimc.World.PhantomSpawner` runs with the group's other
spawners: every one to two minutes while the sky is dark it visits each
player under the open sky at or above sea level, likelier the harder the
local difficulty and the longer since the player rested
(`time_since_rest`), and spawns up to difficulty + 1 phantoms 20 to 34
blocks above (the `spawn_phantoms` game rule).

Checks: `hostile_ai` adds a phantom circling its anchor and one swooping at
a player twice (bit for bit); `hostile_spawns` and `loot` add phantoms.

Not modelled yet: cats hissing at phantoms (the cat search only ends the
swoop), phantoms of other sizes from spawn eggs or data.

Client scenario (with VMC-043b's handoff): survival, normal difficulty.
`/summon witch ~8 ~ ~` at night: it drinks swiftness, closes in and
throws slowness, then poison or harming. `/summon slime ~6 ~ ~
{Size:3}`: it hops at you and hurts; killed, it splits into smaller ones,
the smallest harmless. Mine an infested stone (`/setblock ~2 ~ ~
infested_stone`): a silverfish comes out; hit it near more infested stone
and others break out. At night under the open sky, `/summon phantom ~ ~20
~`: it circles, swoops at you and bites, then circles again; at dawn it
burns. (The spawner needs three in-game days without sleep; its timing is
checked by tests, not on the client.)
