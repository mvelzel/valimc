# VMC-017a1 — Death and respawn lifecycle

Status: In progress. Local drop/save foundations verified. October 15, 2026.
Parent: [health/fall damage](VMC-017a.md). Health/login/save is already validated;
do not repeat that implementation.

Acceptance: player-owned death fences pending movement and interaction; dead health,
last-death location and remaining inventory survive save/reconnect. Conserved drops
drain through bounded durable batches. Living players cannot request respawn; a
dead player's request waits for delivery, then resets health/input/motion and
synchronizes the world. Preserve source sequence/receipt consistency and never
publish a partially applied death inventory. Default overworld spawn is the current
preview scope; beds, hardcore, XP and additional gamerules remain parent work.

Reference: pinned Mojang 26.3 `Player.destroyVanishingCursedItems/dropEquipment`,
`Inventory.dropAll`, `EntityEquipment.dropAll`, `InventoryMenu.removed`,
`ServerPlayer.die`, `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.handleClientCommand`,
`PlayerList.respawn/ServerPlayer.restoreFrom`. Use native codecs from `36c2c50`.

Drop checkpoint: original `DeathInventoryOracle` verifies 516 native enchantment
decisions and 41 physical inventory/equipment drops. A present vanishing curse
applies even at level0; stored enchantments do not. Cursor/grid are menu cleanup,
outside native equipment destruction. `Inventory.death_batch` removes prevented
drops and selects at most five stacks in native inventory/equipment order, followed
by menu contents. Only dead-owner preparation may account for that destruction;
ordinary conservation stays strict. Thirty-nine affected tests pass: full inventory,
multi-batch durable close, source loss, already removed destination replay and
vanishing-only close without an outbox. Raw evidence:
`.local/death-inventory-{native,check,tests}.log`. World item physics are excluded
from this probe and remain VMC-018 work. Live death/respawn is not yet wired.

Client gate: actual death screen and respawn button, visible full health after
respawn, movement/inventory afterward, death drops and empty respawn inventory.
Fresh reconnect while dead must retain death UI/state and resume cleanup; test a
death with more than five stacks and a second fatal event after respawn. Native/TCP
negatives cover living/duplicate/early requests, stalled delivery and stale workers.
