# VMC-026b2 — Nether portals

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-026b1](VMC-026b1.md).

Players light nether portals and travel through them between the overworld
and the Nether, arriving at the portal vanilla would pick or at a new one
vanilla would build.

Acceptance:

- Flint and steel or a fire charge used on a block sets fire where vanilla's
  `BaseFireBlock.canBePlacedAt` allows it (soul fire on soul fire bases, fire
  facing the burnable neighbours), and inside an empty obsidian frame (2 to
  21 wide, 3 to 21 high, corners free) of the overworld or the Nether opens
  the portal instead. Survival wears the flint and steel by one (breaking it
  at its last use) and uses up the fire charge; creative keeps both.
- A portal whose frame or opening stops being a complete portal shape goes
  out.
- A player in a portal travels after vanilla's portal time (80 ticks in
  survival, none in creative: the game rules' defaults) and leaves the portal
  with a cooldown it renews while it stays inside, so it does not bounce back;
  a tick outside takes 4 off the time. Spectators touch no portals.
- The destination is the other dimension at the position scaled by the
  coordinate scales (8) and clamped to its border: the closest stored portal
  block within 16 blocks (into the Nether) or 128 (out of it), nearest then
  lowest, else a new 2 × 3 portal where vanilla's `PortalForcer` builds it
  (the best spot within 16 columns, or a floating platform). The player
  arrives at its place in the exit portal matching its place in the entry
  portal, turned a quarter when the axes differ, keeping its motion, and
  hears the travel sound.
- Shapes, fire placement, rectangles, relative positions, the search and the
  creation (every block set, in order) equal vanilla's (`portals` stage).

## Design

- `Valimc.Portal.Shape`, `Forcer` and `Fire` port `PortalShape`,
  `PortalForcer`, `BlockUtil.getLargestRectangleAround`,
  `BlockPos.spiralAround` and `BaseFireBlock`'s placement over block readers;
  `Valimc.Portal.Blocks` has the facts they read (`canBeReplaced`, `isSolid`,
  `isFaceSturdy(UP)`, `canBurn` from `priv/worldgen/blocks.json`, the frame
  and fire tags, fluids).
- `Valimc.Portal.Access` reads blocks and MOTION_BLOCKING heights from region
  owners' published snapshots (`BlockSnapshot.chunk/3`, `find/3`), lists the
  portal blocks of stored chunks (loaded regions and regions on disk, which it
  loads; never-generated chunks hold none, as vanilla's POIs exist only for
  saved chunks) and commits block changes per region owner, fenced on the
  observed positions, retrying busy owners.
- `Valimc.Player.Portals` keeps a player's portal contact (the portal blocks
  its box met on the way since the last tick, from published snapshots),
  cooldown and process each tick, then runs the destination
  (`Valimc.Portal.Destination`) as a connection job whose target becomes a
  travel (`Valimc.Player.Travel`, VMC-026b1).
- `Valimc.Portal.Items` lights fire through the placement job; the client's
  predicted fire is corrected by the owners before the acknowledgement.
- `Valimc.Portal.Warm`: a lit portal, and a portal process that starts, admit
  the region owners of the exit's search and creation square (18 blocks
  around the scaled position) in a pooled task. Owners generate whole
  regions as they load, so the first visit's generation overlaps the time
  before the player steps in. A destination job that fails, crashes or misses
  its deadline (vanilla's is synchronous and cannot) ends the player's
  cooldown; one still in the portal tries again after 20 ticks instead of
  renewing the cooldown until it steps out.
- `Valimc.Block.NetherPortal`: a neighbour change schedules a tick when the
  portal's shape is incomplete; the tick removes the connected portal blocks.

## Evidence

- `portals` stage (new; `PortalOracle` over a fake level with vanilla's block
  and fluid tags bound, `check_portals.exs`), October 21, 2026: 600 frames
  (3,600 shape queries; 527 find an empty opening, 143 complete portals), 600
  fire placements, 300 rectangles, 300 relative positions, 60 creations (1,656
  block sets in order; 16 floating platforms, 16 in the Nether, 9 against a
  border) and 300 searches (7 with equally near and low candidates, where
  vanilla's POI hash order decides) all equal vanilla's.
- `test/portal_connection_test.exs`: a portal lit with flint and steel
  opens; a creative player in it arrives in the Nether at a new portal near
  an eighth of its position, stays there on cooldown, then (out and back in)
  returns into the first portal; breaking the frame puts the portal out.
  Lighting admits the Nether's regions around the scaled position before
  anyone steps in.
- Warming, October 21, 2026 (load 30 to 48): a first Nether destination on a
  fresh world took 1,247 ms cold; warmed, its owners loaded in 414 ms and the
  destination then took 41 ms.
- Client run (VMC-026b): lighting, the incomplete frame, travel both ways
  into a new and then the first portal, and the frame's collapse pass. A walk
  through a freshly lit portal travelled only after its first destination
  generated, which the warming addresses. In the rerun, walking in right
  after lighting travelled in 2 to 5 s, and a `/tp` into a freshly lit portal
  arrived in a new Nether portal near the scaled position.

## Limits

- Warming is best effort (skipped while the worker pool is full), and a
  destination never visited and entered at once still waits for its first
  generation, where vanilla's server stalls instead.
- Portal collapse waits a tick (vanilla's is immediate), and a portal
  reaching past its region's edge never collapses from a change in the other
  region (no cross-region shape updates, as for support breaks).
- `findCollisionFreePosition` is the identity: a player always fits inside a
  valid portal's opening; a block directly behind a found portal could push
  vanilla's arrival a little.
- Game rules keep vanilla's defaults until `/gamerule` stores them
  (VMC-053c). Items, orbs and mobs do not use portals; the portal's ambient
  sound, nausea and particles are the client's own.
- Flint and steel lights neither campfires, candles nor TNT; fire neither
  spreads nor burns out (fire ticks), and its use sound is the user's only.
