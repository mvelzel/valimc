# VMC-043e23 — Turtle growth at a restored age boundary

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e6](VMC-043e6.md).

A turtle restored at age −1 must drop its growth gift on its first accepted
tick, including with NoAI. Compare the actual before/after ages rather than
remembering whether the AI saw a baby on an earlier tick.

Acceptance: native turtle `aiStep` and the live ticker agree at ages −2, −1,
0 and 1, with locked/unlocked age, mob drops enabled/disabled and NoAI. An
owner produces one scute on the transition and none on later adult ticks.
Actual-client testing remains deferred.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `AgeableMob.aiStep`, `setAge` and
`Turtle.ageBoundaryReached`. `TurtleGrowthOracle` intercepts only the native
gift request; it leaves the age and boundary callback unchanged.

Implementation: the turtle's existing `after_ai` hook compares its age before
and after shared animal settling. The obsolete remembered baby flag and its
setter are removed. The existing gift and owner drop path is unchanged.

Validation: before the repair, both the native comparison and the restored-owner
test fail at age −1: ValiMC reaches adulthood without its gift. Afterwards all
32 native traces (128 steps) match, and 26 focused animal, turtle, owner and
headless socket tests pass. The restored NoAI turtle drops exactly one scute;
the locked baby and pre-existing adult drop none, and later ticks add none.
All six affected native stages (`turtle_growth`, `mob_ai`, `animals`,
`animal_facts`, `turtle_egg_place`, `water_age`) pass in 74 seconds with five
cache hits. Evidence:
`.local/m3-turtle-growth-{native,before,before-test,check,tests,stages,audit}.log`.

The former parent note about an immediate feeding scute does not describe the
pin's ordinary food path. `AgeableMob.getSpeedUpSecondsWhenFeeding` truncates
`(ticks / 20) * 0.1F`; it is zero for the final 199 ticks. Feeding therefore
cannot itself cross age zero, and the natural boundary above handles the gift.

Deferred client scenario: let an ordinary baby turtle grow up, pick up its
scute, then observe that the adult does not keep dropping scutes.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
