# VMC-044a2 — Iron golems

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-044a1](VMC-044a1.md).

Part of [VMC-044a](VMC-044a.md).

- **Iron golems:** their goals (melee, moving toward a target, back to
  the village, strolling in it, offering a flower, looks) and targets
  (hurt by, monsters but creepers, angry at players), their hit and its
  upward knockback, their attack animation and cracks.
- **Villagers summon them:** gossiping at the meeting point and panicking,
  villagers that slept lately and saw no golem summon one near them.

Acceptance:

- The oracle's golem scenes (a golem strolling in a village, defending a
  villager from a zombie, villagers summoning one) match.
- On the real client, zombies attack villagers and the village's golem
  kills them.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

**Iron golem** (`Valimc.AI.Mobs.IronGolem`): vanilla's goals and targets
at their priorities — `MeleeAttackGoal`, `MoveTowardsTargetGoal` (new,
`Valimc.AI.Goals.MoveTowardsTarget`), `MoveBackToVillageGoal` and
`GolemRandomStrollInVillageGoal` (a `RandomStroll` with a guard and a
target of its own: a villager that wants a golem, its claimed POIs, or a
land position; `Pois.closest_village_section`), `OfferFlowerGoal`, looks;
`DefendVillageTargetGoal`, `HurtByTargetGoal`, nearest attackable players
it is angry with and enemies but creepers (every fifth tick), and
`ResetUniversalAngerTargetGoal`. Its hit is 7.5 plus `nextInt(15)`, shows
entity event 4, plays its attack sound and lifts its target by 0.4 less
its knockback resistance (a new `lift` on hits, for players and mobs);
being hurt plays its damage sound when its crackiness drops a step
(`hurt_events`). It stays angry with a player who hurt it (the neutral
fields), never targets players when a player built it (`PlayerCreated`),
and pushing an enemy targets it one time in twenty. Its sprint particles'
draws of its random happen in its tick. `enemy` is a generated entity fact
(`EntityOracle`, made by each type's factory), `invisible` a block fact
(`RenderShape.INVISIBLE`, for the sprint particles' block).

**Villagers summon golems** (`Valimc.AI.Brains.Villager`): gossiping with
another villager at the meeting point (`TradeWithVillager`'s gaze and
gossip) and panicking (every hundredth tick), a villager that slept in the
last 24000 ticks and saw no golem in the last 600 makes one when enough
villagers within ten blocks want one too (five with it when gossiping,
three when panicking) (`SpawnUtil`'s legacy iron golem spots: ten tries in
a 17×13×17 box, down to a solid top, an empty space that fits it), which
finalizes on the level's random like any spawned mob and joins the section.

Checks: stage `village` now has three golem scenes — a golem strolling
where there is no village, a golem guarding a villager and killing a zombie
near it, three villagers summoning a golem at a zombie — and passes with
5110 villager and golem ticks of nine scenes equal to vanilla's (position,
velocity, rotations, random, goals, target, health, the level random and
the summoned golem's id and seed). `test/villager_region_test.exs`: in a
region owner a golem kills a zombie hunting a villager (without the golem
the zombie lives).

Not modelled yet: repairing a golem with iron ingots, a golem's anger
saved with it, `DefendVillageTargetGoal` choosing a player (it needs
reputation, VMC-044), the wither as a counted enemy, building a golem from
blocks, and the one-tick lag of a kill: mob hits apply after the tick, so a
golem clears its dead target a tick later than vanilla (the check stops at
the death).

Client scenario (phase-end handoff): survival at night in a fresh world's
plains village. `/summon iron_golem` and `/summon zombie` a few blocks
apart near a villager: the zombie goes for the villager, the villager
flees, the golem walks to the zombie, swings its arms and throws it up,
and kills it in a few hits. Hit the golem: it turns on you and throws you
up; it cracks as it loses health. By day it strolls about and now and then
holds out a poppy to a villager.
