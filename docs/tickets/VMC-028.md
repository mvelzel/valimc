# VMC-028 — Generation settings, presets, data packs and older worlds

Status: In progress
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-020](VMC-020.md) (world NBT and schemas), [VMC-021](VMC-021.md)
(Anvil import and export), [VMC-024](VMC-024.md) and [VMC-025](VMC-025.md)
(generation, live decoration), [VMC-026](VMC-026.md) (dimensions).

A ValiMC world records its generator as a seed and two flags in `world.json`:
the normal overworld preset, or ValiMC's own flat profile. Vanilla 26.3 keeps a
world's generation settings in `data/minecraft/world_gen_settings.dat`
(`WorldGenSettings`: seed, structures, bonus chest and, per dimension, its type
and generator), lets a new world pick a preset (normal, flat with its layers,
large biomes, amplified, single biome, debug), loads worldgen from the world's
data packs, and upgrades worlds of older versions through its data fixers.
ValiMC reads none of that: imports keep the file for export only, exports of
generated worlds write none (vanilla would then generate a different world),
and worlds of older data versions are refused (VMC-020).

Acceptance (parent):

- A new world created with each preset, exported, opens in vanilla 26.3 with the
  same generation settings and continues the same terrain.
- Large biomes, amplified and single-biome worlds generate vanilla's terrain and
  decoration live (parity for a fixed order, as VMC-025d); flat worlds generate
  vanilla's flat chunks.
- A data pack's worldgen changes (noise settings, biomes, features) are generated
  as vanilla generates them, or the world is refused with a clear reason.
- Worlds of the previous release import with vanilla's upgrade, or the ticket
  records what blocks it.
- Client: a large-biomes or amplified world, and an imported older world.

## Design notes

- Format: vanilla's `WorldGenSettings` codec writes `data` with `seed`,
  `generate_structures`, `bonus_chest` and `dimensions` (dimension id to its
  `type` and `generator`; a noise generator names its `settings` and
  `biome_source`, a flat one its layers and biome). `world.json` keeps exactly
  that, as JSON, under `generator.settings`, so import and export are a
  conversion, not an interpretation; ValiMC's `features` flag (live decoration,
  VMC-025d) stays beside it.
- Imports keep generating as before (flat outside imported chunks): vanilla's
  proto chunks around the full ones are not imported, so live generation next
  to them would cut features. Importing proto chunks into proto owners is the
  way to continue an imported world's generation; it is out of scope here.
- Dimensions: VMC-026b owns the dimension lifecycle. This ticket stores and
  converts every dimension's generator; generation reads the overworld's, and
  the Nether's and End's when VMC-026b brings them live.

## VMC-028a — generation settings (October 21, 2026)

- Facts (`WorldSettingsOracle facts`, stage `world_settings`):
  `priv/worldgen/world_presets.json`, the `worldgen/world_preset` registry as a
  server loads it from the vanilla pack (7 presets), as `WorldPreset`'s codec
  writes it.
- `Valimc.World.GenSettings`: settings as a dedicated server creates them from
  `server.properties` (`DedicatedServerProperties.WorldDimensionData`): the
  level type names a preset, an unknown one is the normal preset; only the flat
  preset takes `generator-settings`, parsed as `FlatLevelGeneratorSettings`
  (unknown blocks are air, lakes and features false unless given, the biome
  plains unless a known one is given, structure overrides only when they name
  known structure sets, a lenient field) and ignored when they do not parse;
  structures from `generate-structures`, never a bonus chest. Conversion to and
  from `world_gen_settings.dat` (`data` plus `DataVersion`; `seed` a long, flags
  bytes, numbers ints) for noise generators over named settings and multi-noise
  preset, fixed, checkerboard or End biome sources, flat and debug generators;
  anything else (a data pack's inline noise settings or parameter lists) is
  refused with its reason.
- `world.json` records the settings: new vanilla worlds (`VALIMC_LEVEL_TYPE`,
  `VALIMC_GENERATOR_SETTINGS`, `VALIMC_GENERATE_STRUCTURES`, as
  `server.properties` names them; only the normal preset generates until
  VMC-028b), worlds created before (the normal preset with structures), and
  imports (`"type": "imported"`, from their `world_gen_settings.dat`). Imports
  keep flat terrain outside their imported chunks, but clients now get the
  imported seed's hash (biome blending as vanilla) and `/seed` shows it.
- Export: a world never imported gets its settings in
  `data/minecraft/world_gen_settings.dat` (ValiMC's flat profile as a flat
  generator: bedrock, 123 stone, 3 dirt, grass over plains) and a new
  `level.dat` initialized at its spawn; imports write back the files they kept.
  Exporting a world without regions creates the destination.
- Stage `world_settings`: for 12 cases (every preset, two seeds and both
  structure flags for the normal one, three flat settings among them one that
  does not parse and one with an unknown structure tag, an unknown level type),
  ValiMC's settings equal the JSON vanilla's codec writes, its file equals
  vanilla's NBT, and it reads vanilla's file back. Then four ValiMC worlds
  (normal, structures off, decorated, the flat profile) are exported and run on
  the pinned server, which keeps their settings and spawn and logs no error.
- Tests: `test/gen_settings_test.exs`.

## VMC-028b — world presets live (October 21, 2026)

- `Generator.spec_of/1`: what generation needs from the overworld's generator.
  Noise generators over the overworld, large biomes or amplified noise
  settings with the overworld preset's or a fixed biome source (worldgen state
  per seed and noise settings); flat generators without features or lakes
  (their layers from the bottom, at most the world's 384 blocks, and their
  biome). Debug worlds and flat worlds with features or lakes are refused at
  creation with the reason.
- Fixed biome sources in `valimc_core::worldgen` (`fixed:BIOME`): chunk and
  carver biomes are the one biome, and decoration derives from the preset
  holding it (`Decoration::fixed`): `FeatureSorter` over one biome orders each
  step as the biome lists it, so the feature seeds follow that list. NIF
  `worldgen_proto_decorate/4` takes the biome source.
- Live: region loads, proto owners and the spawn search follow the world's
  spec; flat worlds store column images of their layers (NIF
  `column_layers_image`) like generated ones and are not decorated. The spawn
  search starts where vanilla's does: a noise generator's spawn target and
  height 64, a flat one at chunk (0, 0) and the bottom plus its layers
  (`ChunkGenerator.getOrigin`, `getSpawnHeight`).
- Stage `worldgen_live` gains three sequences, each world created from its
  preset: large biomes (seed 20261020, regions (2, −3), (3, −3)), amplified
  (seed 12345, region (0, 0)) and single biome (plains, seed 20261020, region
  (−5, 4)); the oracle builds vanilla's terrain with the preset's noise
  settings and biome source and decorates with the feature order vanilla's
  `FeatureSorter` gives that source. All 640 final chunks of the six sequences
  (62,914,560 blocks) equal vanilla's with their marks, ticks and 45 block
  entities. The check stops each case's proto owners, whose region keys an
  earlier case may share in another world.
- Stage `world_settings`: a flat export without structures (bedrock, 40
  deepslate, 20 sandstone, 3 sand, desert) runs on the pinned server, which
  generates chunk (0, 0) from ValiMC's settings with exactly ValiMC's column
  and biome. A flat world without structure overrides gets every structure set
  in vanilla (its first run placed a mineshaft); structures are VMC-025f's.
- Tests: `test/world_presets_test.exs`.
- Not done: debug worlds (vanilla refuses every block change in them, which
  would touch every edit path), flat worlds with features or lakes, structures
  in any preset (VMC-025f).
