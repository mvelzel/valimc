# VMC-043b5 — Endermen

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Endermen as vanilla's `Enderman`: picking up and placing blocks, teleporting
away from water, projectiles and attacks, stare aggro through a player's
look (a carved pumpkin hides it), its creepy sound and loot.

Acceptance:

- An enderman's block carrying, teleports and stare aggro match the
  oracle's fixtures.
- On the real client, an enderman a player stares at screams, teleports
  and attacks; a carved pumpkin hides the stare.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Enderman` holds endermen: vanilla's goals with their own
`Freeze` (still while its target player stares), `TakeBlock`, `LeaveBlock`
and `LookForPlayer` (a stare or anger makes a player pending, the target
three updates later; stared at from within four blocks it teleports away,
far from its target it teleports toward it), universal anger through
`Valimc.AI.Neutral`, the attacking speed and creepy flag on a target,
daylight teleports and `randomTeleport` (down to a block entities may
teleport onto, fitting, out of liquids, not over water; the trail and
sounds). `isLookingAtMe` reads the player's look and line of sight; a
carved pumpkin hides it.

Hurts: projectiles never hurt it (the new `repelled/2` hook of turned-away
hurts sends it off on its next tick, 64 tries); other hurts without a
living attacker teleport it nine times in ten; water and rain hurt the
types the hostile facts call water sensitive (blazes, endermen, striders,
snow golems). A carried block drops on death through its block table with
a Silk Touch diamond axe (`death_drops/1`).

Blocks: a taken block becomes its fluid with neighbour updates; a block put
down goes through the rules engine's mob edit, which now updates
neighbours and sets only a state that survives there; one that does not
returns to its enderman.

Shared: mobs swing as vanilla's `SwingState` (`Self.swing/2`: no new swing
in the first half of one); a teleport this tick turns the body from where
it landed (`Mind.snapped`); players in the mob AI oracle can face a
direction.

Checks: `hostile_ai` adds six scenes (staring from eight and three blocks,
a carved pumpkin, taking a flower, putting a block down, teleporting by
day), 1620 mob ticks bit for bit; `hostile_spawns` and `loot` add
endermen.

Not modelled yet: splash water potions, `updateFromNeighbourShapes` of a
placed block, visual shapes for the stare's line of sight (collision
shapes stand in), a hurt teleport within the same tick (it waits for the
next one).

Client scenario (with VMC-043b's handoff): survival, night, open ground.
`/summon enderman ~8 ~ ~` and look at its eyes: it shakes, screams and
comes for you; up close it teleports when stared at. With a carved pumpkin
on your head it ignores your stare. Shoot it with an arrow: it teleports
and takes nothing. One near grass now and then picks a block up and puts it
down elsewhere; killed, it drops what it carried.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
