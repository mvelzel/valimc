# VMC-044c3 — Zombification and curing

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044c1](VMC-044c1.md).

Part of [VMC-044c](VMC-044c.md).

- A zombie's kill of a villager (normal: half the time, hard: always)
  converts it into a zombie villager with its villager data, offers,
  gossip and experience (`Zombie.killedEntity`, `convertTo`).
- A golden apple on a zombie villager under Weakness starts its cure
  (3600 to 6000 ticks, the sound, Strength); it shakes, and at the end
  becomes a villager with its data, offers, gossip and experience, the
  curer's cure gossip and Nausea.

Acceptance: conversions both ways equal vanilla's (data, offers, gossip,
timers); client scenario: a zombie kills a villager on hard, the zombie
villager is cured with a splash of Weakness and a golden apple.

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**Zombification:** `Hooks::killed` runs after a melee kill; a zombie's
(`Parts::killed`) on normal draws its random's boolean, on hard always
converts: the effect `{:merchant, :zombify, uuid}` and level event 1026.
The owner (`RegionData.zombify`) reads the villager's family data and
offers (drawn first if it had none), converts it with the shared
conversion step (equipment, effects, loot pickup kept) and gives the
zombie villager its type, profession, level, finalized flag, gossip,
offers and experience. The new body's `finalizeSpawn(CONVERSION)` runs
pending on its first tick (reason `zombified`: its group data given, so no
baby draw; `Mob`'s follow range and handedness, can-break-doors, the
Halloween head, attributes, on the level's random), not the attribute
re-roll of `convertToZombieType`.

**Curing:** a golden apple on a zombie villager under Weakness
(`Kind::interact`) starts its cure: 3600 plus `nextInt(2401)` ticks, the
curer kept, Weakness removed, Strength (amplifier 0) for the same time,
entity event 16 (the shake and its sound); the apple is used unless the
player has infinite materials. Each tick its countdown falls (by more near
iron bars and beds, `getConversionProgress`); at zero the effect
`{:merchant, :cure, starter}` has the owner (`RegionData.cure`) convert it
without its equipment (preserved gear drops), restore its villager data,
gossip, offers and experience, finalize it as a conversion, add the
curer's cure gossip (+20 major positive, +25 minor positive) and Nausea
for 200 ticks, and play level event 1027.

Checks: `test/merchant_connection_test.exs` adds a golden-apple cure
(timer within 3600–6000, apple used, villager data and the curer's gossip
after the countdown) and a hard-difficulty kill turning a villager into a
zombie villager with its profession, level, experience and finalized flag.
Both conversions run vanilla's step order with the decompiled draws; no
oracle trace compares them (a cure lasts minutes, and the level random's
draws are not traced by `VillageOracle`).

Not yet: a drowned's kill (drowned is a separate family with no zombify
hook); the advancement trigger (`CURED_ZOMBIE_VILLAGER`, VMC-047).

## Awaiting client

On hard: a zombie kills a villager standing in a corner; it turns into a
zombie villager with the villager's clothes. Throw a splash potion of
Weakness at it and use a golden apple on it: it shakes with the cure
sound and its apple is used; after 3 to 5 minutes it turns into a villager
of its old profession with Nausea, and the curer's prices with it are
lower than before.
