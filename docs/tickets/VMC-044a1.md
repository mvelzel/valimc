# VMC-044a1 — Points of interest and a villager's day

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-041b](VMC-041b.md), [VMC-040a](VMC-040a.md).

Part of [VMC-044a](VMC-044a.md).

- **Points of interest:** vanilla's POI types (beds' heads, job site blocks,
  bells) from generated facts (`VillageOracle facts`), read from published
  block snapshots. A POI's taken tickets are the villagers whose home, job
  site, potential job site or meeting point memory names it, across the
  group's sections, so a claim needs no store of its own and ends with its
  villager. A 16-block section near one holding an occupied village POI is
  a village (`sectionsToVillage`).
- **Villager:** its entity data (`VillagerData`, finalized), what generation
  gave it, its brain with vanilla's sensors (nearest bed, hostiles, secondary
  POIs, golem detected) and the core, idle, work, meet, rest and panic
  activities, chosen by the `villager_activity` schedule of the day; it
  acquires a home, a job site and the meeting point, takes the profession of
  its job site and loses it with it, sleeps in its bed at night (the bed's
  `occupied` and the sleeping pose), wakes at day, panics at nearby zombies
  and illagers and when hurt. Its POI memories are saved.

Acceptance:

- The oracle's villager scenes (a villager finding a bed and sleeping at
  night, taking a job site's profession, working and meeting at the bell,
  panicking at a zombie) match tick by tick.
- In a region owner, villagers of a generated village claim beds and job
  sites and sleep at night.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

**Facts** (`VillageOracle facts`, `priv/registry/village_facts.json`, stage
`village_facts`): every POI type's block states, tickets, valid range and
tags; each profession's held and acquirable job sites, secondary POI
blocks and work sound in registry order; villager types and the type of
each biome; the `villager_activity` and baby schedule of every dimension
over a day (the same timeline everywhere); the family's sound ids.
`Valimc.AI.Villagers` reads them, `Valimc.AI.Pois` the POI types.

**Points of interest** (`Valimc.AI.Pois`): vanilla's `PoiManager` read
from published block snapshots (`getInRange`'s chunk square, square and
sphere, closest first, `findClosest`, `getType`). A POI's taken tickets
are the claiming memories (home, job site, potential job site, meeting
point) of the living villagers in the group: each section keeps its
villagers' claims (`RegionData.poi_claims`, refreshed after its mob tick)
and shows them to its siblings; a tick's world sums its own and theirs,
and a claim earlier in the tick counts for the mobs after it.
`sectionsToVillage` is the chessboard distance to a 16-block section with
an occupied village POI.

**Villager** (`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Villager`, `Valimc.AI.Brains.Villager`,
`Valimc.Entity.Villager`): `VillagerData`, finalized, the unhappy counter,
age, experience and the bed it lies in as its own data (entity data fields
from `EntityOracle`); generation's NBT read once. Its brain has vanilla's
nine sensors (nearest bed for babies, hostiles at their distances,
babies, secondary POIs, golems) and the core, idle, work, meet, rest,
panic and play packages at vanilla's priorities, with `AcquirePoi` (its
batch of five, retry markers, a deferred search's answer on a later tick),
`ValidateNearbyPoi`, `SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory`, `StrollAroundPoi`,
`StrollToPoi`, `StrollToPoiList`, `VillageBoundRandomStroll`,
`GoToClosestVillage`, `SetClosestHomeAsWalkTarget`, `InsideBrownianWalk`,
`PoiCompetitorScan`, `GoToPotentialJobSite`, `YieldJobSite`,
`AssignProfessionFromJobSite`, `ResetProfession` (both make the brain
again), `WorkAtPoi` (its work sound), `SocializeAtBell`, `SleepInBed`,
`WakeUp`, `VillagerPanicTrigger`, `VillagerCalmDown`, `SetRaidStatus`'s
draws, `JumpOnBed`, and the schedule. Lying down sets the bed's
`occupied` (head and foot, `{:bed, head, true}`, in this or a sibling
section), the sleeping pose and place; standing up uses the bed's
stand-up search. Its saved memories go with its record. Doors on its
paths open and close (`InteractWithDoor`, `{:door, pos, open}` as a use
of the door through the rules).

**Shared** (an earlier commit): gates with entry conditions and erased
memories, trigger gates, untimed behaviours, explicit priorities and
vanilla's activity order, remote changes to another mob's brain, Swim,
InteractWithDoor, SetWalkTargetAwayFrom, land targets away and toward.
Mobs' melee hits on mobs (a zombie's on a villager) land through the one
hurt path.

Checks: stage `village` (`VillageOracle cases`, `scripts/check_village.exs`,
vanilla's own `PoiManager` in memory over each scene, the overworld's
timelines on the level's clock): 3500 villager ticks of six scenes equal
vanilla's bit for bit — position, velocity, rotations, random, running
behaviours, activity, profession, POI, walk and look memories, sleeping
place, path, level random, tickets and POI block states: an unemployed
villager claiming a bed by day, walking to it at dusk and sleeping, waking
in the morning and standing up beside it, taking a lectern's trade and
working there, meeting at the bell, panicking at a zombie. The oracle
seeds each brain's gates (and a remade brain's) by key and records its
activity order per priority, which the check follows.
`test/villager_region_test.exs`: in a region owner a villager by day
claims the bed and lectern beside it and becomes a librarian, at night
lies in its bed (both halves occupied) and its record keeps its claims.

The full M3 suite exposed a fixture-only intermittent night-transition
timeout. Repeating that existing owner test reproduces it: a random stroll
reaches the lone loaded chunk's edge, leaving the mind at tick 80 while the
owner continues over 34,000 ticks. The movement kernel correctly refuses
an unreadable neighbor, so the villager cannot turn back toward its bed.
The fixture now seeds the surrounding chunks and keeps the scene near
the section center; its floor and POIs are written together before loading.
No brain, pathfinding, random or timeout rule changes. Failure evidence is
retained in `.local/m3-villager-diagnostic.log`; the full-suite failures
remain in `.local/m3-full-suite.log` and `.local/m3-full-suite-final.log`.
The existing two owner tests then pass all 31 repeated runs (62 checks)
in `.local/m3-villager-loaded-fixture-tests.log`.

village by day. Villagers wander and gather; ones by a job site block wear
its trade and stand at it (green sparkles when one takes a job site or

day`: they get up beside the bed. Break a librarian's lectern: it loses its
trade. `/summon zombie` beside a villager: it runs from it.
