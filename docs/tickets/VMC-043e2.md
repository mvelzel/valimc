# VMC-043e2 — Squid, glow squid and fish

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e1](VMC-043e1.md).

The ocean's small life (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- **Fish** (cod, salmon, pufferfish, tropical fish) panic when hurt, flee
  players within eight blocks and swim to random spots in water by their
  own move control; they sink slowly unless they have a target, flop and
  drown out of water, and play their swim sounds. Cod, salmon and tropical
  fish school behind a leader. A pufferfish puffs up near what it fears
  and stings it with Poison; a salmon's size and a tropical fish's variant
  come with its spawn.
- **Squid** beat their tentacles and stroke along a random heading, flee
  what hurt them and squirt ink; out of water they fall. A glow squid
  darkens when hurt.

Acceptance:

- Fish and squid behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) equal vanilla's
  tick for tick.
- Their combat facts, loot and entity data match the oracle.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- **One mob at a time.** An owner's mobs now tick as vanilla's level ticks
  them: each thinks, travels and pushes before the next thinks, so a
  follower sees its leader where it moved to. `MobTicks.steps/4` drives
  `AI.think_mob/4` and a kernel ticker (`Kernel.mobs_begin/4`,
  `mob_step/3`, `mobs_finish/1`: a resource holding the view, the mobs'
  motion and the push grid for the tick, `physics::mob::Ticker`); others
  read a moved mob through the world's overlay (`World.moved`,
  `mob_list/1`, `mobs_of/2`). A 300- and 600-zombie dev sample of
  `bench/mob_ticks.exs` measured 17.8 and 40.9 ms a tick against main's
  19.6 and 44.9 under the same load.
- **Kernel.** A mob's own move control (`Control::Set`: `setSpeed`, the
  eye-in-water lift), an impulse after the dead zone, the fish, guardian
  and squid travels, fluids that do not push water animals, vertical
  collisions, and the distance walked with its swim sounds
  (`applyMovementEmissionAndPlaySound`). The server's mobs settle their
  ground state even when still (`isLocalInstanceAuthoritative`).
- **AI.** Families' `move_control/1` (before the look control), water
  animals' air in their base tick (drowning before their AI), dying mobs
  that still run their own `tick` and `aiStep` (a dead fish flops), the
  first tick's fluids read before the AI, swim sounds with their pitch
  draws, `RandomSwimmingGoal` (`RandomStroll` with `swim:`), stable
  destinations by navigation kind, and changes to other mobs' own state
  (`{:own, uuid, change}`, a school's).
- **Families.** `Mobs.Fish` (cod, salmon; what every fish shares, the
  flock goal), `Mobs.Pufferfish` (`Puff`), `Mobs.TropicalFish`,
  `Mobs.Squid` (`RandomMovement`, `Flee`). Entity events gained particles
  (a squid's ink, thirty a hurt; a view keeps 40 events).

Client scenario (milestone handoff): in an ocean or a dug pool. `/summon
cod`, three more nearby: they gather and swim together. Walk up to them:
they flee. `/summon pufferfish` beside you: it puffs up, stings and
poisons you, and shrinks once you leave. Hit a squid: it squirts ink and
jets away. A cod on land flops and dies after about seventeen seconds.

## Client death-message fix (October 24, 2026)

M3 client run 4's lethal pufferfish sting disconnected the player while
formatting the death screen. `Living.DamageSource.death_message/2` accepted
only atom sources and its fact table covered only environmental damage;
contact hits carry a structured combat source. The shared formatter now
unwraps those sources. `DamageMessageOracle` extracts every registered
unattributed source (50), except intentional bed/anchor deaths, which keep
their separate linked message. `Player.Hurt` uses the attacker's generated
display name when the hit has no explicit killer label.

The existing combat socket scenario now ends with a lethal contact hit:
the death packet names the pufferfish and the connection still answers
`/stats`. Fifteen combat/periodic/item-use socket checks pass, and the
native damage-message stage passes 100 source/name cases and 800 death/chat
packet comparisons. Private logs: `.local/m3-death-tests.log` and
`.local/m3-death-vanilla.log`; failed client evidence:
`.local/client-tests/M3-run-04/pufferfish-disconnect.png` and
`pufferfish-trace.txt`. The run-05 graphical retry passed: a lethal sting
showed “ValiMCTest was slain by Pufferfish”, actual Respawn worked and the
player typed `/stats` and received a reply on the same connection. Nonlethal
puff/poison and the family's other scenarios remain pending. Details:
`.local/client-tests/M3-run-04/run-05-results.md`.
