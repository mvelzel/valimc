# VMC-081c2 — Constant-cost chunk adoption

Status: Done
Milestone: M3, at the user's request
Parent: [VMC-081](VMC-081.md).
Depends on: [081c](VMC-081c.md), [081c1](VMC-081c1.md), [081c3](VMC-081c3.md).

The 081x whole-tick probe (`a75a314c`, explore, two pinned cores) found the
owner spending 17.6 s a minute in generation callbacks (4,531 calls at 3.9 ms
each, about 1.1 chunks per delivery): more than its whole tick budget, and the
largest item while exploring.

## Where a delivery's time went

Spans around each step of a delivery, the harness flying at 60 blocks per
second on the development Mac (`+S 2:2`, 8,094 chunks in the minute; the
host was shared, so only the shares count):

| Step | Per call | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Install in the section (`put_columns_lit`, dirty) | 741 µs | 27% |
| Siblings' rings (`halo_put` per sibling, dirty) | 2,189 µs per delivery | 22% |
| The chunks' first creatures (`spawn_generated`, dirty) | 570 µs | 21% |
| Generated ticks scheduled, then frozen | 268 µs | 10% |
| Frames: a commit in the section and a cut per delivery | 152 + 694 µs | 13% |
| Everything else (parts, entities, publication, light queue) | | 7% |

The native work itself was small: on an idle scheduler `put_columns` took
83 µs a chunk, a ring chunk 131 µs (it was lit alone again from its image,
whose stored light the ring ignored) and `spawn_generated` 3 µs a chunk. The
rest was the dirty schedulers' queue on two cores, decoding and checking every
column once in its section and again in each neighbour's ring, and Elixir
building and tearing down tick containers. Encoding the chunk's first packet
on the owner (sections, heightmaps, and a search for block-entity carriers
that decoded a list of thousands of states per chunk) cost another 194 and
232 µs a chunk in `call` callbacks.

## Design

- **Column handles.** A full job decodes and checks its column once on a
  generation thread (`column_handle_background`) into a resource holding the
  column (shared sections), the light it was lit with alone and its first
  packet's parts. The full chunk carries it as `:column` beside its image,
  which the log still stores.
- **Install from handles.** `put_columns` takes the handles (normal
  scheduler for a few chunks, `Native.dispatch`): the store clones the
  section `Arc`s and light only crosses the new chunks' faces. The siblings'
  and neighbour groups' rings get the same handles: `halo_put` shares the
  sections and installs the stored light, as it does for the section's own
  chunks (a core test and a real chunk show it lights as one lit there), so a
  ring chunk is neither decoded nor lit again.
- **Prepared packets.** The job encodes the chunk packet's sections and
  heightmaps with the dimension's spec (`Blocks.packet_spec/1`) and finds its
  block-entity carriers (now a native set installed once,
  `carriers_install`, also for every later `chunk_entities/2`). The store
  keeps them per chunk until its first packet takes them (`chunk_prepared`),
  only while the column's sections and biomes are the same `Arc`s: the
  prepared parts hold them, so any write clones them first and no longer
  matches. Later packets encode on the owner as before.
- **First creatures** run on a normal scheduler (`spawn_generated`, a few
  µs a chunk).
- **Ticks and post-processing off the heap.** Generated ticks of chunks
  outside simulation go straight into frozen containers
  (`ScheduledTicks.schedule_generated/3`, equal to scheduling then freezing);
  a chunk's post-processing marks and fluid ticks wait as one binary
  (`{:frozen, binary}`, `RegionGeneration.post_entry/1`) until the chunk
  first ticks. Post-processing itself stays in the block phase
  ([081g](VMC-081.md#slices) owns it).
- **One frame per commit interval.** A delivery cuts no frame: the
  section only registers the hand-over to run once the next cut is durable,
  and the group's 50 ms commit timer cuts the columns with everything else.

## Results

Two matched pairs, `a75a314c` (`valimc-bench:head081x`) against this slice on
the same base, explore at 60 blocks per second on two pinned cores of the
development Mac's Linux VM, 60 seconds, interleaved. Other agents' builds and
benches shared the host, so throughput varied (the generation threads set
the chunks per minute here, not the owner); the owner's time per chunk is the
comparison. Per minute:

| | Before 1 | After 1 | Before 2 | After 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Chunks generated | 2,860 | 2,694 | 4,315 | 2,641 |
| Generation callbacks | 13.76 s (5.41 ms mean) | 1.76 s (0.68 ms) | 16.46 s (4.65 ms) | 2.45 s (0.97 ms) |
| Generation callback p99 | 20 ms | 7.5 ms | 20 ms | 7.5 ms |
| Commit callbacks | 0.72 s | 1.58 s | 0.56 s | 1.87 s |
| Chunk requests (`call`) | 1.45 s | 0.42 s | 1.51 s | 0.76 s |
| Ring light messages (`light`) | 1.88 s | 0.27 s | 1.90 s | 0.66 s |
| Owner busy outside ticks | 23.8 s | 10.0 s | 26.6 s | 12.4 s |
| ... per chunk generated | 8.3 ms | 3.7 ms | 6.2 ms | 4.7 ms |
| Generation + commit + requests per chunk | 5.6 ms | 1.4 ms | 4.3 ms | 1.9 ms |
| Completed TPS (mean) | 19.8 | 19.3 | 19.0 | 19.0 |
| Tick mean / p99 | 18.6 / 75 ms | 17.0 / 75 ms | 18.9 / 100 ms | 23.1 / 100 ms |
| Owner heap at the end | 125 MiB | 68 MiB | 136 MiB | 115 MiB |

A delivery's callback fell by 80–87%; the frames it no longer cuts show up in
the commit timer's callbacks (about +1 s a minute), and the owner's time
outside ticks per chunk generated fell by about half. The tick moved with the
host's load (after 2 ran beside another agent's image build); after 1 had one
1-second stall with the player's chunk missing 1.15 s while its terrain was
still generating. A mobs pair was not taken (the coordinator measures all
slices together on a quiet host).

## Left

- Light still crosses a new chunk's faces on the owner (about 50 µs a chunk
  on a normal scheduler); vanilla's light status with its neighbour
  dependency in the pipeline would remove it.
- The Elixir part of an install (full-chunk parts, entity records, the
  section's maps, the group's section bookkeeping) is now most of a
  delivery's 0.7–1 ms.
- The explicit full collection after section merges stays (081b measured
  the alternatives as slower).
- A neighbour's `hello` still sends images without light, lit alone in the
  ring.

Acceptance:

- Generated chunks, their light and their packets equal the reference
  stages; adoption needs no decode, relight alone or dirty hop on the owner.
- Matched two-core explore runs: owner time per chunk adopted falls, chunks
  per minute and the tick no worse; a mobs pair shows no regression.

Checks: worldgen background (new: a handle installs as its image, lights a
neighbour's ring as the image lit there, its prepared packet equals the
owner's encoding once and not after a write), tick pipeline (new: frozen
generated ticks equal scheduling then freezing), scheduler, decorated world,
region generation, region light, light updates, region lifecycle, region log,
storage, fluid region, directory recovery, blocks, chunk view, connection,
view distance, dimension travel, player handoff, anvil, frontier, proto,
structures and spawn search tests pass; `valimc_core` column tests (new: a ring
chunk with its light alone lights as one lit there), clippy without new
warnings. `check_vanilla.py --changed` selected 119 stages (a `column.rs`
change reaches every stage that installs block facts); all passed, world
settings extraction not verified (no cached regeneration); `chunk_view`,
`heightmap` and `chunk_height` pass. After rebasing onto 081g1/081g2,
`light`, `chunk_sections`, `chunk_view`, `worldgen_live`,
`natural_spawning` and `spawn` pass again.
