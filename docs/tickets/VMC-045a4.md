# VMC-045a4 — The dragon's body and multipart combat

Status: Foundation
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-045a3](VMC-045a3.md), [VMC-037n](VMC-037n.md),
[VMC-037o](VMC-037o.md).

The section's entity store owns one living dragon. Its eight client-created
parts reserve consecutive network IDs and route melee and projectile hits to
the same body. Its phase metadata, sitting damage and death time persist;
head/neck hits take full damage and other parts reduce it. The following
slice connects the complete flight model and world effects, then the arena
spawn, boss bar, exit and respawn lifecycle follow in the parent.

Acceptance:

- Native fixtures match part damage, sitting projectile immunity, hurt
  cooldown, takeoff after sitting damage and flying/sitting lethal hits.
- Each spawned/restored dragon reserves one root plus eight part IDs; handoff
  keeps them. Parts never become independent stored entities or processes.
- Owner and socket checks exercise actual melee/projectile part routing and
  metadata, with subsequent player input still admitted.
- The finale client batch checks head/body hits and phase reactions after
  flight and the arena lifecycle are live. This model is a foundation until
  those slices make the full fight playable.

## Implementation and evidence (October 24, 2026)

One root reserves nine consecutive network IDs. Only it enters the store,
durable record and tracker; transfer retains the reservation and a disk
restore allocates a fresh range. The observation contains eight bounded part
boxes, while the full flight history stays with the owner. A client's part
ID selects that box for melee reach and routes its hit to the root UUID.
Projectile collision uses the eight boxes; potions and clouds still see the
single living root, avoiding duplicate effects.

The shared hurt path exposes subclass admission, killing-blow and knockback
hooks. Dragon parts reduce damage with native float rounding; head and neck
take full damage. The three podium phases ignite and reject arrows, tridents
and player wind charges; hovering remains vulnerable. Perching damage over a
quarter of maximum health starts takeoff. Flying lethal hits leave one health
and enter dying; perched deaths wait for the later 200-tick actor rather than
the ordinary twenty-tick removal or immediate XP. Explicit killing removes
the dragon. Part attacks cannot be critical hits, show no living-target damage
indicator and wear the player's weapon against the parent as vanilla does.

`DragonDamageOracle` probes all eleven native phases, eight parts, ten damage
sources and two starting health/cooldown scenes through seven consecutive
hits: 1,760 sequences / 12,320 hits, plus eleven exact phase-metadata packets.
They match admission, health, cooldown, last hurt, sitting damage, phase and
projectile ignition, including the part's base immunity check before it
forwards a hit to the root. Generated entity facts supply the phase field's index
and serializer. Focused entity, combat and socket checks pass 29 tests,
including range reservation/restore, perched arrow bounce and ignition,
flying head damage, socket attacks on part IDs and subsequent player input.
The affected vanilla run passed 39 stages; after the final part-admission
correction, all sixteen newly affected stages passed. The strengthened dead
body timer check and remaining dragon checks then passed all three tests.
The publication, documentation and layering audits pass.
