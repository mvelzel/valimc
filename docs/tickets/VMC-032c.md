# VMC-032c — Remaining containers

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Lane: Containers, after [VMC-004b](VMC-004b.md).

Shulker boxes keep their contents when broken and carried, ender chests
hold per-player contents, barrels and the rest of 26.3's containers work,
and an occupied furnace can be removed ([VMC-034f](VMC-034f.md)). Every
container menu is a durable transaction: a crash never loses or
duplicates an item, and contents survive a restart and a group handoff.
The brewing stand belongs to the Effects lane (VMC-034o) and villager
trading to the Villages lane (VMC-044b). Slices are written when 004b
lands.
