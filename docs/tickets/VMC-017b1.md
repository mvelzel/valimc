# VMC-017b1 — Freezing damage

Status: Live composition automated-verified; graphical gate pending. October 15, 2026.
Existing [VMC-015c](VMC-015c.md) owns completed freeze accumulation/rendering.
Existing health, death/respawn, landing and fall-protection implementations must
not be repeated. Their outstanding graphical gates stay in the current queue.

Acceptance: survival players at native full freezing take the periodic freeze
damage, with native wearables/mode immunity and applicable enchantment defenses.
Use the existing player owner, logical clock and fresh fenced environment samples.
Bound/coalesce delayed work; stale geometry, replayed ticks, death and respawn
must not duplicate a consequence or reuse an earlier body's pending damage.
Preserve health/inventory journals and durable fatal cleanup. No new global tick
owner, per-block processes or independent damage timer.

Reference: pinned Mojang 26.3 `LivingEntity.aiStep/hurtServer`,
`DamageSources.freeze`, damage-type tags and `LivingEntity.canFreeze`. Jar SHA1:
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`. `aiStep` checks tickCount modulo40,
full freezing and canFreeze, then requests 1.0 freeze damage; its thaw/immunity
updates precede this check. Cadence/order are verified against pinned bytecode;
original probes exercise native eligibility, defenses and codecs below.

Source tags and defenses must be verified for freeze, rather than reusing fall's
Feather Falling rule. Resistance/absorption and other environmental sources remain
follow-ups unless needed for this slice.

Client scenario: actual survival powder-snow contact, visible frost and at least
two separated health losses; leaving/wearing protective gear stops subsequent
damage. Creative immunity, damaged reconnect and a fatal freeze/respawn retain
inventory consistency. Delegate this after the already queued client scenarios.

Implementation: the admitted owner clock advances a transient 40-tick phase.
A single pending bit coalesces missed periods while geometry is delayed. Only a
fresh, fenced observation consumes it, after thaw and current equipment immunity;
the existing health cooldown, protection and durable death lane applies damage.
Death clears the phase/pending bit; respawn starts a new environment. Neither
transient field is saved. No extra timer, process or unbounded backlog is added.
`PlayerDamageProtection` generalizes the earlier fall-only helper: Protection
applies to freeze; Feather Falling does not. Freeze bypasses ordinary armor/wear.
