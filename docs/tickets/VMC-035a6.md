# VMC-035a6 — Automatic item simulation

Status: In progress. Parent: VMC-035a. VMC-035a5 is validated and published.

Acceptance: normal player drops use the saved native launch intent and advance
automatically through gravity, contacts and currents. Landing, owner crossings and
fresh reconnect preserve one stack. No controller-supplied steps in the client test.
Delayed geometry/storage cannot publish stale steps, run overlapping simulations,
lose transfer receipts or accumulate unbounded work. Measure moving and resting
populations across multiple owners, including256 active items in one owner.

Use owner-local cadence and immutable cohorts of at most32; the256-item limit must
not silently reduce each moving item's cadence to one update per eight ticks.
Bound simultaneous jobs globally and per owner, retain epoch/revision/deadline
fences and define overload behavior. Geometry/disk waits stay outside owners.
Partition accepted steps into owner-local motion and durable border intents;
destination capacity/backpressure retains source authority until withdrawal commits.
Ordinary saves use VMC-035a4; critical transfers/removals retain their stronger gate.

Integrate launch at every player drop producer, retaining canonical retry payloads.
Recheck sampled geometry when composing results, including local edits and neighbor
incarnation changes. Block edits must wake affected resting items. Separate movement
work from age/pickup-delay deadlines so stationary populations need no full geometry
scan each tick. Preserve sentinel counters; despawn commits removal with receipts.

Reference: pinned26.3 `ItemEntity.tick`, `Entity.move` and existing VMC-035a/A3 native
probes. Vanilla refreshes low-horizontal-speed grounded movement every four ticks
and discards at age6000; BEAM scheduling can differ while preserving gameplay.
Do not copy runtime-ID staggering as an ownership model. Embedded escape, block
effects, merge/pickup and broader lifecycle behavior need their own verified slices.

Client scenario: fresh direct launch, actual Q/whole-stack drops from several poses,
observe launch/fall/landing and water transport, cross x128 naturally, alter support,
then stop/relaunch and verify conservation and saved motion. One dedicated tester
owns the verdict; record limitations without promoting partial scope to full parity.

Local cadence checkpoint: each owner advances counters independently of geometry
and runs one bounded coordinator, at most eight geometry jobs at once, cohorts≤32,
owner deadline350ms. A separate item geometry pool preserves player query capacity.
All256 active items participate; disjoint cohorts split to fit read budgets.
Full-precision physical revisions fence results independently of quantized packets
and counter revisions. Existing durable handoffs handle crossings; despawn retains
birth receipts. Tiny grounded/floating motion (horizontal squared≤1e-10, zero Y)
sleeps against verified frame stamps; either block-edit path invalidates them.
Manual fixtures disable cadence for newly loaded owners; production enables it.
