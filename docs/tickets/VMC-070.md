# VMC-070 — The client's view distance decides which chunks it gets

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M2 real worlds (player-visible)
Depends on: [VMC-069](VMC-069.md) (replication module), [VMC-065](VMC-065.md)
(region publications).

Before this slice ValiMC ignored `client_information` and gave every player a
fixed square of `view_distance` (3) chunks around it; the login packet told the
client 3, so its render distance slider had no effect beyond 3 chunks.

Vanilla keeps the distance each client requests (`client_information`, sent in
configuration and again whenever the player changes it), tracks
`clamp(requested, 2, server view distance)` chunks around the player's chunk and
re-applies that view every tick, so a change takes effect at once. The server
view distance is `view-distance` (default 10, clamped to 2..32); the login packet
carries it and `simulation-distance` (default 10). A view is a cylinder with a
ring of neighbours: a chunk is tracked when `max(0, |dx| − 2)² + max(0, |dz| − 2)²
< distance²`, so distance 2 is a 7×7 square and distance 10 holds 473 chunks.
Leaving chunks the client has get `forget_level_chunk`; pending ones are dropped.
`set_chunk_cache_radius` and `set_simulation_distance` go out only when the
server's own distances change, not when a client changes its own.

Scope: that view and its updates, vanilla's chunk send pacing (the larger views
need it), and the entity tracking ranges that depend on the view distance.

Acceptance:

- `client_information` is decoded in configuration and play (language at most 16
  characters, signed-byte view distance, the remaining settings), malformed
  settings disconnect, and the requested distance is kept per connection
  (vanilla's default 2 until the client sends one).
- `view_distance` defaults to 10 and `simulation_distance` to 10, set by
  `VALIMC_VIEW_DISTANCE` and `VALIMC_SIMULATION_DISTANCE` (2..32); the login packet
  carries both.
- Tracked chunks equal vanilla's `ChunkTrackingView` for every distance, and a
  view change (move, distance change, teleport) enters and leaves the same chunks
  (oracle stage `chunk_view`). A changed distance in play applies immediately;
  the chunk cache centre is sent only when the centre moves.
- Chunk sending follows `PlayerChunkSender`: per 50 ms tick the quota grows by the
  client's desired rate (9 at first, then 0.01..64 from each
  `chunk_batch_received`) up to one tick's worth, at most one unacknowledged batch
  before the first acknowledgement and ten after, nearest chunks first. Chunks of
  a region still loading stay pending instead of blocking the connection; a batch
  asks its region owners at once.
- Players see a peer within `min(32, view distance)` chunks horizontally and items
  and XP orbs within `min(6, view distance)` chunks (vanilla `clientTrackingRange`,
  generated into `priv/protocol/entities.json`).
- Player-visible: render distances 2, 6 and 16 against server view distance 10
  show 2, 6 and 10 chunks of terrain, and changing the render distance mid-game
  loads or drops chunks without a reconnect.

Design:

- `Valimc.Session.ChunkView`: views `{center, distance}`, `contains?/2`,
  `chunks/1` (nearest first), `difference/2` over the union's bounding box like
  vanilla, `intersects?/2` for the regions a view needs.
- `Valimc.Session.ChunkSender`: the pacing state (desired rate, quota,
  unacknowledged batches). `Replication.chunk_tick/1` refills it on the 50 ms
  `publish_movement` tick; `stream_chunks/1` (also run right after a view change
  or the play teleport confirmation) spends it. Acknowledgements never send, so
  they cannot outrun the rate.
- `World.ready_chunks/1` sends one request per chunk with
  `:gen_server.send_request` and collects the replies against one deadline; a
  chunk whose region is loading, cannot be admitted or dies is left pending.
- `Replication.update_view/1` compares `{centre, distance}` with the tracked view
  and admits every region of a new view at once, as vanilla's player tickets start
  loading the whole view; `sees?/3` and `entity_chunks/2` apply the tracking
  ranges.

Limits and known differences:

- The simulation distance is only what clients are told: regions still simulate
  every loaded chunk, and loaded regions stay loaded (chunk tickets, VMC-013).
- `entity-broadcast-range-percentage` is fixed at 100.
- The server distances cannot change at runtime yet, so `set_chunk_cache_radius`
  and `set_simulation_distance` are never sent.
- A chunk is "ready" when its region is loaded; vanilla checks each chunk.
- Vanilla pairs an entity only once its chunk was sent. Peers here are tracked
  from the join on anywhere in the view (as before, so they appear with the first
  chunks); items and orbs only in sent chunks.
- Peers' tab-list entries still come and go with visibility (unchanged).

Progress (October 21, 2026):

- Oracle stage `chunk_view` passes: 31 views (distances 2..32, centres up to
  ±1,875,000 chunks) and 206 view changes (moves, distance changes, teleports,
  empty views) enter and leave exactly vanilla's chunks. Chunks per distance: 2:
  49, 4: 117, 6: 213, 10: 473, 16: 1,057, 32: 3,725.
- `test/chunk_view_test.exs` (6): clamps, view shape and order, differences,
  region intersection, `PlayerChunkSender` pacing (start rate, the
  unacknowledged-batch limits, quota cap, rate clamp with NaN and infinities) and
  the `client_information` layout and refusals.
- `test/view_distance_connection_test.exs` (3), real TCP with server distance 10:
  requested 4, 16, 1 and −7 receive exactly the 117, 473, 49 and 49 chunks of
  distances 4, 10, 2 and 2, with login radius and simulation distance 10, a first
  batch of 9 and no batch over 64; in play, 4 → 6 streams the 96 entering chunks
  and 6 → 3 forgets the 132 leaving ones, and repeating 3 sends nothing; malformed
  settings disconnect.
- Full socket suites: 571 tests pass in 151 s. Each join now streams the vanilla
  minimum of 49 chunks at the vanilla rate instead of 9 at once, which costs a
  median 74 ms per socket test (73 s → 120 s of test time).
- Flaky tests found on the way and fixed: `WeatherConnectionTest` waited about
  five minutes and failed when an earlier test left rain falling (the start event
  fires only when the rain level crosses 0.2), now it starts dry;
  `CommandConnectionTest` left `/weather rain 600` behind and raced the clock
  (6001 for 6000), now it restores the weather and stops time meanwhile;
  `ClockConnectionTest` assumed the clocks equal the game time, now it compares
  their offset; `MenuOverflowTest`'s barrier exceeded the 300-packet `await`
  budget with its 252 items.
- The load benchmark (`bench/load.py`) now waits for 49 chunks per client at view
  distance 2; earlier saved runs used 1.
