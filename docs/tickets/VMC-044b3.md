# VMC-044b3 — Restocks and prices

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044b2](VMC-044b2.md).

Part of [VMC-044b](VMC-044b.md).

- **Restocks:** `WorkAtPoi`'s `useWorkstation` restocks when
  `shouldRestock` holds (at most twice a day, 2400 ticks apart; a new day
  by half a day or the overworld day's period count), with `updateDemand`;
  a new day catches up the missed demand updates (`catchUpDemand`).
- **Prices:** `getModifiedCostCount` (demand times the price multiplier,
  the special price, clamped to the stack size), `updateSpecialPrices` on
  opening, at a restock, a level-up and new gossip: reputation (VMC-044c
  fills the gossip) and Hero of the Village (`0.3 + 0.0625 × amplifier`
  of the base cost, at least one).

Acceptance:

- Village scenes with a trader at its job site restocking through days
  equal vanilla's offers, restock times and counts.
- Prices for reputations and Hero amplifiers equal `MerchantOffer`'s.
- Client scenario (M4 run): exhaust a trade, watch the villager work at
  its job site and the trade come back.

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**Restocks** (`village::merchant::should_restock` and `restock`, from
`WorkAtPoi`'s start after the work sound): a new day (12000 ticks since its
last restock, or a later overworld day than it last checked) catches up the
missed demand (uses back, `updateDemand` once per restock it missed, new
prices for a trading player) and resets its count; then, with none today
or one 2400 ticks ago and a trade used, it restocks (demand, uses back,
prices, the time and the count). Its last restock and count are saved;
the day it last checked and its trading player's Hero amplifier are not
(cleared on restore). A villager without offers at a check asks its owner
(`{:merchant, {:offers_wanted, uuid, demand updates}}`), which draws them
after its turn with the catch-up's demand updates, as vanilla's
`getOffers` would have made them there.

**Prices:** `updateSpecialPrices` in the merchant (`start` with the
player's Hero of the Village amplifier, which the interaction carries; on
new offers while trading, at restocks and a day's catch-up): special prices
reset, less `0.3 + 0.0625 × amplifier` of the base cost (at least one);
reputation stays 0 until VMC-044c's gossip. `Menus.Merchant.cost_count/1`
is `getModifiedCostCount`. A player's open menu takes changed offers from
its merchant each tick and sends them again.

Checks: stage `village` gains three librarian scenes at a lectern (a
restock with demand, a new day's catch-up, no third restock in a day):
10894 ticks of 18 scenes equal vanilla's, now with each offer's uses and
demand and the restock count and time; Hero of the Village's special prices
and costs at six amplifiers (none, 0, 1, 2, 4, 9) equal
`updateSpecialPrices`.
