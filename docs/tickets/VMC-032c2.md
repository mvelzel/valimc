# VMC-032c2 — Shulker boxes

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md). Depends on [032c1](VMC-032c1.md), [VMC-004b6](VMC-004b6.md).

The plain and sixteen dyed shulker boxes.

- Placement faces the clicked face; placing an item that carries a
  `container` (and a `custom_name`) fills the new entry with it.
- Opening needs room for the lid (`ShulkerBoxBlock.canOpen`: the box's
  shape moved half a block along its facing must not collide); the lid
  opens and closes over ten ticks (block event 1 with the opener count),
  with `block.shulker_box.open`/`close`.
- The `shulker_box` menu; its slots refuse items that cannot fit inside a
  container item (`Item.canFitInsideContainerItems`: shulker boxes), and
  so do hoppers and droppers (`canPlaceItemThroughFace`).
- Breaking drops the box with its contents as a `container` component (the
  loot table's `copy_components`), in creative too when it holds anything
  (`playerWillDestroy`); explosions drop it the same way.
- Contents survive a restart and a handoff in the entry, and in the item
  as a component wherever the item goes (a chest, an item entity, a
  player's inventory).

Acceptance: placement and loot oracles with contents and names; the
`canOpen` clearance cases; the menu's slot restriction; durable
place/break cycles that never lose or duplicate contents. Client scenario
(M4 batch): fill a shulker box, break it, carry it, place it elsewhere and
find the contents; a box blocked above does not open; a shulker box cannot
go into another.

## Result (October 31, 2026)

`Blocks.ShulkerBox` is the `ShulkerBoxBlock` behaviour for all seventeen
boxes; its block-entity kind `:shulker_box` is a 27-slot storage kind
(`Blocks.Storage`, storage id 1), so the entry, the lifecycle, chunk
carriers, Anvil import, hoppers and comparators take it as they take a
barrel. Placement already follows the generated placement facts (the
clicked face).

Contents travel with the box. A held box whose only component is
`minecraft:container` places (`Placement.placeable_stack?`), and the
player's placement passes its slots to the owner (`Region.place_block/5`),
which fills the new entry in the commit that creates it. A box with a
custom name, lock or loot table still does not place (names are 032c5);
one whose container fills a slot past the 27th is refused rather than
truncated. Removed entries now carry their block. A retired shulker entry
never spills: `ContainerSpills.add/4` pops the box of that colour holding
its slots (`ShulkerBox.drop/2`, the trimmed `ItemContainerContents`) as its
loot table's `copy_components` does, and a survival break drops that record
in place of the player's plain loot box (`without_boxes/2`). A creative
break (`drop: :creative`) drops a filled box at the block centre with the
default pickup delay and an empty one not at all. Every path stays in the
existing joint block/item/container commit, and a loot-table box unpacks
before it breaks or opens (`LootChests`).

Opening: the owner reads the cell in front of a closed box (in another
section from published blocks) and `MenuAccess` refuses
`:blocked_shulker_box` when that block's collision meets the half block the
lid needs (`ShulkerBox.lid_clear?/3`, `Kernel.obstructs?`). The menu is its
own `Menu.shulker_box` (window type 20, `container.shulkerBox`), whose
container slots refuse shulker boxes for clicks, swaps, drags and
shift-click; `ItemTransfer.can_place?` refuses them for hoppers and
droppers. The first opener and the last to leave send block event 1 for the
box's own block with the chest lid route and publication, and
`block.shulker_box.open`/`close` at the centre (volume 0.5, the chest pitch
draw).

Not done, as stated: explosions still leave every block-entity cell
standing, shulker boxes included; a piston does not destroy an occupied
box; the lid check ignores entities that collide like blocks (boats,
shulkers) and counts a box as closed as soon as its last viewer leaves
(vanilla's lid takes ten ticks); the intermediate opener-count block events
vanilla sends between the first and last viewer are not sent (the lid reads
only the first and last).

Oracles: `ChestSoundOracle` records 12 shulker box states through a real
`ShulkerBoxBlockEntity` (events, sounds and draws for three viewers with a
spectator) and the shulker sound ids (`priv/protocol/chest_sounds.json`);
`ChestMenuOracle` records two `ShulkerBoxMenu`s (63 slot identities, each
slot's `mayPlace` for four probes, the titled open body); `LootOracle blocks`
fills every shulker box case with random stacks and the check compares
`ShulkerBox.drop/2` with vanilla's `getDrops` (357 filled boxes).

Tests: `shulker_box_region_test.exs` (facing from four clicked faces and a
placement with contents; the slab clearance cases, lid events and sounds,
an open box admitting another viewer; the menu's click and shift-click and
a hopper refusing a box, a comparator; survival and creative breaks with
contents riding the item and no spill; handoff and restart) and a shulker
case in `anvil_block_entity_test.exs`. The handoff tests here and in
`barrel_region_test.exs` now flush each group once (they raced a stopped
group). The affected container, chest, hopper, loot, lifecycle, placement,
menu, mining, Anvil and spill suites pass (77 tests). Eighteen vanilla
stages pass: chest_menu, chest_sound, block_loot, placement,
container_drop, hoppers, block_entities, loot, anvil_content, redstone,
table_menu, crafting_table_access and the chest access, click, view,
opening, placement and block entity stages. The client scenario is pending.
