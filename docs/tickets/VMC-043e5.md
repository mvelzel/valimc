# VMC-043e5 — Dolphins

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e4](VMC-043e4.md).

The ocean's smart swimmers (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- A dolphin swims to random spots by vanilla's smooth swimming controls
  (turning ten degrees a tick, pitching toward its target, its input split
  between forward and up), looks around with the smooth look control,
  travels in water at its own speed (sinking slowly without a target) and
  leaps out of the water now and then.
- It breathes air: 4800 ticks under water, then it swims up for air,
  moving itself along as it goes; out of water it is full at once. On land
  it dries out over two minutes (rain keeps it moist), flopping about, and
  then is hurt each tick.
- Hit, it bites back and its kin join in; it shies from guardians; it
  swims along with a swimming player and gives Dolphin's Grace (now a live
  effect: the player swims at 0.96 drag). It plays with items in the
  water, carrying one and tossing it, and eats fish from a player's hand.

Acceptance:

- Dolphin behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) equal vanilla's tick
  for tick.
- Its combat facts, loot and entity data match the oracle.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- **Kernel.** A vertical input (`yya`); a move control's own inputs with
  `Control::Set` (`set_inputs`); the dolphin travel; a goal's own move
  before the travel (`pre_move`, `BreathAirGoal`) and its swim sound; the
  eye's fluid where the move left it (`eye_after`) and on a first tick
  (`Kernel.eye_block/3`); the sink as `add(0, -0.005, 0)`.
- **AI.** `Mobs.Dolphin` with its treasure, swim-with-player, jump,
  move-to-item and play goals; shared `Goals.BreathAir` (the rest of the AI
  reads where its move took the mob, `BreathAir.predicted/1`),
  `Goals.TryFindLiquid`, `Goals.FollowRidden`. Kind hooks `breath/1` (air
  in the base tick, before the ambient sound), `look_control/1`,
  `pick_up/2`, `mob_interact/2`, `max_air/0`; `AI.play/4` (a sound at a
  fixed pitch). Sensed players say whether they swim.
- **Region and players.** `{:effect, target, actions}` (a gift without a
  hurt), thrown items with their own pickup delay, Dolphin's Grace in the
  player's water travel.

Checks: stage `mob_ai`: 42 scenes, 14795 ticks equal (a dolphin swimming
and leaping, one low on air swimming up, one drying and flopping on land,
one biting back, one swimming with a player and gracing it); see the
commit for the other stages.

Not modelled yet: treasure (the structure search cannot run inside a
mob's tick, so a fed dolphin gives up at once), babies from spawn groups,
following boats (no boats), the exact collisions of `BreathAirGoal`'s own
move for the rest of that tick's AI (it reads the move without them), an
item's own water state (a dolphin plays with items whose block is water).

Client scenario (milestone handoff): in an ocean, `/summon dolphin`: it
swims and leaps. Swim (sprint in water) near it: it follows and you swim
faster (Dolphin's Grace). Hit it: it bites. Give it a cod: it eats it.
Drop it on land: it flops and dries out.
