# VMC-077j3 — Block rules across a group's sections

Status: Done
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077j1](VMC-077j1.md), [VMC-077j2](VMC-077j2.md),
[VMC-077m](VMC-077m.md) (the block-update engine and its border function).

Before: the block-update engine's border (`Live::owns`) was the section's own
generated chunks. An update at a cell of the next section, or a rule that read
one, was dropped even when the same group owned that section, so a wire, a
torch or a flower one block across a section line did not react.

After: inside a group the rules read and write across section borders and
stop only at the group's edge, as VMC-077m planned for this ticket.

Design:

- **One run over the group.** The group hands each section, as its code runs,
  what the rules read of its siblings (`RegionData.siblings`: each sibling's
  store, its chunks not generated yet and whether a planning transfer holds
  it). `Rules.run/5` passes the siblings' Region resources to the NIF, which
  locks every store of the group for the run (only the owning group runs its
  sections' rules; readers take one store at a time) and runs the engine over
  them: `Live` finds a cell's store by its chunk, and `owns` is "a generated
  chunk of one of the group's sections". Placement's `canSurvive`
  (`first_surviving`) reads the same way.
- **Each section commits its part.** The run's changes, destroyed blocks and
  ticks are split by section. The section commits its own as before; the
  others go out in `sibling_rules`, and the group applies each to its sibling
  (`RegionData.apply_rules/2`, a raw batch that starts no updates of its own)
  right after the section's call returns and before the group's next cut, so
  a run that spans sections is one log record. A run that drops loot in a
  section behind a planning transfer waits for the next tick, whole, as it
  did for its own section.
- **Light** already crossed inside a group by the group's mailbox
  ([VMC-077j1](VMC-077j1.md)).

Acceptance:

- Wire beside a redstone block one section over stays unpowered while the
  sections are separate groups, carries power 15 and 14 once they share one,
  and loses it when the block in the other section is removed
  (`region_group_test.exs`).
- The engine's border is the group's edge (`cargo test`, `live.rs`).

Measurements (October 22, 2026, `MIX_ENV=bench` on 232f86f3, 10-core host
shared with other sessions at load 10 to 12; `bench/regionizer.exs`, raw
results in ignored `bench/results/regionizer-077j.json`; each section
generated, with 64 dropped items and one edited block per chunk, all chunks
simulated, `:manual` ticks, medians):

| Group | Tick | Per section |
|---|---|---|
| 1 section | 0.13 ms | 0.13 ms |
| 4 sections | 0.52 ms | 0.13 ms |
| 16 sections | 2.22 ms | 0.14 ms |
| 64 sections | 10.74 ms | 0.17 ms |

| A section joins a group of | Merge (giver) | Adopter | Split |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 150 µs | 7 µs | 116 µs |
| 8 | 65 µs | 181 µs | 121 µs |
| 32 | 71 µs | 616 µs | 122 µs |

The adopter's pause is its adoption and one full collection of its heap, so
it grows with the group. Two groups of 16 sections far apart, 20 ticks each:
87 ms one after the other, 46 ms at once (1.88 ×): they tick in parallel.
Measuring showed two effects the numbers above exclude: items do not move
while an item plan is in flight, so the bench waits for none before each
sample; and a moved section ticked at twice its cost until the adopter's
heap was collected once, which the adopter now does after each merge.

Checks: `cargo test` (valimc_core, including the group border in
`live.rs`), `cargo clippy`; `test/region_group_test.exs` (6),
`test/regionizer_test.exs` (2), `test/region_light_test.exs`; on 232f86f3
(rebased on a997e797): `mix test.fast` 474 of 474, `mix test --only slow
--only tcp` 200 of 200, `check_vanilla.py --changed` 209 of 209 stages (all
selected: `config/config.exs` changed). Earlier runs on the way found and
fixed: tests that read a group's "first" section or the stripe of a key
rather than of its group, `abort_box` sent to a group the section had left,
a section lost when its adopter died before adopting it, and
`World.owner/1` answering busy while a section moved.

Client: none per slice; the VMC-077 milestone run covers play across
sections and groups.

Deferred:

- **Item motion inside a group** still crosses a section line through the
  entity handoff (a durable intent, a delivery job to the same group, a
  receipt, a clear): correct, but three frames where one group commit would
  do. A direct move between sections of one group belongs with VMC-077h's
  handoff primitive and entity store, which are changing now.
- Behaviours with effects of their own (portal shape updates) still read only
  their section.
- A run locks every store of the group and hands the engine every sibling,
  so its fixed cost grows with the group; a run could take only the
  sections within its reach.
