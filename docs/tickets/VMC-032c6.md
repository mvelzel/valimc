# VMC-032c6 — Other item-holding blocks

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md).

The block entities that hold items outside a chest menu: the crafter (a
3×3 grid with disabled slots, crafting on a redstone pulse), the decorated
pot (one stack, filled by use, wobbling), the chiseled bookshelf (six books
by the face's hit position), the shelf (three items, swapped with the
hotbar), the lectern (a book, page and comparator output), the jukebox (a
disc and its song) and the campfire (four items cooking). Each may split
into its own slice when it starts; each is a durable entry with an oracle
for its interaction.
