# VMC-044d3 — Evoker, fangs and wololo

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044d1](VMC-044d1.md).

Part of [VMC-044d](VMC-044d.md).

- The spellcaster base: the spell byte, `SpellTicks`, the casting and
  use-spell goals.
- Fangs: a line of 16 or rings of 5 and 8 near the target; the fang entity
  (warmup, 6 magic damage past owner and allies, event 4, gone after 22
  ticks, saved).
- Wololo: a blue sheep within 16 turns red while mobs may grief.
- The evoker's avoidance of players and its loot (a totem, an emerald).

Acceptance: scenes (fangs in a line and a ring, fleeing, wololo with and
without mob griefing) match vanilla, fang hurts included; an evoker drops
a totem.
