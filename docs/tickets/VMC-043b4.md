# VMC-043b4 — Creepers and spiders

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Creepers as vanilla's `Creeper`: the swell goal, fleeing cats and ocelots,
exploding through VMC-050a's explosions (stronger when charged), ignition
with flint and steel, music discs when a skeleton kills one and heads from
a charged creeper's blast. Spiders and cave spiders: climbing, leaping at a
target, neutral by day, spawn effects on hard, cave spiders' poison, spider
jockeys' draws.

Acceptance:

- A creeper's swell, explosion and loot, and a spider's climbing, leap and
  bite match the oracle's fixtures.
- On the real client, a creeper hisses and blows up next to the player, and
  a spider climbs a wall after them.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Creeper` holds creepers: vanilla's goals (`SwellGoal` as
`Creeper.Swell`, fleeing cats and ocelots, a melee goal whose hits do
nothing), the fuse in `tick_start` (the hiss as it starts, the explosion at
30 through VMC-050a: power 3, 6 when charged, blocks only while mobs may
grief, drops decaying by `mob_explosion_drop_decay`; the creeper is then
discarded) and lighting by flint and steel or a fire charge
(`mob_interact/2`, the new `Mobs.interact/2` after leads and animals).

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Spider` holds spiders and cave spiders: vanilla's goals
(the shared `LeapAtTarget`, `Spider.Attack` giving up in the light, targets
only in the dark), climbing (`after_move` sets it from the kernel's
horizontal collision; the kernel climbs while it is set, and
`WallClimberNavigation` paths to the target's block where none leads),
`finalizeSpawn` (a skeleton jockey one time in a hundred, finished with the
same level random through `Spawn.companion`; on hard an endless effect,
with its attribute modifiers through `Mob.add_effect`) and the cave
spider's poisoning bite.

Loot: entity predicates read an attacker's type (`minecraft:entity_type`,
so a creeper a skeleton killed drops a music disc), and a count set below
zero grows from zero, as `ItemStack.getCount` reads it (a spider's eye).
The loot facts carry entity type tags.

Checks: `hostile_ai` (18 scenes, 5470 mob ticks bit for bit) adds a
creeper walking up and exploding, a lit and charged one, a spider leaping
and biting, a cave spider poisoning, a spider climbing a wall and one
neutral by day. `hostile_spawns` adds creepers, spiders and cave spiders
(11880 spawns in all). `loot` adds their tables (40500 deaths).

Not modelled yet: a creeper swelling from a fall, the lingering cloud of
its effects, charged creepers' heads from their victims, cobwebs not
holding spiders, spiders' poison immunity, riding (spider jockeys,
VMC-046).

Client scenario (with VMC-043b's handoff): survival, normal difficulty, at
night. `/summon creeper ~4 ~ ~`: it walks up, hisses, swells and explodes,
breaking blocks; light another with flint and steel: it explodes where it
stands. `/summon spider ~6 ~ ~` beside a wall: it chases and leaps at you,
and climbs the wall when you stand on top. `/summon cave_spider`: its bite
poisons you.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
