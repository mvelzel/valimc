# VMC-043b4 — Creepers and spiders

Status: Planned
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Creepers as vanilla's `Creeper`: the swell goal, fleeing cats and ocelots,
exploding through VMC-050a's explosions (stronger when charged), ignition
with flint and steel, music discs when a skeleton kills one and heads from
a charged creeper's blast. Spiders and cave spiders: climbing, leaping at a
target, neutral by day, spawn effects on hard, cave spiders' poison, spider
jockeys' draws.

Acceptance:

- A creeper's swell, explosion and loot, and a spider's climbing, leap and
  bite match the oracle's fixtures.
