# VMC-077j2 — The regionizer merges and splits groups

Status: Done
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077j1](VMC-077j1.md) (group processes and handoff),
[VMC-077](VMC-077.md) (decision D1, option C; finding B8).

Before: after [VMC-077j1](VMC-077j1.md) every section was admitted into a
group of its own and stayed there, so neighbouring sections still ticked,
committed and interacted across processes.

After: one regionizer per dimension (`Valimc.World.Regionizer`) decides which
loaded sections share a group, and groups move sections between them with the
VMC-077j1 handoff.

Design:

- **Rule.** Folia's regionizer at section size: grid exponent 3 (an 8 × 8
  chunk section), create radius 1 and merge radius 1, so a section's reach is
  three sections (Chebyshev). Groups report the sections they load and unload
  (`Regionizer.loaded/2`, `unloaded/2`). A loaded section's group merges with
  every group that has a loaded section within reach, into the largest; on a
  tie the group that was there first, so a group's log stripe stays put while
  sections join it. Separate groups therefore keep their loaded chunks at least
  25 chunks apart, and nothing that can interact sits in two groups.
- **Split.** A group counts the sections that unloaded since its last
  recalculation; once that reaches a sixth of the sections it had then
  (Folia's dead-section ratio), the regionizer recomputes its connected
  pieces under the same reach and moves every piece but the largest into a new
  group (`Region.split/2`).
- **Serialization.** The regionizer is one process per dimension and makes
  one change at a time; each is a call the group handles between ticks, so a
  ticking group finishes its tick first. Merges and splits need no time
  offsets: every group numbers its ticks from the world's game time.
- **Scope.** Sections merge only within one world folder and tick mode
  (concurrent test worlds); `:regionizer` turns it off per section (tests
  that need separate owners). A restarted regionizer rebuilds from the
  Directory's routes; it watches groups and forgets a stopped group's
  sections.
- **Settled state.** `Regionizer.settle/1` answers once every report sent
  before it is handled, merges and splits made and routes moved; a section's
  load is reported before `World.owner/1` first answers for it, so tests
  assert grouping after a settle instead of polling. A merge of several
  groups moves them one after another, so a poll on two of them could pass
  before the third had moved (a flake in `regionizer_test.exs`).
- **Reports behind the groups.** One tick can unload several sections of a
  group, each with its own report, so the regionizer can decide a merge or
  split naming a section that is already gone. A hand-over moves the
  sections the group still holds and answers which (`{:ok, moved}`). The
  regionizer books only those, and an adopting group reports each section
  it adopts, so the regionizer's view converges whichever report comes
  first. A split that moved nothing stops the group it started. Before
  this, such a split was refused whole as `:invalid_handoff`. That happened
  once in the final VMC-077 client run after a 590-block teleport: nothing
  was lost, but the group stayed unsplit and the empty group lingered.
- **Sections gone before their report.** A load report whose section has no
  route any more merges nothing, so groups never join through a section that
  is gone. A merge that finds a source no longer holding a section it was
  thought to hold counts that section as gone from the merged group, and the
  group splits at once if it fell apart. Tests hold the sections that
  connect groups with tickets and release them on purpose, and they wait for
  a report to be sent (the section leaves its group's list, or an emptied
  group exits) rather than for its route to go, which happens first.
- **Tests** follow sections across groups: `TestRegion.section/2` resolves an
  owner handle wherever its section moved, and `TestDisk.stripe/2` returns the
  stripe of the group that commits the section.

Acceptance:

- Sections within three sections of each other share a group, eight apart do
  not; a section between them brings both groups into one with their state
  (`regionizer_test.exs`).
- A group whose bridge section unloads splits into its pieces.
- Two groups far apart tick in parallel (measurements).

Reference: Folia's regionizer rules (merge and split, dead-section ratio) as
the code audit summarizes them (`compare-paper-folia.md` §1–2, finding B8).

Measurements: see [VMC-077j3](VMC-077j3.md).

Checks: see [VMC-077j3](VMC-077j3.md).

Client: none per slice; the VMC-077 milestone run covers play across
groups.

Deferred:

- The regionizer merges on load and splits on unload only. Folia also
  merges when a region's sections grow towards another while ticking; here a
  section only joins groups when it loads, which is when it can first
  interact.
- A merge moves every section of the smaller group, one handoff each; a very
  large merge pauses the source group for the sum of their copies.
