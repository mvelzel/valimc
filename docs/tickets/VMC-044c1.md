# VMC-044c1 — Gossip and reputation

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044b3](VMC-044b3.md).

Part of [VMC-044c](VMC-044c.md).

- **Gossip** (`GossipContainer`, `GossipType`): per target its entries by
  type (major and minor negative and positive, trading) with weights,
  caps, daily decay and transfer decay; the weighted reputation; saved
  with the villager.
- **Events** (`onReputationEventFrom`): a trade (+2 trading), a hurt
  (+25 minor negative and angry particles for a player), a villager's
  murder told to the villagers that saw it (+25 major negative), a cure
  (+20 major positive, +25 minor positive).
- **Sharing:** `Villager.gossip` as two villagers meet
  (`TradeWithVillager`): up to ten entries drawn by weight from the other,
  each less its transfer decay; daily decay (`maybeDecayGossip`).
- **Reading it:** special prices (`reputation × price multiplier`), and
  `DefendVillageTargetGoal`: an iron golem targets a player any villager
  within its box holds at -100 or worse.

Acceptance:

- Village scenes with gossip (two villagers meeting, a villager hurt by a
  player, a golem near a hated player) equal vanilla's gossip, draws and
  targets tick by tick.
- Prices for reputations equal `updateSpecialPrices`.

Note: vanilla orders an entity's gossip by an identity hash of its types,
which differs between runs; scenes give each target one type, and ValiMC
orders types by their declaration.

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**Gossip** (`valimc_core::mobs::village::gossip`): `GossipContainer` with
`GossipType`'s weights, caps and decays: `add` (summed up to the cap,
under 2 removed), `decay`, `reputation` and `transfer_from` (cumulative
weighted ranges, ten draws on the villager's random, `Arrays.binarySearch`'s
pick, each entry once, less its transfer decay, merged by the greater
value). Targets keep insertion order and the most they ever held, so the
walk a transfer draws over is vanilla's `HashMap` order (bucket by the
spread `UUID.hashCode` under the table's capacity, then insertion); a
restored record is read back as vanilla reads its saved list. Within a
target, types go in declaration order (vanilla's differs between runs).
It is family data (`gossips`, `last_gossip_decay`), saved.

**Events:** a villager's hurt by a living entity (`Family::hurt`,
`setLastHurtByMob`: +25 minor negative, angry particles for a player); its
trade, told after its brain (+2 trading); a murder, told as the owner takes
the death to the villagers of its section the victim last saw (+25 major
negative for the killing blow's attacker); gossip shared in
`Villager.gossip` as two villagers meet; a day's decay in its tick. A
trading player's prices follow every change.

**Readers:** special prices subtract `floor(reputation × multiplier)`;
`DefendVillageTargetGoal` (iron golems): a survival or adventure player in
its box and sight whom a villager there holds at -100 or worse, kept as
vanilla keeps it.

Checks: stage `village` adds two scenes (two villagers sharing gossip at
the bell; a golem turning on a hated player) and prices for four
reputations and Hero with reputation: 20 scenes, 14779 ticks, now with
each villager's gossip, and 11 price cases equal vanilla's.
`test/merchant_connection_test.exs`: a player's punch adds minor negative
gossip and raises its prices. `gossip.rs` unit tests: caps, decay,
removal, a weighted transfer.

Not yet: murder witnesses in sibling sections (only the victim's section
hears); cures' gossip (VMC-044c3).
