# VMC-043e14 — Guardian retaliation on refused hits

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e3](VMC-043e3.md), [VMC-037n](VMC-037n.md).

A stationary guardian attempts its thorns retaliation and triggers its
stroll before its own damage is admitted. Reuse the existing refused-hit
reaction hook so a hurt-window refusal does not suppress that retaliation.
Classify direct attackers by the generated living-entity fact, including
living entities in the miscellaneous category.

Acceptance:

- Native guardian and elder-guardian attempts match for repeated hits,
  moving/stationary states, living/nonliving direct attackers and thorns
  avoidance. Health admission remains separate from retaliation.
- A live owner can refuse a second hit while its next tick still delivers
  the retaliation through the ordinary player damage path.
- Targeted tests and native checks pass; actual-client testing stays
  deferred at the user's request.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `Guardian.hurtServer`.
It checks movement, the damage's thorns-avoidance tag, thorns recursion and
whether the direct attacker is a `LivingEntity` before delegating to
`Monster.hurtServer`.

Implementation: `Guardian.repelled/2` reuses the ordinary guardian hurt
reaction before a refused damage result is returned. Retaliation remains
queued for the next owner tick through the existing mob-effect path.
`EntityTypes.living?/1` supplies the generated direct-attacker classification.

Validation: the native oracle's 36 cases cover guardian/elder, moving/still,
player/armor-stand/arrow direct attackers, normal/thorns/fire sources and two
immediate hit attempts. Before the fix, the second refused hit produced one
retaliation instead of two. All 36 now match. The nine combat and live socket
tests pass, including a real guardian owner that refuses health damage but
still hurts the player on its next tick. All 51 affected vanilla stages pass
(183 seconds, 57 oracle cache hits). Evidence:
`.local/m3-guardian-hurt-{before,check,tests,changed}.log`.

Actual-client scenario, deferred at the user's request: hit a stationary
guardian twice inside its hurt window; the second hit still retaliates.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
