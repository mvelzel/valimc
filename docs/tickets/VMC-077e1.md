# VMC-077e1 — Simulation reach as vanilla's

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3 (survival core)
Depends on: [VMC-077e](VMC-077e.md) (chunk tickets), [VMC-070](VMC-070.md)
(view distance), [VMC-042a](VMC-042a.md) (its client run found the gap).

Before: a player's simulation ticket covered the chunks within
`min(simulation_distance, client view distance)` (Chebyshev) of the player,
and its loading ticket only the client's view. The test client plays at
`renderDistance:4`, so ValiMC ticked 81 chunks around it where vanilla ticks
441: in M3 client run 2 monsters 5 to 10 chunks away stayed in peaceful until
the tester came near (VMC-042a).

Vanilla 26.3 (`DistanceManager`, `ChunkMap`; decompiled privately with
Vineflower 1.12.0, `research/decompiled/vmc-042a`):

- `ChunkMap.setServerViewDistance` clamps the server's view distance to 2..32
  and passes it to `DistanceManager.updatePlayerTickets`. Its
  `PlayerTicketTracker` gives every chunk within that distance (Chebyshev, the
  `FixedPlayerDistanceChunkTracker` level) of a player a `PLAYER_LOADING`
  ticket at `ENTITY_TICKING` level (31). The client's requested distance is
  used only by `getPlayerViewDistance` (clamped to 2..server), which picks the
  `ChunkTrackingView`: the chunks sent and the entities tracked.
- `addPlayer` puts one `PLAYER_SIMULATION` ticket on the player's chunk at
  level `31 - simulationDistance`. The simulation tracker spreads it one level
  a chunk, so chunks within the simulation distance are entity ticking (random
  ticks through `forEachBlockTickingChunk`, scheduled ticks through
  `isPositionTickingWithEntitiesLoaded`, entities, spawning) and one more ring
  is block ticking (block events only).
- A simulation ticket does not load: a chunk ticks only when the loading
  tracker made it a ticking chunk with its entities, so the effective reach is
  `min(simulationDistance, serverViewDistance)`, whatever the client asks.

Decision: the simulation ticket covers the chunks within
`min(simulation_distance, view_distance)` of the player, the server's settings
only; the loading ticket covers the view and those chunks, since ValiMC's
simulation ticket does not load either. ValiMC does not load vanilla's ring
between the simulation distance and the server's view distance for a player
whose client renders less: those chunks neither tick nor reach the client, and
at view-distance 32 with a client at 4 they would be 3,784 of 4,225 chunks.
The visible difference is limited to that ring's entities: they stay unloaded,
so selectors and the global mob cap do not count them. VMC-077e's flags
already leave out vanilla's loaded non-ticking border.

Cost, per player at the defaults (view and simulation distance 10), chunks
loaded / simulated:

| Client distance | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 117 / 81 | 441 / 441 |
| 10 | 473 / 421 | 493 / 441 |
| 32 at server 32 | 3,725 / 441 | 3,725 / 441 |

A client at 4 makes its owners generate, hold and tick about 3.8 times as many
chunks, the same as vanilla. Generation is nearest first (VMC-077u), so the
view's chunks still come first. Players whose clients render less now hold
chunks out to 10, so their groups merge as soon as those reach each other
(VMC-077j2), as full-view players' already did. Tests run at
view-distance 2, where the reach was already 2, so the suites do no extra work.
