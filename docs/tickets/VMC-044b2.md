# VMC-044b2 — The merchant menu and trading

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044b1](VMC-044b1.md).

Part of [VMC-044b](VMC-044b.md).

- **Opening:** `Villager.mobInteract` (a baby or a villager without offers
  is unhappy: its counter and the no sound; a sleeping or busy one passes)
  and `WanderingTrader.mobInteract` open the merchant menu: the screen,
  the offers packet with level, experience, progress bar and restock flag.
- **The menu:** `MerchantMenu` with `MerchantContainer`: the two payment
  slots and the result (`updateSellItem` with the selection hint, either
  payment order), `ServerboundSelectTradePacket` (`tryMoveItems`), the
  shift-click routes, taking the result (`MerchantResultSlot`: pick up,
  quick move repeated while it holds, drop) and closing (payments back to
  the inventory, or dropped by a dead or gone player). `stillValid`:
  within 4 blocks of a living trader that still trades with the player.
- **A trade:** `notifyTrade` (uses, the ambient sound delay),
  `rewardTradeXp` (the villager's experience, its draw of the orb, the
  next level's offers and Regeneration at a level-up), the trade sound,
  entity event 14 the next tick. A wandering trader's orb only when its
  offer rewards experience.
- **Brain:** `LookAndFollowTradingPlayerSink`, `ShowTradesToPlayer`, the
  wandering trader's trading goals; trading stops when offers are empty,
  the villager dies, teleports or sleeps.
- **Durability:** the player's inventory, the payments, the villager's
  offers, experience, level and the orb change in one group callback and
  one frame.

Acceptance:

- Stage `merchant_menu`: click, select and close sequences over a real
  `MerchantMenu` equal vanilla's slots, cursor, offers, experience and
  level; trade draws equal.
- A group crash after a trade restores both sides from the same frame.
- Client scenario (M4 run): trade emeralds for a librarian's book to its
  second level; the result slot fills and empties as payments change; a
  level-up shows new offers and particles.

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**The merchant's side** (`valimc_core::mobs::village::merchant`,
`Native.mob_section_merchant/3`, `RegionData`'s `{:merchant, …}` and
`Region.merchant/3`): what its owner reads of a villager or trader (alive,
baby, asleep, profession, level, experience, type, offers, trading
player, name) and the trade (`notifyTrade`: a use and the ambient timer;
`rewardTradeXp`: the orb's draw on its random, experience, a level-up with
Regeneration 200; a trader's orb only when the offer rewards experience),
`setUnhappy` and `notifyTradeUpdated`'s yes or no once in 20 ambient
ticks. Its trading and last-traded players are family data that a record
never restores. `mobInteract` runs in the owner before the other
interactions: spawn eggs, dead, busy and sleeping merchants (and baby
traders) pass; a baby villager is unhappy; offers are drawn when first
asked, and an exploration map's search runs as a job (`:trade_locate`,
one at a time, at most 64 waiting) whose answer draws again; without
offers the villager is unhappy (main hand) and the use is taken; else it
trades and the reply opens the menu. A level-up appends the next level's
offers (or queues them behind a search); the orb drops half a block up.

**The menu** (`Valimc.Player.Merchant`, `Valimc.Menus.Merchant`,
`Menu.merchant/0`): the payment slots are the player's `Inventory`'s
(saved with it; returned on close, dropped at death, disconnection or
back in play after a crash); the result comes from
`updateSellItem` with the hint; clicks on the payments and inventory go
through the click engine (shift routes of `quickMoveStack`, collects take
nothing); the result's gestures are `MerchantResultSlot`'s (pickup,
repeated shift-moves, ctrl-throws, a swap into an empty hotbar slot,
creative clone), each take paying the payments and asking the merchant
for its trade in the same group callback. `select_trade` is
`tryMoveItems`. The open screen is window type 19 with the merchant's
name; the offers packet (`ItemStack.encode_cost/1` for `ItemCost`) goes
after the contents and again at a level-up. The player's tick closes the
menu once the merchant is gone, dead, trades with another or is out of
reach (the entity range plus 4 from the eyes to its box); closing and
disconnecting end its trading.

**Brains:** a villager follows and looks at its trading player
(`LookAndFollowTradingPlayerSink`) and shows a player near it the results
of offers its main-hand item pays (`ShowTradesToPlayer`, holding them with
no drop chance); after its brain a trade shows (event 14) and it stops
trading without offers or once its player is gone (a guard: vanilla's
player closes the menu as it leaves); its ambient sound is the trade sound
while trading. A wandering trader stands still and looks at its trading
player (`TradeWithPlayerGoal`, whose stop ends the trade, and
`LookAtTradingPlayerGoal`) and its despawn delay waits while it trades.

Checks: stage `merchant_menu` (`MerchantMenuOracle`, a real `MerchantMenu`
over a scripted merchant): 7 scenarios, 63 steps (selections, every
result gesture, payments by hand, out-of-stock and demand-priced offers,
a full inventory, drags, collects, the hint) equal vanilla's slots,
cursor, uses and drops; the offers packet (with a water-bottle cost) and
the open-screen packet are vanilla's bytes. Stage `trades` and the
village stages pass. `test/merchant_connection_test.exs`: a librarian's
first use draws its offers and opens the menu; a selected trade's
payments, its pickup, uses, experience and orb; closing returns the
payments and ends its trading; a group crash after a durable trade
restores the villager with its use and experience and the player with
exactly one price paid; a wandering trader's three sets and its menu.

Not yet: reputation and gossip of a trade (VMC-044c), prices (VMC-044b3),
the trader spawning with trader llamas (VMC-043), statistics and
advancements (VMC-052), map ids (VMC-051). A merchant that walks into
another section while trading closes the menu (vanilla's would not).
