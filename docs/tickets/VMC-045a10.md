# VMC-045a10 — Preserve actor-owned dragon flight motion

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-045a5](VMC-045a5.md), [VMC-045a9](VMC-045a9.md).
Parent: [VMC-045a](VMC-045a.md).

After the despawn repair, the normal recovered dragon stays alive but turns in
a tiny horizontal circle and descends slowly instead of crossing arena nodes.
Its full live body has zero velocity, while its retained flight actor has
nonzero velocity. The ordinary mob ticker marks a dragon fixed to suppress
ordinary travel and pushing; its final result zeros fixed mobs. Shared owner
finalization incorrectly copies that result over the actor's own motion.

Acceptance:

- Keep flight motion under the dragon actor's authority at shared mob-tick
  finalization; ordinary mobs and their late pushes retain the kernel result.
- A normally admitted dragon makes multi-tick owner flight progress and keeps
  its actor velocity, including a section handoff and reload. Existing pinned
  actor and affected owner/combat checks pass.
- The dedicated tester observes ordinary arena traversal on a new frozen
  runtime, then completes the parent's batched fight. Never patch the active
  dragon's pose, health, AI or phase to manufacture progress.
