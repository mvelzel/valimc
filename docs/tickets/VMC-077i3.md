# VMC-077i3 — Players in owners

Status: In progress
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077i](VMC-077i.md), [VMC-077i1](VMC-077i1.md),
[VMC-077i2](VMC-077i2.md).

Before: the connection process holds the player's session (body, inventory,
living state, open menus, movement, mining, item use) next to its socket, and
every interaction with the world crosses to a group as a transfer.

Acceptance:

- The connection is the player's stable address: the session's timers,
  replies and deliveries name the connection, never the process the session
  runs in.
- The group that owns the player's section hosts the session. The connection
  keeps the socket, framing, encryption, compression, login and
  configuration, keepalive, the chunk view and streaming, and replication
  that reads publications; it forwards play input to the owner and writes
  what the owner sends back.
- Interactions are plain calls inside the owner; the transfer families, the
  player store's journal kinds and the connection's recovery lane, guards and
  queues are gone.
- A player's record rides in its group's frames; the player file is its
  checkpoint.
