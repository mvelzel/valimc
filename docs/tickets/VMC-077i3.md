# VMC-077i3 — Players in owners

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077i](VMC-077i.md), [VMC-077i1](VMC-077i1.md),
[VMC-077i2](VMC-077i2.md).

Before: the connection process holds the player's session (body, inventory,
living state, open menus, movement, mining, item use) next to its socket, and
every interaction with the world crosses to a group as a transfer.

Acceptance:

- The connection is the player's stable address: the session's timers,
  replies and deliveries name the connection, never the process the session
  runs in.
- The group that owns the player's section hosts the session. The connection
  keeps the socket, framing, encryption, compression, login and
  configuration, keepalive, the chunk view and streaming, and replication
  that reads publications; it forwards play input to the owner and writes
  what the owner sends back.
- Interactions are plain calls inside the owner; the transfer families, the
  player store's journal kinds and the connection's recovery lane, guards and
  queues are gone.
- A player's record rides in its group's frames; the player file is its
  checkpoint.

## Progress

- 077i3a (1f4b2fdc): the session's timers, replies and deliveries name the
  connection.
- 077i3b (99ce4ce4): `Valimc.Session.Host` runs the session inside the group
  that owns the player's section; `Valimc.Session.Link` is the connection's
  side (socket, view, streaming, replication). Sessions move with their
  section in a handoff and follow the player across owners.
- 077i3c: pickup, item drops, mining drops, placement, chest, furnace and
  table clicks, crafting, experience pickup, cooking awards and death drops
  are plain calls into the owner (`Region.call/3` runs them inline when the
  session is hosted there). Deleted: the `player_*_{intent,query,transfers}`
  modules, `Player.Transfers`, `TransferDelivery`, `Txn.Operation`,
  `Txn.Delivery`, the world's pickup, placement, mining, container,
  experience and item transfer, permit and request modules,
  `World.ItemRecords`, `World.MiningRecord`, the player store's prepare and
  outbox journal and its versions, the receipt tables for player kinds, the
  connection's recovery lane, `inventory_busy`/pending guards and the table
  and placement queues (150 files, +1,665 / −11,649 lines). The Mojang oracle
  no longer waits for a second completion after a drop.

- 077i3d: a player is a storage owner `{:player, uuid}` (`Player.Storage`):
  the hosting group cuts its changed records into the group's own commit
  record each 50 ms (the pose, motion and tick counters at most once a
  second), so a callback's changes to the player and the world are durable
  together. The file is the checkpoint, replaced after 1,200 frames, on
  `/save-all` and when the player leaves (the connection's call is answered
  once the file holds the last frame); a login waits until no group holds
  the player in `Valimc.PlayerRegistry` and folds the frames into the file;
  a stripe that finds frames no file holds folds them. Deleted: `PlayerStore`
  and its registry and supervisor, the save timer, every durable wait of the
  session (death stages, respawn, travel and item use now run at once), the
  damage deferral they needed and the log's player-file records and stripes.
  `Log.frames/3` returns only frames no checkpoint covers. A crash of a
  group disconnects its players (their clients hold newer state than the
  group's durable state); the next login has every durable frame.

`PlayerMotionQuery`, `PlayerPoseQuery` and `RainQuery` stay: they are
physics reads, not transfers. `Txn.Permits` and `Txn.Receipts` stay for box
edits and entity handoffs between groups. Known window: a commit held by a
moved section's frames still in flight elsewhere, at the moment of a section
handoff, can split a cross-section pickup's two halves between groups; a
crash in those milliseconds could keep one. An item plan still running on
the owner's write slot refuses an interaction or holds back its drops until
the slot frees.
