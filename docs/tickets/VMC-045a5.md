## Implementation and evidence (October 24, 2026)

The sequential mob ticker dispatches the dragon to its phase/flight actor,
without ordinary collision, gravity, pushes or goal AI. One body retains
its bounded history, phase/path state, ambient and rotation clocks, selected
crystal, flame UUID and death time. Hurt sounds reset the same ambient
clock. The owner's graph samples motion-blocking heights without leaves;
block reads are batched, and changes use the existing fenced block rules.

Native contact order uses the newly placed wings/body and the previous
head/neck boxes. Local mob contacts land mid-tick; sibling changes use the
shared entity change, and hosted players admit against their current hurt
state. Wings push even while their damage is suppressed by recent mob
hurt. Destructible blocks disappear without drops, transparent blocks are
ignored, and protected blocks set the next flight's wall slowdown.

Fireballs and radius-five perched clouds use the existing projectile and
cloud paths. The cloud UUID survives a section transfer and takeoff removes
it across the group, including a phase forced by damage. Crystal removal,
blast and head damage preserve their order; destroying the selected crystal
hurts the dragon and a holding dragon retaliates against an eligible player.
The death actor rises for 200 ticks, emits particles and its global event,
awards first/repeat kill XP in bursts and reports completion once.

A group tick stamp prevents a root, projectile or cloud moved into a later
sibling from ticking twice. New breath/fireballs start on the next tick;
body data includes the stamp so a record can restore it. The dragon's hurt
timers also run once, and its transient mind resumes after a same-tick
handoff without discarding the actor's random or clocks.

`DragonActorOracle` calls complete native ticks, including inherited living
and mob work: 46 sequences / 15,585 ticks across all eleven phases, NoAI,
crystals and their destruction, active players, breakable/protected walls,
and first/repeat death sequences. Position, velocity, parts, phase, health,
head/body rotation, ambient time, crystal, random, wall edits/events, contact
pushes and 5,728 hurt calls, sixteen aimed fireballs, 24 breath clouds,
particles and XP bursts match. Native packet fixtures also cover both death
particles. The focused owner, socket, entity, projectile, combat, explosion,
item, animal and player suites pass 90 tests. Four actor owner scenarios
cover one-tick cross-section movement and reload, sibling breath creation
and takeoff cleanup, selected-crystal damage across End sections and the
repeat death's 500 XP without duplication.

All fifty affected vanilla stages passed. A separate regeneration run
verified the combat, entity and dragon-flight fact sources, all three
passing; the final projectile/socket/actor follow-up passed 21 tests.

The actor is connected to live entity ticking. This checkpoint remains a
foundation until arena admission makes the fight reachable in normal play;
durable fight lifecycle still precedes the graphical finale gate.
