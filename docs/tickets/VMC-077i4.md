# VMC-077i4 — Players in the owner trackers, one tab list, one way into a level

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077i](VMC-077i.md), [VMC-077i3](VMC-077i3.md),
[VMC-077h](VMC-077h.md) (entity trackers and publication).

Before: every connection replicated every nearby player on its own path: each
tick it read each peer's 22-word view from an ETS table, diffed it and encoded
the moves, metadata and equipment itself (O(N²) encodes), with a constant
ground flag and no head rotation (player-connection E5). A player left the tab
list when it left view (E4). Logging in, respawning and changing dimension
were three copies of entering a level (B9).

Acceptance:

- The group hosting a player keeps its tracker (`Valimc.World.PlayerTracker`,
  vanilla's `ServerEntity` for a player): every second tick, or when its data
  changed, it encodes once for every observer a relative move (`Pos`,
  `PosRot` or `Rot`) with the player's rotation and ground flag, or an
  absolute sync when the ground flag changed, the delta is too long or 400
  updates passed; `rotate_head` when the head turned; the entity data and
  equipment that changed. The section the player stands in publishes the
  player's row and these packets with its items and orbs.
- Connections spawn, move and remove players from those rows and frames like
  any entity (`EntityReplication`): themselves excluded, spectators shown
  only to spectators, a player in the credits or past its death animation
  not at all. The per-connection peer path (`PeerViews`, peer diffs, the
  peers map) is gone.
- The tab list holds every online player from login to logout, near or far;
  game-mode changes reach every tab list.
- One way into a level (`Valimc.Player.Level`): the world views every entry
  starts from, and one respawn path for a death's respawn and a dimension
  change in vanilla's order (respawn, difficulty, permission level, teleport,
  abilities, the level's info, the player's own state), keeping or resending
  entity data and attributes per the respawn's flags. Login shares the views
  and the effects.
