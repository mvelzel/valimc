# VMC-032c5 — Occupied furnaces and named containers

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md). Finishes [VMC-034f](VMC-034f.md).

Removing a furnace, smoker or blast furnace that holds items spills its
three slots and pays out the experience of its stored recipes as orbs
(`AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity.preRemoveSideEffects`), in survival and
creative, in the same commit as the block change. A container placed from
a named item keeps the name (`CustomName`), shows it as its menu title and
drops it again with the block.

Acceptance: the cooking loot and experience oracles with occupied
furnaces; mining and creative removal spill once; a named chest, barrel,
furnace and hopper keep their names through placement, a restart and
breaking. Client scenario (M4 batch): break a furnace mid-smelt and
collect its items and experience; rename a chest on an anvil, place it and
read its title.

## Result (October 31, 2026)

Occupied furnaces. `ContainerLifecycle` retires a furnace, smoker or blast
furnace entry like any other when its block becomes another kind, however
full it is; `ContainerSpills.add/4` scatters its three slots as a chest's
(`Containers.dropContents`) and pays out its stored recipes as orbs at the
block's centre (`ContainerSpills.cooking_orbs/3`: `CookingAwards.plan` with
a random draw per fractional recipe, then `ExperienceOrbs.award`, each
recipe on its own as `getRecipesToAwardAndPopExperience` does). Survival
mining (`mine_block`) and creative removal (`destroy_block`) both go
through it, as `preRemoveSideEffects` runs for both, and the items, orbs,
block and container change land in the same joint commit, so a crash keeps
all or none. Raw edits still cannot remove an occupied furnace: the
protection now admits one only with the exact retired entry. A same-kind
change (lit) keeps the entry; cooking's lit commits never retire viewers.

Names. An entry may carry `name`, a text component tag (vanilla's
`CustomName`), on any owned kind. `Containers.put_name/4` sets it; the
snapshot writes a named entry under tag 4 (the name's network NBT, then the
entry's own tagged bytes), so unnamed entries and older snapshots read as
before. A held chest, barrel, shulker box, furnace-family block, hopper,
dropper or dispenser whose only component is `minecraft:custom_name` (a
shulker box's contents besides) now places (`Placement.placeable_stack?`),
and the player's placement passes the name to the owner with a shulker
box's slots (`fill`), which names the new entry in the commit that creates
it. Menus take their title from the view's `name`, else the default
translation (`Menus.Packets.open_chest/3`, `open_storage/3`,
`open_machine/3`, `open_cooking/3`); a joined chest is titled by its first
half's name, else its second's (`ChestBlock`'s double-chest display name).
Breaking a named container names its own item among the player's block
loot (`ContainerSpills.loot/2`) where its loot table copies
`minecraft:custom_name` from the block entity (`Loot.names?/1`, read from
the generated tables: the crafter's table, for one, does not); a shulker
box drops named with its contents in survival and creative. Anvil import
owns a container with a `CustomName` it can hold (a name with a click
event stays preserved) and export writes it back; generated template
containers with a name stay preserved. Ender chests take no name: a named
one still does not place.

Oracles: `CookingExperienceOracle` removes 8 occupied furnaces, smokers and
blast furnaces through the real `preRemoveSideEffects` with recipes loaded
and every fraction draw fixed; the check spills the same entries and
compares items per kind, orb values and positions (the centre) and draw
counts. `LootOracle blocks` names half of the container block entities
(508 cases) and `check_block_loot` names our drops the same way.
`ChestMenuOracle` records 9 named containers' open bodies (chest, barrel,
shulker box, the furnace family, hopper, dropper, dispenser).
`AnvilContentOracle` records each container's `CustomName`, and the check
requires the owned entry to hold it (the named chest and furnace fixtures
are owned now; one locked loot chest remains).

Tests: `named_container_region_test.exs` (an occupied furnace mined in
survival and removed in creative spills its 17 items and pays out its
experience once, at the centre, and survives a restart; a named chest,
barrel, furnace, hopper, dropper and shulker box keep the name through
placement, the menu view and title, a restart and breaking; joined chest
titles and the snapshot round trip), `cooking_lifecycle_test.exs` (occupied
entries retire and spill with orbs; lit changes keep them), a named
shulker box with contents in `shulker_box_region_test.exs` and named
containers in `anvil_block_entity_test.exs`. The affected cooking, chest,
barrel, shulker, ender chest, container, lifecycle, placement, mining,
hopper, loot, menu, merchant and Anvil suites pass (104 tests, socket ones
included). Fifteen vanilla stages pass: chest_menu, block_loot,
cooking_experience, anvil_content, container_drop, loot (its regeneration
step not re-run), placement, block_entities, cooking_menu,
cooking_placement, table_menu, merchant_menu, chest_click, chest_view and
chest_access.

Not done: renaming an item on an anvil is VMC-038b's, so the client
scenario names its chest with a creative or `/give` stack instead;
pick-block of a named container does not copy the name; explosions still
leave block-entity cells standing, so they remove no furnace. The client
scenario is pending.
