# VMC-032c5 — Occupied furnaces and named containers

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md). Finishes [VMC-034f](VMC-034f.md).

Removing a furnace, smoker or blast furnace that holds items spills its
three slots and pays out the experience of its stored recipes as orbs
(`AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity.preRemoveSideEffects`), in survival and
creative, in the same commit as the block change. A container placed from
a named item keeps the name (`CustomName`), shows it as its menu title and
drops it again with the block.

Acceptance: the cooking loot and experience oracles with occupied
furnaces; mining and creative removal spill once; a named chest, barrel,
furnace and hopper keep their names through placement, a restart and
breaking. Client scenario (M4 batch): break a furnace mid-smelt and
collect its items and experience; rename a chest on an anvil, place it and
read its title.
