# VMC-010 — Configuration, administration and permissions

Status: In progress
Milestone: M2 real worlds (administration); the console and RCON span M3
Depends on: [VMC-053](VMC-053.md) (command tree and sources).

A vanilla server keeps its operators, whitelist and bans in list files next to
the world, checks them at every login, and lets operators change them with
commands. Before this ticket ValiMC's operators were a list of names from
`VALIMC_OPERATORS`, there were no bans or whitelist, and a full server refused
everyone with a plain-text message.

## VMC-010b, c, f — Save control, idle timeout and the console

- ValiMC saves through region, player and clock logs with group commit as
  things happen. Vanilla's autosave corresponds to each region owner's
  30-second save of changed cells and scheduled ticks; `/save-off` stops that
  (`Valimc.World.Saving`, back on at startup like vanilla's `noSave`) while
  players, the world clock and journaled transfers keep saving, as do unloads
  and shutdown. `/save-all` checkpoints every online player, has every region
  owner save now and writes the clock; `flush` waits until each region's writes
  are durable. Messages and failures are vanilla's.
- `/setidletimeout <minutes>` sets vanilla's `player-idle-timeout`
  (`VALIMC_PLAYER_IDLE_TIMEOUT`, saved in `admin-settings.json`). The packets
  after which vanilla resets a player's idle time do so in ValiMC: actions,
  item use, held-slot changes, chat and commands, swings, attacks and
  interactions, container clicks, recipes and signs, input once the client
  has loaded, and movement that moves the player. A player idle longer than the
  timeout is disconnected with "You have been idle for too long!". The check
  runs with each keepalive (every 10 seconds), not every tick, and the
  handlers' own conditions (an item use out of range, say) are not repeated.
- The console (`Valimc.Console`) reads standard input when the server runs in
  a terminal and runs each line as the Server source: permission level 4, the
  world spawn, no player, a leading `/` optional. Feedback prints as vanilla's
  English text (`Valimc.Command.Render` over `priv/lang/en_us.json`, the
  `language` stage), and operators see its broadcasts as `[Server: …]`.
  Commands that need a player answer "A player is required to run this command
  here". Players' admin broadcasts and `/say`, `/me` and `/msg` are logged as
  vanilla logs them. `VALIMC_CONSOLE=false` turns the console off.

- Joining and leaving (`Valimc.Session.Presence`) log vanilla's lines
  (`Name[/address:port] logged in with entity id …`, `Name lost connection:
  reason`) and send every player the yellow "joined the game" and "left the
  game" messages.

Client run, October 21, 2026, release `a54bca5`, fresh vanilla world: 4 of 4 passed.
`/save-off`, `/save-on`, `/save-all` and `flush` gave vanilla's lines.
`say hello from console` showed "[Server] hello from console". The console's
`save-off` reached the operator as the gray "[Server: Automatic saving is now
disabled]", and `gamemode creative` logged "A player is required to run this
command here". `gamemode survival ValiMCTest` switched the player, `nonsense`
logged both error lines, and `/time query time` logged the clock. With
`/setidletimeout 1` the untouched client was disconnected after 67.8 s with
"You have been idle for too long!". The run found the missing join and leave
lines, added since.

Checks: `test/console_test.exs` and `test/server_connection_test.exs`; the
`commands` stage parses the new commands as vanilla does, and the `language`
stage regenerates the English strings.

## VMC-010e, g — Session replacement and RCON

- A login of a profile that is already online kicks that session with "You
  logged in from another location" and continues once it has left (at most 10
  seconds), as vanilla does. Another profile with the same name is still
  refused.
- RCON (`Valimc.Rcon`) speaks vanilla's protocol on `VALIMC_RCON_PORT`
  (25575) when `VALIMC_RCON=true` and `VALIMC_RCON_PASSWORD` is set; without a
  password it logs vanilla's warning and stays off. A login answers with the
  request id, or -1 for a wrong password; a command runs as the Rcon source at
  level 4 and answers its messages' text without separators, in pieces of
  4,096 characters; other types answer "Unknown request". Packets outside 10
  to 1,460 bytes close the connection, and at most 16 clients connect at
  once. Operators see its broadcasts as `[Rcon: …]` unless
  `VALIMC_BROADCAST_RCON_TO_OPS=false`, and the log records them.

Checks: `test/rcon_test.exs` (login, commands, output, unknown types,
oversized packets over a socket) and the replacement test in
`test/admin_connection_test.exs`.

## VMC-010a — Lists, login checks and the admin commands

`Valimc.Admin.Lists` owns the lists and writes `ops.json`, `whitelist.json`,
`banned-players.json`, `banned-ips.json` and `usercache.json` in the world
directory in vanilla's format (`Valimc.Admin.ListFile`): Gson pretty printing,
dashed UUIDs and `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss Z` dates. Vanilla's own files are read as
they are. Logins and permission checks read published ETS rows; only commands
call the owner. The whitelist switch that `/whitelist on|off` changes is saved
in `admin-settings.json`.

- Login (`Valimc.Admin.Login`), in vanilla's order: a banned profile gets
  "You are banned from this server. Reason: …" (with the expiry date for a
  temporary ban), a player who is neither operator nor whitelisted gets "You are
  not white-listed on this server!" while the whitelist is on, a banned IPv4
  address gets the IP ban message, and a full server admits only operators with
  `bypassesPlayerLimit`. Expired bans are ignored.
- Every login adds its profile to the name cache. A typed name resolves through
  the cache, then (offline mode) to the offline profile of the lower-cased
  name, as vanilla does.
- A player's permission level is its `ops.json` level. `/op` and `/deop` send
  the permission entity event and a new command tree to an online player.
  `VALIMC_OPERATORS` names are added to `ops.json` at startup; `*` still makes
  every player an operator on a local test server.
- `/ban` and `/ban-ip` disconnect the affected players, `/whitelist` with
  `enforce-whitelist` disconnects unlisted ones, and the suggestion providers
  offer the same names vanilla's do.
- Transfer handshakes are refused with "This server does not accept transfers"
  unless `VALIMC_ACCEPTS_TRANSFERS=true`.

Differences:

- `white-list` defaults to false; vanilla 26.3's `server.properties` defaults
  it to true. `VALIMC_WHITE_LIST` and `VALIMC_ENFORCE_WHITELIST` set the switches.
- Profiles are not looked up at Mojang. In online mode only players who have
  joined resolve by name.
- List files keep ValiMC's entry order; vanilla writes hash order.
- A temporary ban's expiry date names the zone as its GMT offset.

Acceptance:

- The commands parse as vanilla's (oracle stage `commands`), and their messages
  and results follow vanilla's `OpCommand`, `DeOpCommands`, `BanPlayerCommands`,
  `BanIpCommands`, `BanListCommands`, `PardonCommand`, `PardonIpCommand`,
  `WhitelistCommand` and `TransferCommand`.
- `test/admin_connection_test.exs` covers bans and pardons with a refused login,
  IP bans, the ban list and transfer, op and deop permission events, and a
  whitelist refusal and admission over sockets. `test/admin_list_file_test.exs`
  covers the file format.
- A client run confirms the login messages and the permission changes.

Checks: see the VMC-053 checks; the `commands` stage covers the 053f2 parse
cases.

Client run, October 21, 2026, release `cddb0ce`, one fresh flat dataset across four
starts: 10 of 10 passed.

- `ops.json` listed ValiMCTest at level 4 from `VALIMC_OPERATORS`, with the
  offline UUID, and `usercache.json` held the login.
- `ban-ip`, `banlist ips` and `pardon-ip` gave vanilla's lines, and the
  `pardon-ip` suggestion offered the banned IP. `ban Griefer bad build` banned
  `griefer`, and the files matched vanilla's fields and date format.
- `whitelist add`, `list` and `on` saved `whitelist.json` and
  `admin-settings.json`. `/transfer localhost 25565` ended on "This server
  does not accept transfers".
- `deop` left exactly the eight level-0 commands, and `/gamemode` became
  unknown. A restart without operators let the whitelisted player in.
- `ban ValiMCTest testing` disconnected with "You are banned from this server",
  and the next login showed "You are banned from this server. Reason:
  testing". After the ban was removed from the file, an operator joined past the
