# VMC-044a3 — Cats

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-044a1](VMC-044a1.md), [VMC-043c](VMC-043c.md).

Part of [VMC-044a](VMC-044a.md).

- **Cats** (strays): their goals (tempted by fish, avoiding players,
  hunting rabbits, strolling), crouching and sprinting, their variants (a
  swamp hut's black, some black at full moon) and sound variants.
- **The cat spawner:** cats come to villages with more than four occupied
  homes and to swamp huts.

Acceptance:

- The oracle's cat scenes (wandering, avoiding a player, tempted, hunting
  a rabbit) match tick by tick, and so do cats' spawns, breedings and drops.
- In a region owner a village's homes bring a cat.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

**Cat** (`Valimc.Entity.Animals.Cat`, an animal over
`Valimc.Entity.Animal`): vanilla's goals and targets at their priorities —
`TamableAnimalPanicGoal`, `CatTemptGoal` (cod and salmon at 0.6, now and
then not scared of the player it picked), `CatAvoidEntityGoal` (survival
players, 16 blocks, walking 0.8 and sprinting 1.33, added last as
`reassessTameGoals` adds it), `LeapAtTargetGoal` (the spiders') and
`OcelotAttackGoal` (new, `Valimc.AI.Goals`), `BreedGoal`, the stroll and look, and
`NonTameRandomTargetGoal` for rabbits (and turtles, which are VMC-043e's).
The tamed cat's goals (sitting when ordered, relaxing on its owner, lying
and sitting on blocks, following its owner) are registered and never start
for a stray. `customServerAiStep` crouches it at 0.6 and sprints it at
1.33 (the sprint speed modifier, the shared flag); sprinting draws for its
particles (`Valimc.AI.Self.sprint_particle/1`, shared with the golem). A
stray begs for food every 100 ticks while tempted. `finalizeSpawn` runs
`Mob`'s part, then picks its variant (`selectVariantToSpawn`: spawn
conditions now include a structure, read from the kept starts the AI world
holds, and the moon's brightness) and a sound variant; its sounds are its
sound variant's (a kitten's own). Entity data: tamable flags and owner,
variant, lying, relaxing, collar and sound variant.

**Cat spawner** (`Valimc.World.CatSpawner`, after a group's natural
spawns, overworld, while mobs spawn): every 1200 ticks a random player of
the group and a spot 8 to 31 blocks away on each axis, from the group's
level random; where the chunks around are loaded and a cat may stand
(`isSpawnPositionOk`, the spawn kernel's own, `Native.spawn_position_ok`),
it spawns a cat near a village (two sections) with more than four occupied
homes within 48 blocks and fewer than five cats around, or a persistent
one in a swamp hut with no cat near. Each group counts down on its own,
where vanilla's countdown is the level's.

Checks: stage `animals` (`AnimalOracle`): 10206 animal ticks of 20 scenes
equal vanilla's, among them a stray wandering past a creative player,
creeping and then sprinting away from a survival player, creeping toward
a player holding cod (picking the player and forgetting it), and leaping
at and killing a still rabbit (the check lands mob hits after the tick and
stops at the kill); 448 spawns (cats' variants at full moon and sound
variants among them), 280 breedings, 600 drops. Stages `animal_facts`
(cat dimensions, food, variants with structure and moon conditions, sound
variants) and `ai_facts` (its goals). `test/natural_spawning_test.exs`: a
group's cat spawner brings a cat near five claimed homes, not four.

Not modelled yet: taming (feeding a stray takes the fish and does nothing
yet), a tamed cat's goals, collars, its morning gift and purring, lying
with a sleeping player; these are VMC-043's taming. Cats scaring creepers
and phantoms are those mobs' tickets.

Client scenario (phase-end handoff): survival, a fresh world's village by
day. Cats are about the village (or `/summon cat`); walking up to one makes
it creep away and then dash off; holding cod and standing still, it creeps
up and follows; `/summon rabbit` near a cat: it pounces on it.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
