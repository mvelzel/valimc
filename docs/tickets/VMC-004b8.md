# VMC-004b8 — A destroyed item drops its contents

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-004b](VMC-004b.md). Depends on [004b6](VMC-004b6.md).

Vanilla's `ItemEntity.hurtServer` calls `Item.onDestroyed` when the entity's
health runs out (fire, lava, cactus, explosions, lightning); a shulker box
and a bundle then spill their contents as item entities where it was
(`ItemUtils.onContainerDestroyed`). An item falling out of the world is
discarded without spilling.

Acceptance: an item entity holding a shulker box with contents, or a filled
bundle, that burns in lava leaves its contents as items at its position in
the same owner frame. Nothing is duplicated or lost through a crash: the
spill and the removal commit together. Void removal spills nothing.

Client scenario (M4 batch): throw a filled shulker box into lava; its
contents float out, and a bundle does the same.

## Result (October 31, 2026)

`Entity.ItemSpill` gives what a destroyed stack releases: a block item's
`container` slots (filled ones, in order; `BlockItem.onDestroyed`) and a
bundle's contents (`BundleItem.onDestroyed`). Each becomes a new item where
the destroyed one was, moving as `new ItemEntity(level, x, y, z, stack)` sets
out (a random horizontal push under 0.1, 0.2 up).

Both ways an item dies call it inside the change that removes the item, so
the store change, and with it the owner's frame, holds both or neither: an
item burned by fire, lava or a cactus (`RegionData`'s burn-up, at the step's
position when it is still in this section, otherwise where the item was) and
one an explosion destroys (`Explosion.apply_hits`). A spill that cannot be
admitted keeps the destroyed item for the next tick instead of losing its
contents. The void discard path is untouched and spills nothing.

The rule itself is two short methods with no randomness to compare beyond
the launch, so there is no oracle stage; ExUnit covers it: a burning shulker
box and bundle in a region owner leave exactly their contents where they
were, a plain stack leaves nothing, a stick carrying a container component
leaves nothing, and an explosion's destroy spills a box in the same store
change.

Client scenario (M4 batch): throw a shulker box holding a few stacks and a
filled bundle into lava; both burn and their contents float out of the lava
(and burn too unless fire-immune). A creeper blast does the same.
