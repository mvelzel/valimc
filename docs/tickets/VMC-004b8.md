# VMC-004b8 — A destroyed item drops its contents

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-004b](VMC-004b.md). Depends on [004b6](VMC-004b6.md).

Vanilla's `ItemEntity.hurtServer` calls `Item.onDestroyed` when the entity's
health runs out (fire, lava, cactus, explosions, lightning); a shulker box
and a bundle then spill their contents as item entities where it was
(`ItemUtils.onContainerDestroyed`). An item falling out of the world is
discarded without spilling.

Acceptance: an item entity holding a shulker box with contents, or a filled
bundle, that burns in lava leaves its contents as items at its position in
the same owner frame. Nothing is duplicated or lost through a crash: the
spill and the removal commit together. Void removal spills nothing. The
oracle compares the spilled stacks with vanilla's.

Client scenario (M4 batch): throw a filled shulker box into lava; its
contents float out, and a bundle does the same.
