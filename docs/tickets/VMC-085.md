# VMC-085 — M3 leftovers

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Carried from: [VMC-083](VMC-083.md), [VMC-043e26](VMC-043e26.md).

M3 closed on its gate and the VMC-083 client run. These loose ends were
left open and are small enough to clear before M4's systems start:

1. **A playing-dead axolotl heals twice as much as vanilla allows.** In the
   client run it went from 4 to 11 health. Vanilla's `PlayDead` behaviour
   gives Regeneration I for 200 ticks, about 4 health. The likely cause is
   that the behaviour starts a second time after its 200 ticks, while its
   countdown and the hurt memory still hold. Reproduce the hurt with a real
   attacker entity so `HurtByEntity` is set, and compare the brain's tick
   order with vanilla's (`ValidatePlayDead` in the core activity, then the
   timed behaviour's stop).
2. **Dolphin treasure guidance** ([VMC-043e26](VMC-043e26.md)) has not been
   seen on the client. A fed dolphin in open ocean should lead the player
   toward a nearby shipwreck or ruin.
3. **A stray cat taking cod** (fixed in 6d75f7d7) needs one client recheck.
4. **Commands:**
   - `/gamerule mob_griefing`, which needs a stored rule; it is VMC-006's
     first rule beyond the three the clock stores.
   - `/effect … saturation`.
   - `/item replace` for mob equipment and player slots.

Acceptance: the axolotl heals as vanilla does, checked with a region test
that uses a real attacker; the two client observations pass in M4's first
client run; the commands run with vanilla feedback.
