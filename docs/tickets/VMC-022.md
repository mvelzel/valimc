# VMC-022 — Heightmaps and light propagation

Status: Done
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-021](VMC-021.md) (imported worlds, stored light), VMC-065 (published
snapshots for reads across owners).

ValiMC computes no light. Generated chunks are sent full-bright; imported chunks
send the light vanilla stored, which goes stale when blocks change (a torch
placed in a cave lights it only for players who saw it placed).

Scope: compute vanilla's sky and block light for every chunk and keep it current
when blocks change, across chunk and region borders, and send it to clients.

Subtickets:

| Ticket | Slice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| VMC-022a | Generated light facts: emission, dampening, face occlusion per state | Done |
| VMC-022b | Rust light engine: batch sky and block light, golden parity with vanilla | Done |
| VMC-022c | Incremental relighting on edits, region borders, light packets; client run | Done |

Acceptance (parent):

- Light facts come from the pinned jar: `getLightEmission`, `getLightDampening`,
  and `LightEngine.getOcclusionShape` per face, with `Shapes.faceShapeOccludes`
  between touching faces, verified exhaustively per equivalence class.
- Rules, from `javap` of the pinned `LightEngine`, `BlockLightEngine` and
  `SkyLightEngine`:
  - Light entering a block loses `max(1, dampening)`.
  - It does not cross a face where the two touching face shapes occlude.
  - Block light starts from emission. Sky light is 15 at and above each
    column's lowest sky source (`ChunkSkyLightSources`, the existing
    `sky_blocks` facts).
  - The result is the unique fixpoint, however it is computed.
- Golden parity: for every chunk of a vanilla world whose eight neighbours are
  full, ValiMC's computed sky and block light equal what vanilla's own light
  engine computes from the same blocks.
- Edits relight incrementally, as vanilla's increase and decrease queues do, also
  across region borders. Clients receive `light_update` for changed sections, and
  chunk packets carry computed light.
- Benchmarked: full relight per chunk and incremental updates per edit.

## VMC-022a/b — facts and batch engine (October 20, 2026)

- `LightFactsOracle` writes `priv/physics/light_blocks.json` (stage
  `light_facts`). For each of 35,723 states it records emission and dampening and
  a face-occlusion class per direction (26–28 classes per direction). Per
  direction there is an occlusion table between a face and the opposite face,
  from `Shapes.faceShapeOccludes` over `LightEngine.getOcclusionShape`.
  11,359,914 checks confirm every state against every class representative.
- `valimc_core::light`: the dampening loss, face occlusion and column sky
  sources follow the existing `sky_blocks` facts. The fixpoint is computed with
  one bucket queue per level, highest first. Sky sources are enqueued only where
  a neighbouring cell is darker: the lowest source, and sources beside lower
  columns. `Valimc.World.Light` installs the facts at start. `light_columns`
  (dirty CPU) lights a rectangle of column images and returns vanilla nibble
  arrays.
- Stage `light`:
  - `ChunkOracle` generates the world, and `LightOracle` feeds its 400 full
    chunks, as ProtoChunks, to vanilla's own `LevelLightEngine` (status updates,
    `setLightEnabled`, `propagateLightSources`, updates run to completion).
  - ValiMC equals vanilla in every one of 63,700,992 sky and block values of the
    324 chunks with full neighbours.
- The light vanilla stores in region files is not a reliable reference, which
  is why the oracle runs the engine:
  - A uniform zero layer is omitted on save (`DataLayer.isEmpty`), just like a
    section with no layer, so absent arrays are ambiguous.
  - Stored light can also be stale: one oak tree's leaves were stored at 15
    beneath them.
- Speed: 400 chunks light in a median 292 ms on one core (about 0.73 ms per
  chunk, including image decoding and nibble packing).
- Tests: `cargo test` (falloff and stops at opaque blocks, sky under overhangs,
  vanilla nibble layout), stages `light_facts` and `light`.

## VMC-022c — live light (October 20, 2026)

- `valimc_core::light_grid::LightGrid`: sky and block light stored per section
  as one uniform level or a vanilla nibble array.
  - `update` follows vanilla's decrease and increase queues. Light that may have
    depended on changed cells, or on sky sources a column lost, is cleared while
    dimmer than what removed it. Brighter cells met on the way, and every source
    in the cleared region, spread again. A column whose lowest sky source moves
    gains or loses sources.
  - A `cargo test` property applies random edits (opaque, emitting, dampening and
    face-occluding states, anywhere in the column) and checks every cell
    against a full recompute after each round. It passed 60 rounds as committed,
    and 400 rounds of up to 9 edits once.
  - Full computations copy the area into a dense array first, filling uniform
    sections at once; a flat region lights in about 19 ms.
- The region's `ColumnStore` holds the grid over its chunks plus a one-chunk
  ring of neighbour chunks (`halo`; flat terrain until a neighbour reports).
  Light travels at most 15 blocks, so the region's own cells are exact.
  - The region is lit on the first light request (a chunk packet). Until then
    edits need no relighting, since the first computation sees them.
  - Every block commit relights incrementally, cooking included (lit furnaces
    emit light). After durability the owner publishes `light_update` packets
    (two VarInts and the light data, checked with `javap`) for its chunks whose
    light changed, after the block updates.
- Chunk packets carry computed light. Flat chunks keep their cached body
  (heightmaps, sections) with the chunk's own light appended. All-zero layers go
  in the empty masks, as vanilla sends them.
- Across regions (`Valimc.World.LightHalo`), plain messages only:
  - After loading, an owner sends each loaded neighbour the column images of its
    chunks in that neighbour's ring, and the neighbour answers with its own.
  - Durable changes in ring chunks are forwarded as edits.
  - Ring images are applied together and relit once: incrementally, or from
    scratch when more than 16,384 cells changed.
- Measured (one region, flat terrain): placing a light source relights in a
  median 311 µs and removing it in 99 µs; mining grass takes 4 µs.
- Tests: `test/region_light_test.exs` covers flat terrain light, a lamp across a
  chunk border with `light_update` frames, a shaft opening to the sky, removal,
  recomputation after a restart, and light across a region border both ways
  (forwarded edits, and a restarted owner's hello).
- Suites: the full `mix test` (1,649 at `aa4091a`) and all 187 oracle stages
  pass, TCP included.
  - `anvil_content` now checks that chunk packets cover every light section (as
    data or empty). The light values are the `light` stage's job.
  - `block_batch_test` counts light frames apart from block frames.
  - `player_motion_connection_test` now stands its sweet berry bush on dirt:
    since VMC-029c an unsupported bush breaks, as in vanilla.

Client run 1 (October 20, 2026; frozen `aa4091a`; `.local/client-tests/VMC-022/`):

- PASS: join (15 sky on open ground); a sealed shaft is 0 sky, also for a
  freshly joined client; sky light shades under acacia canopy (14, 13, 10 down
  the shaft), under a dirt overhang (10) and in a covered cave (8); live block
  light from torches, including 12 across the x = 0 region border; logs clean.
- FAIL, both on rejoin: a torch's block light reached the client live (it lights
  locally), but a fresh client saw 0. There were two causes:
  - Creative placement of items without placement facts (torches) used the raw
    single-block `set_block` path, which never relit, sent no light and
    forwarded nothing to neighbours.
  - Owners cache each chunk's packet by block version. A chunk whose light
    changed only because of a neighbour's edit (the other side of a border, or
    the next chunk) kept serving its old packet.
- Fixed: the raw path relights, publishes light frames and forwards ring edits.
  Every chunk whose light changes (from commits, the raw path or neighbours'
  ring updates) drops its cached packet and moves its version, so a running
  encode is discarded as stale. Commits invalidate each chunk once.
  `region_light_test` now checks fresh chunk packets (`World.chunk`) after edits
  in and beside a chunk, a raw torch, and across a region border. Both new
  checks fail without the fix.
