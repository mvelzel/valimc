# VMC-032c1 — Barrels

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md).

A barrel is a 27-slot container that faces where it was placed from and
shows `open` while anyone views it. It works as a chest does, without the
lid, the double form or the obstruction check.

- Placement faces away from the player's look (`BarrelBlock`'s nearest
  looking direction, opposite), all six directions.
- The section owns a 27-slot entry; the `generic_9x3` menu titled
  `container.barrel`, the shared click engine, shift-click and collect.
- The first viewer sets `open=true` and plays `block.barrel.open`; the
  last one leaving sets it back and plays `block.barrel.close`, at the
  barrel's centre, as `ContainerOpenersCounter` counts them.
- Hoppers, droppers and comparators treat it as a 27-slot container;
  breaking it spills its contents (`Containers.dropContents`); a
  generated barrel's loot table unrolls on first open, as a loot chest's.
- The storage entry is a tagged kind beside the machines (one container
  format version), so shulker boxes reuse it.

Acceptance: the oracle's placement, opener-count, sound and menu traces;
contents through a restart and a group handoff; no item lost or duplicated
through a crash mid-click (the shared container commit). Client scenario
(M4 batch): place barrels in all six facings, fill and empty one,
watch it open and close, feed it with a hopper, break it.

## Result (October 31, 2026)

`Blocks.Barrel` is the `BarrelBlock` behaviour; its block-entity kind
`:barrel` makes barrel states owned (`BlockEntityTypes.supported?`), so
placement, chunk carriers and the container lifecycle take them without
special cases. Placement already follows the generated placement facts,
which face the barrel away from the player's look in all six directions.

`Blocks.Storage` names the storage kinds and their sizes (barrel 27). A
section's storage entry is `%{kind, slots}`, encoded in container format
version 3 under tag 3 with a kind id; older tags still decode.
`Containers.create_owned/4` builds the empty entry of any owned kind, and
the lifecycle (place, ensure, retire), loot unpacking and Anvil import use
it instead of per-kind branches. Hoppers, droppers and comparators match an
entry to its block by block-entity kind, so they treat a barrel as a 27-slot
container; breaking one spills through the existing joint commit.

Menus: `MenuAccess` adds the `:storage` menu (no obstruction check, unlike a
chest); its view carries the entry's kind. `Menu.storage(:barrel)` is the
three-row chest menu (`ChestMenu.threeRows`), so clicks, shift-click,
collect and drags run through the shared engine and the region's existing
container commit. `Menus.Packets.open_storage/2` takes the window type from
that menu and the title from the kind (`container.barrel`). Chests and
storage menus count openers (`MenuAccess.openers/0`); opening a barrel
angers piglins as a chest does.

Open state: the viewer index's first-open and last-close transitions at a
barrel write `open` through the furnace `lit` path (the entry, its viewers
and container grants stay) and publish `block.barrel.open`/`close` at the
centre of the front face, volume 0.5, the chest pitch draw. Barrels are left
out of chest lid events and the lid publication. A barrel removed while
open closes silently. As in vanilla, a crash while open leaves `open=true`
until the next open and close.

Generated and imported barrels: a loot-table barrel stays a preserved entry
and unpacks into its storage entry on first open or break (the VMC-072b
path); an empty template barrel is the block's fresh entry; an imported
barrel holding only items becomes an owned entry and exports as it came.

Oracles: `ChestSoundOracle` now records all 12 barrel states through a real
`BarrelBlockEntity` (three viewers including a spectator: one open write and
sound, one close write and sound, at the front face) and 48 barrel sound
codec bodies; `ChestMenuOracle` records the barrel's own menu (a three-row
`ChestMenu`, 63 slot identities) and its titled open body.
`priv/protocol/chest_sounds.json` adds the two barrel sound ids.

Tests: `barrel_region_test.exs` (placement in six facings and the owned
entry; first/last viewer open state and sounds with a spectator; menu
clicks, a hopper feeding it, comparator output and the spill on breaking;
contents through a group handoff and a restart; a seeded loot barrel
unrolling once) and a barrel case in `anvil_block_entity_test.exs`. The
connection test for refused block entities now uses an ender chest.

Checks: the barrel and Anvil tests plus the affected container, chest,
hopper, loot, lifecycle, placement, cooking, import and menu suites (105
tests, socket ones included) pass. Eighteen vanilla stages pass: placement,
hoppers, container_drop, loot, block_loot, block_entities, anvil_content,
redstone, table_menu, crafting_table_access and every chest stage
(chest_access, chest_click, chest_view, chest_opening, chest_placement,
chest_block_entity, chest_menu, chest_sound). The client scenario is pending.
