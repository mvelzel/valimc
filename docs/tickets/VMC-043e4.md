# VMC-043e4 — Bats

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e3](VMC-043e3.md).

The caves' ambient fliers (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- A **resting** bat hangs under a block that conducts redstone, snapped to
  its block's top, now and then turning its head and squeaking one time in
  four. A player within four blocks, losing its perch or a hurt wakes it
  (its takeoff, level event 1025).
- A **flying** bat steers its motion toward a random spot a few blocks
  around (picked again when reached, filled, or one time in thirty), its
  vertical motion fading each tick, and now and then perches under a
  conductor above it. It neither pushes nor is pushed, takes no fall
  damage and squeaks a little lower than others.

Spawning (dark caves) is VMC-042a's.

Acceptance:

- Bat behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) equal vanilla's tick for
  tick.
- Its combat facts and entity data match the oracle.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- `Mobs.Bat`: no goals; `custom_step/1` (resting or flying, its target
  spot in `Mind.own`), `after_tick/1` (the hang and the fade),
  `hurt/2`, its flag (`bat_flags`). The forward input its `customServerAiStep`
  sets is zeroed by its move control, as in vanilla, so the kernel needs no
  new control: its steering is an impulse after the dead zone.
- Shared: a family's ambient sound may draw (`{name, self}`), a voice
  `{base, scale}` (`Random.voice_pitch/2`), `falls?/0` (no landing),
  `World.conductor?/2`.

Checks: stage `mob_ai`: 37 scenes, 13675 ticks equal (bats resting, woken
by a player and flying about a cave, flying in a walled yard, woken by a
hit); `ai_facts`, `combat_facts`, `nether_mobs`, `animals`, `paths`,
`mob_travel`, `combat` and `combat_packets` pass.

Client scenario (milestone handoff): in a dark cave, `/summon bat` under
the roof: it hangs; walk under it and it flies off squeaking; it never
pushes you.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
