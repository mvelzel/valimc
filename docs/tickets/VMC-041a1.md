# VMC-041a1 — Goal selectors, sensing, navigation and the shared goal library

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-015i](VMC-015i.md), [VMC-037n](VMC-037n.md).

Mobs think (part of [VMC-041a](VMC-041a.md)). A new AI context
(`lib/valimc/ai`, ranked with Entity) runs vanilla's `Mob` AI for each
living mob of a section inside the owning group, once per section per tick,
before the physics kernel moves them:

- **Tick.** `checkDespawn`'s idle reset near a player (despawning itself is
  VMC-042a's; its random draw is kept), `tickCount`, the expiry of who hurt
  it, `Mob.baseTick`'s ambient sound, a monster's idle time in light, then
  `serverAiStep`: the target and goal selectors (a full update on alternate
  ticks by entity id, else only goals updated every tick), navigation and
  the look control. The kernel runs the move and jump controls (an AI jump
  input is new) and travel; the body rotation control and an animal's age
  and love run after the move. The kernel no longer sets the head yaw.
- **Selectors.** `GoalSelector` with goal flags (move, look, jump, target),
  priorities, interruption and the every-tick goals, over goal modules
  (`Valimc.AI.Goal`) in registration order. Each modelled type's goals are
  checked at compile time against the goals `MobAiOracle facts` reads from
  vanilla's own selectors.
- **Randomness.** Each mob draws from its own `LegacyRandomSource`
  (`Valimc.AI.Random`), seeded from its uuid and saved with it; the hurt
  and death sounds' pitch and an animal's experience draw from it too, so a
  mob draws what vanilla's would from the same seed.
- **Sensing.** `TargetingConditions` (combat, range with the visibility
  percent of crouching, invisible and head-wearing players, line of sight,
  selectors), nearest player and nearest mob of some types, and
  `Sensing.hasLineOfSight` through collision shapes (`Physics.Clip`'s new
  collider mode), cached per tick. Sensors read the group's players
  (`Player.Host.sensed/1`), the section's mobs and published snapshots.
- **Navigation.** `GroundPathNavigation`'s surface target, node following,
  stuck detection, node timeout, cauldron trim and wanted position at the
  node's floor. Until VMC-041b a path is one node, the target: chases go in
  a straight line.
- **Random targets.** `RandomPos`, `DefaultRandomPos` and `LandRandomPos`
  with `GoalUtils`, path types (`getPathTypeStatic` over each state's
  `getPathTypeFromState`, generated) and per-type malus, and walk target
  values (grass for animals, light for animals and monsters).
- **Goal library.** Float, random stroll and its water-avoiding form, look
  at player, random look around, melee attack (and the zombie's raised
  arms) driving VMC-037n's `Mob.doHurtTarget` and a swing, panic, avoid
  entity, tempt, breed (the offspring is VMC-043c's), follow parent, and the
  nearest-attackable and hurt-by targets (alerting others of the kind).
  Goals whose system is not modelled never run and draw nothing (spear
  use, village walks); the zombie's turtle egg search keeps vanilla's timer
  and draws but tramples nothing until VMC-043b.
- **Mobs.** Zombies and pigs have their vanilla goals; pigs are modelled
  (their combat facts and loot). `NoAI` mobs think nothing. The aggressive
  flag (a zombie's raised arms), head yaw, pitch, ambient sounds and the
  26.3 swing packet reach clients.

Acceptance:

- A zombie targets a survival player, chases it (in a straight line) and
  hits it; behind a wall it wanders; it ignores a creative player but looks
  at it; hit by a player it targets it and alerts another zombie.
- A pig wanders over grass, panics when hit, and follows a player holding a
  carrot until close.
- Every tick of each scene matches the oracle bit for bit: position,
  velocity, rotations, random seed, running goals, target, path, hits and
  sounds.
- The cost per mob per tick is bounded (no goal scans more than a bounded
  box, sensing is cached per tick) and linear in the section's mobs.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI` (`think/2`, `settle/3`) is called by `Entity.MobTicks` around
the kernel call; `RegionData.move_mobs/1` builds the AI's world (players,
blocks through `World.View`, game time, difficulty, build height) and sends
each `{:attack, player}` to `mob_attack/3`. Combat's one hurt path records
the attacker and damage type in the mind (`AI.hurt/3`) for the hurt-by
target and panic. A mind saves only its random (vanilla saves no goal
state); its changes wait for the motion pass like travel state.

Facts (`MobAiOracle facts`, stage `ai_facts`): `Mth.atan2`'s tables, every
state's path type and the default malus, items' `mob_visibility`, the swing
packet's fields, and per mob its head limits, ambient sound and interval,
malus overrides and registered goals. `CombatOracle` and `LootOracle` add
the pig.

Not modelled yet: light reads (walk targets and a monster's idle time read
no light, as the oracle's scenes), the heart particles' draws of a mob in
love and breeding itself (VMC-043c), a burning mob's search for water when
panicking, homes and restrictions, riding, mob-against-mob attacks (targets
are players; mobs sense only their own section), the zombie's spear and
sun burning (VMC-043b), step sounds.

Checks: stage `mob_ai` (`MobAiOracle cases`: whole vanilla zombies and pigs
with their own goals over block scenes, players standing still and pushing
as their connection's tick does): 2280 mob ticks of 7 scenes equal bit for
bit. `ai_facts`, `combat_facts`, `combat`, `combat_packets`, `loot` (3000
deaths now, zombie and pig tables) and `mob_travel` pass.
`test/mob_ai_connection_test.exs --include tcp`: in a live group a summoned
zombie targets a logged-in survival player, walks to it and hits it for 3;
a pig follows the player's carrot to within 3 blocks and panics when hit.
`test/combat_connection_test.exs`, the mob travel, entity body, explosion
and falling block suites pass. A dev-build sample of `bench/mob_ticks.exs`
(zombies chasing a player on one floor; the whole mob phase with the AI):
1.4 ms a tick for 100, 4.0 ms for 300, 8.6 ms for 600, about 14 µs a mob.

Client scenario (phase-end handoff): survival, normal difficulty. `/summon
zombie ~6 ~ ~`: it turns its head to you, walks to you with its arms
raised and hits you about once a second; walk away and it follows. Hide
behind a wall: it loses you and wanders. In creative it only looks at you.
`/summon pig ~4 ~ ~` on grass: it wanders and looks around; hold a carrot:
it follows and stops beside you; hit it: it runs off.
