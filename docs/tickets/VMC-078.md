# VMC-078 — M3 gate

Status: In progress
Milestone: M3
Depends on: every M3 slice in the [backlog](../backlog.md#m3--survival-core).

M3 is done when, on the real client, with nothing but vanilla play:

1. **Survival run.** A fresh vanilla world runs from spawn to the Ender
   Dragon's death:
   - Gather, craft, smelt and farm.
   - Fight overworld mobs by day and night.
   - Build a Nether portal from lava and water.
   - Take blaze rods from a fortress spawner and ender pearls from endermen.
   - Throw eyes of ender to a stronghold and fill its portal.
   - Kill the dragon (crystals included) and leave by the exit portal.
   - Along the way, build and use redstone: a button-operated iron door, a
     piston door, a repeater and comparator clock, and a hopper-fed
     furnace.

   Creative and commands are allowed only to shorten travel and gathering
   where the run's script notes it.
2. **Generation.** A sweep over the check seeds makes every chunk without a
   generation error: none fails on a structure's mob or a shape update.
3. **Oracle fixtures.** Every M3 slice's fixtures pass in `check_vanilla.py`.
4. **Tick budget.** A short group tick bench, with natural spawning around
   one player and around eight, stays within the tick budget. It never uses
   the flight bench.

Run 1 is one batched client handoff to the `minecraft-tester` agent.

Acceptance: all four pass; findings become tickets before M3 closes.
