# VMC-024 — Terrain, carvers, aquifers and surfaces

Status: In progress
Milestone: M2 real worlds
Depends on: [VMC-023](VMC-023.md) (noises, compiled density functions, chunk
biomes).

Vanilla 26.3 builds a chunk's terrain in one step (`buildTerrain`) over one
noise chunk: it fills blocks from the final density and the aquifer, applies the
noise settings' material rule (surfaces and ore veins), then runs the carvers
of the biomes around the chunk. Features and structures follow later
(VMC-025). ValiMC still generates flat worlds; this ticket brings vanilla's
terrain, first as a parity-tested foundation, then live in new worlds.

Behaviour comes from the pinned jar (`javap -c` and the private Vineflower
decompilation recorded in [VMC-023](VMC-023.md)) and from oracles that run
vanilla's own classes. The Rust is original.

Subtickets:

| Ticket | Slice | Status |
|---|---|---|
| VMC-024a | Noise fill with aquifers (`doFill`): blocks and fluid post-processing marks equal vanilla's for every noise settings entry | Foundation |
| VMC-024b | Material rules: surfaces, bedrock, ore veins, badlands pillars and icebergs | Foundation |
| VMC-024c | Carvers (caves, canyons, nether caves) and the carving mask | Foundation |
| VMC-024d | Generated terrain live in new worlds (generation on load, heightmaps, light), with an actual-client check | Planned |

Acceptance (parent):

- For every noise settings entry and several seeds, ValiMC's terrain (before
  features and structures) equals vanilla's block for block, including fluid
  post-processing marks, on oracle chunks near and far from the origin.
- New ValiMC worlds generate that terrain when chunks load; a vanilla client
  sees it, with heightmaps and light.
- Generation throughput is benchmarked against vanilla on matched chunks.

## VMC-024a — noise fill with aquifers (October 20, 2026)

- Facts: `noise_settings.json` gains each entry's sea level and default block
  and fluid (vanilla's codec writes a default state as its name);
  `Valimc.Worldgen` turns them into global state ids for the registry, with
  air, water and lava.
- `valimc_core::worldgen::terrain`, from the decompiled `NoiseChunk`,
  `NoiseBasedChunkGenerator` (`doFill`, `createFluidPicker`) and `Aquifer`:
  - The global fluid: lava below -54 (or the sea level, if lower), the default
    fluid below the sea level.
  - Vanilla's noise-based aquifer: aquifer centres on a 16 × 12 × 16 grid from
    the `minecraft:aquifer` positional random, the four closest centres, fluid
    levels from the surface level around them, floodedness, spread and lava
    noises, barriers from pressure, and the fluid-update flag.
  - The fill itself: one cached sampling context per chunk; the aquifer's
    surface-level volume first, then the final density over the chunk at
    block resolution, then each column top-down through the aquifer, so every
    cached volume and point sample happens in vanilla's order. The disabled
    aquifer (End, Nether, caves, floating islands) places the global fluid.
  - `Worldgen::fill_terrain`, NIF `worldgen_fill_terrain`.
- Not covered here: structures (no beardifier) and blending, which later
  slices bring; surfaces and carvers (VMC-024b, VMC-024c).
- Stage `worldgen_terrain` (`TerrainOracle fill`, `check_terrain.exs`): for
  three seeds and all seven noise settings, eight chunks each (two next to the
  origin, five within 32,000 blocks, one up to 30,000,000 blocks away),
  vanilla's own `doFill` runs on a fresh proto chunk with a noise chunk built
  as chunk generation builds it. All 168 chunks (11,403,264 blocks: air,
  stone, netherrack, end stone, water and lava) and all fluid post-processing
  marks equal vanilla's. One changed block or one missing mark fails the
  check.
- A first timing, not a matched benchmark: 2.6 ms per overworld chunk in one
  NIF call (16 chunks, after compiling the samplers).

## VMC-024b — material rules (October 20, 2026)

- Facts (stage `worldgen_facts`): `material_rules.json` (42 entries) and
  `material_conditions.json` (8), the 26.3 registries that replace surface
  rules; `biomes.json` (67 biomes' climate); noise settings gain their
  material rule. `Valimc.Worldgen` turns the rules' block states into state ids
  and passes per-state air, fluid and water flags (block registry and
  `fluid_blocks.json`).
- `valimc_core::worldgen::surface`, from the decompiled `MaterialSystem`,
  `MaterialRuleContext`, `MaterialRules`, the rule and condition types,
  `BiomeManager`, `Biome`, `VerticalAnchor`, `Heightmap`, `ProtoChunk` and
  `TheEndBiomeSource`:
  - Rules (block, bandlands, sequence, condition, ore vein) and conditions
    (biome, noise threshold, vertical gradient, Y, water, temperature, steep,
    not, hole, above preliminary surface, stone depth), with registry
    references, compiled in vanilla's order: ore veins sample their density
    and richness over the chunk through the noise chunk's cached context when
    the rule compiles, as vanilla does.
  - The rule context: surface depth and secondary noise per column, noises per
    column or block, the preliminary surface level (`chunk_surface_level`)
    sampled once per chunk, stone depths above and below, water height.
  - Biomes through vanilla's fuzzy zoom (`BiomeManager`, its seed the first
    eight bytes of the SHA-256 of the world seed, a SHA-256 written here) over
    the chunk biomes of the 3 × 3 chunks around, each filled as chunk
    generation fills it from a fresh lookup history; the End's biome source.
  - Biome temperature as vanilla computes it (frozen modifier noises,
    cooling above the snow line), for snow conditions and icebergs.
  - The material system: clay bands, the eroded badlands pillar and frozen
    ocean iceberg extensions, and the worldgen surface heightmap updated as
    blocks are placed, since later columns read slopes from it.
  - `Worldgen::build_terrain` (fill then surface through one context), NIF
    `worldgen_build_terrain`.
- Stage `worldgen_surface` (`TerrainOracle surface`, `check_terrain.exs`): the
  same 168 chunks after vanilla's own `buildSurface`, with biomes from the
  overworld preset (nether preset and End source for those settings). All
  11,403,264 blocks equal vanilla's, among them bedrock, deepslate, grass,
  dirt, sand, sandstone, gravel, badlands terracotta bands, snow, packed-ice
  icebergs, soul sand and soil, crimson nylium, copper and iron ore veins with
  raw ore blocks and granite and tuff fillers. A changed block fails the check.

## VMC-024c — carvers (October 20, 2026)

- Facts (stage `worldgen_facts`): `carvers.json` (the `worldgen/carver`
  registry: cave, extra underground cave, canyon, nether cave) and each
  biome's carvers in `biomes.json`. `Valimc.Worldgen` adds flags for the
  `uncarvable` block tag, grass and mycelium, and dirt (`Protocol.Data.tags/0`
  exposes the generated tag facts).
- `valimc_core::worldgen::carver`, from the decompiled `WorldCarver`,
  `CaveWorldCarver`, `CanyonWorldCarver`, `CarvingMask`, the value and height
  providers and `NoiseBasedChunkGenerator.generateCarvers`/`applyCarvingMask`:
  - `Mth`'s sine table (a unit test pins its SHA-256 to vanilla's table, so a
    platform whose `sin` differs fails the test), uniform and trapezoid float
    providers, very-biased-to-bottom and uniform int providers, anchored
    heights, `setLargeFeatureSeed`.
  - Caves (rooms, branching tunnels) and canyons (per-height width factors) as
    ellipsoids into the carving mask, in vanilla's order of random draws and
    float arithmetic.
  - The mask applied as vanilla applies it: runs of carved positions column by
    column, each through the noise chunk's aquifer at zero density (kept from
    the fill: `terrain::Aquifer` now outlives it), skipping uncarvable blocks,
    with fluid marks, and dirt uncovered below grass or mycelium turned to the
    material rule's top material (`surface::top_material`, a rule context over
    that block alone).
  - Carver biomes from single biome lookups (climate point samples, fresh
    lookup history) at each source chunk's corner, over the 17 × 17 chunks
    around; the protected top seven blocks.
  - `Worldgen::build_terrain(…, carve)`; NIF `worldgen_build_terrain` gains the
    flag.
- Stage `worldgen_carvers` (`TerrainOracle carve`, `check_terrain.exs`): the
  same 168 chunks after vanilla's own `generateCarvers` (with a biome source
  whose lookups start from a fresh history, as ValiMC's do). All 11,403,264
  blocks and all fluid post-processing marks (3,309) equal vanilla's; carving
  changed 114,489 blocks in 128 chunks (none in the End, which has no
  carvers).
