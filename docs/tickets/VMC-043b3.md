# VMC-043b3 — Skeletons

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Part of [VMC-043b](VMC-043b.md), after [VMC-043b1](VMC-043b1.md).

Skeletons, strays and bogged as vanilla's: the bow goal (strafing, drawing
and shooting through VMC-037o's `mob_shoot`), melee when holding no bow,
fleeing wolves, a skeleton freezing into a stray in powder snow, strays'
slowness arrows, bogged poison arrows and shearing, burning in daylight,
spawn equipment and loot.

Acceptance:

- Each type's finalizeSpawn and its ticks fighting a player match the
  oracle's fixtures.
- On the real client, a skeleton and a player shoot each other.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

`Valimc.AI.Mobs.Skeleton` holds skeletons, strays and bogged: vanilla's
goals with one weapon goal last in order that follows what the mob holds
(`RangedBowAttackGoal` with a bow at its type's interval for the
difficulty, else `MeleeAttackGoal`; it names the goal it stands for),
`finalizeSpawn` (armour, a bow, enchantments, loot pickup, Halloween
heads), the bow's arrows (a stray's slowing, a bogged's poisoning, both on
the hit through `Player.Hurt`) and a skeleton freezing into a stray in
powder snow (`tick_start`, before the rest of its tick, which the
conversion then skips). A conversion is `Valimc.AI.Conversion` now, with
the new kind's `after_conversion/3` (a zombie's attributes).

Shared goals: `RangedBowAttack` (draw, shoot, strafe), `RestrictSun`
(navigation trims a path at its first sunlit node from the shade) and
`FleeSun`. The kernel's move control strafes (`Control::Strafe`; the AI
checks the step's walkability first, as `MoveControl.isWalkable`); mobs use
items (`Mob.using`, the living flags clients see); `Self.look_at_entity` is
`Mob.lookAt`; the AI world knows `isBrightOutside`. Arrows from mobs start
where the mob shot and carry its pitch; arrows carry tipped effects.

Checks: `hostile_ai` (12 scenes, 4604 mob ticks bit for bit) adds a
skeleton walking up, circling and shooting, one without a bow in melee, one
burning by day fleeing into shade, a stray and a bogged shooting, and a
skeleton freezing into a stray. An arrow's flight is drawn from its own
random, so only its type and start compare. `hostile_spawns` adds 3240
skeleton, stray and bogged spawns. `loot` adds their tables. `ai_facts`
gains their goals.

Not modelled yet: a bow's enchantments on its arrows (Power, Punch, Flame,
for players' bows too), the arrows' tint, shearing a bogged, skeleton
horse traps, riding (spider jockeys, VMC-046), effects of arrows hitting
mobs.

Client scenario (with VMC-043b's handoff): survival, normal difficulty, at
night on open ground. `/summon skeleton ~10 ~ ~`: it walks closer, circles
you and shoots about every three seconds; shoot back with a bow: arrows hit
both ways. `/summon stray`: its arrows slow you. `/summon bogged`: its
arrows poison you, and it shoots slower. By day a skeleton catches fire and
walks into nearby shade.
