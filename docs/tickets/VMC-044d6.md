# VMC-044d6 — Patrols, ominous bottle and totem

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044d2](VMC-044d2.md).

Part of [VMC-044d](VMC-044d.md).

- `PatrolSpawner` on the level random in vanilla's order (after phantoms,
  before cats): its timer, daylight, the player draw, no village within
  two sections, the early-game timeline and mushroom fields, group size by
  local difficulty, the leader's target; the `spawn_patrols` game rule.
- The ominous bottle: Bad Omen at its amplifier for 120000 ticks.
- Death protection (the totem): the first hand holding it saves a player or
  a mob at 1 health with its death effects and event 35, unless the
  damage bypasses invulnerability.

Acceptance: the patrol spawner's draws and spawns equal the oracle's
(difficulty, villages, early game, mushroom fields); a bottle of amplifier
N gives Bad Omen N+1 for 100 minutes; a held totem saves players and mobs,
`/kill` excepted.
