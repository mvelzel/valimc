# VMC-041a2 — Brains: memories, sensors, activities; a piglin admires gold

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-041a1](VMC-041a1.md).

Vanilla's `Brain` (part of [VMC-041a](VMC-041a.md)) in the AI context,
beside the goal selectors:

- **Brain** (`Valimc.AI.Brain`): memories with expiry, sensors with their
  scan rates (countdowns drawn from the mob's random when it is made),
  activities (the core set and one other, chosen by the memories each
  requires; a newly active activity erases what the old one erases) and
  behaviours by priority, activity and order, ticked as `Brain.tick`:
  memories age, sensors scan, stopped behaviours of active activities try
  to start, running ones tick or stop. It runs in `customServerAiStep`,
  after navigation and before the look control.
- **Behaviours** (`Valimc.AI.Behavior`): timed behaviours with memory
  conditions and a run time from the level's random, one-shots, `RunOne`
  gates shuffled by their own random, `DoNothing`; shared ones
  (`Valimc.AI.Behaviors`): the look and walk sinks, entity look targets,
  look-and-interact, random stroll, walk to a look target, interact with,
  go to a wanted item. Entity memories hold references read through the
  world each tick; an item's tracker keeps its last place.
- **Sensors** (`Valimc.AI.Sensors`): nearest living entities (their sight
  tested once a scan), players (targetable, attackable), wanted items in
  sight, hurt-by, and the piglin's own (players without gold armor, players
  holding what it loves, adult piglins).
- **Piglin** (`Valimc.AI.Brains.Piglin`): core (sinks, stop holding when
  admiring ends, start admiring a gold item it sees), idle (look at a
  player holding gold, the look and movement `RunOne`s, look at a player
  close by) and admire (walk to the item, stop when too far or tired of
  trying), the activity choice and its sounds, its ambient sound only while
  idle, and looting after the move: a wanted gold item within a block is
  taken into its off hand (the take animation, the item shrinks or goes)
  and admired for 119 ticks. When admiring ends a gold ingot is its barter:
  the throw target is drawn (toward a player it sees, else a random spot)
  and the loot is VMC-043d's; another loved item it drops.
- **Owner**: each section keeps a level random its mobs' behaviours draw
  on; a piglin's pickup and drops change the section's items.

Acceptance:

- A piglin near a dropped gold ingot walks to it, picks it up into its off
  hand and admires it, as the oracle's fixtures do tick by tick.
- Bartering at the end of admiring is VMC-043d's.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

Checks: stage `mob_ai` now also replays two piglin scenes (`MobAiOracle
cases`: a piglin idling over stone for 400 ticks; one walking to a gold
ingot, taking it at tick 15, admiring it for 119 ticks and letting it go,
300 ticks) with the brain's `RunOne` randoms, sensor countdowns and the
level random seeded alike: every tick's running behaviours, activity, off
hand, admire time, level random and item takes equal vanilla's with the
rest, 2980 mob ticks of 9 scenes. `ai_facts` (piglin sounds),
`combat_facts` (piglin) pass. `test/mob_brain_region_test.exs`: in a
region owner a summoned piglin takes a gold ingot lying three blocks away
into its off hand and admires it.

Not modelled yet (VMC-043d, Nether mobs): the fight, avoid, celebrate and
ride activities and their core and idle triggers (attacking players without
gold armor, anger and retaliation when hurt, hunting hoglins, fleeing soul
fire, nemeses and zombified piglins, riding), bartering's loot, food,
nuggets and gear in its inventory, crossbows and spears, zombification
outside the Nether, doors, a player handing it gold. The player sensors
have no vanilla fixture: vanilla's player sensor reads server players,
which the probe cannot make.
