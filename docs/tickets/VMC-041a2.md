# VMC-041a2 — Brains: memories, sensors, activities; a piglin admires gold

Status: Planned
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-041a1](VMC-041a1.md).

Vanilla's `Brain` (part of [VMC-041a](VMC-041a.md)) in the AI context,
beside the goal selectors:

- **Memories** with expiry, **sensors** with their scan rates, **activities**
  with priorities and the memories that start them, and **behaviours** with
  entry conditions, run time and status, as `Brain.tick` runs them.
- A piglin is modelled with the brain parts admiring needs: its sensors
  (nearest players, living entities, wanted items, its own piglin sensor),
  the core activity's look and walk sinks and its admire behaviours, the
  admire activity (go to the wanted item, stop when it is too far or out of
  reach), idle looks and walks, and picking up a gold item into its off hand
  for the admire time.

Acceptance:

- A piglin near a dropped gold ingot walks to it, picks it up into its off
  hand and admires it, as the oracle's fixtures do tick by tick.
- Bartering at the end of admiring is VMC-043d's.
