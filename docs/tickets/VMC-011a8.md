# VMC-011a8 — Geometry admission under larger player loads

Status: Implementation checkpoint; repeated-load/client gates pending. October 15, 2026.
Follows VMC-014c4 (`d10f566`).

128-player dense/spread workloads pass, but 512 spread players sending 20,480
positions over 2.028 s produced 1,056 corrections and failed final reconciliation:
965 admission expirations, 56 worker deadlines, 35 late results; no disconnects.
Only 12,846 geometry jobs started; 103,975 pool-busy attempts. This is an overload
finding, not evidence of 512-player support. Raw workload/fingerprint/counters:
`bench/results/live-motion-spread-512.json`, `.local/live-motion-load-512.log`;
hardware: `bench/results/live-motion-host.txt` (M4, ten schedulers, 24 GiB).

Profile admission, owner-observation waits, pure geometry and publication costs.
Choose BEAM scheduling/ownership from measurements; separate waiting from CPU
capacity if useful. Keep one ordered pending input per player, bounded total work,
source fencing, correction/ack and cancellation semantics. Extending deadlines or
bypassing collision checks does not establish improved throughput.

Acceptance: retain matched before/after workload and implementation hashes;
verify every final authoritative position, corrections, disconnects and elapsed
time. Recheck the 128-player workloads and targeted saturation/source-loss tests.
Delegate fresh actual-client walk/flight/step/tunnel/teleport smoke. Larger flat
creative runs still do not establish survival capacity or fastest-server status.

Implementation: retain one bounded scan plan per pending input/signature instead
of rebuilding it on every rejected pool admission. Each admitted worker resolves
fresh owner incarnations, validates a block sample once, and reuses its immutable
states/fences across body/step/pose queries. Dynamic shapes still resolve for each
context. Owner revisions are rechecked after work; a prepared plan caches no owner
or block facts. Authoritative mode/intent changes invalidate its callback.

The worker cap now follows admitted-player budget (two slots per configured
player, including a cancelled predecessor), rather than scheduler count. Each
player still has one live job; BEAM schedules pure work while owner waits yield.
All packet/mailbox/sample/collider bounds and the 250 ms budget remain.

The original two-second 512-player workload now accepts 20,480 moves with zero
corrections/disconnects and exact final positions (2.046 s). 124 affected tests
pass, including prepared-plan owner replacement, queued mode changes and dynamic
sample contexts. Raw/prepared queries both match 25,631 native collider and 3,072
native composed-pose cases. Evidence: `.local/admission-checkpoint-tests.log`,
`.local/admission-native-geometry-fixed.log`,
`bench/results/admission-player-budget-512.json`.

Profiling sources/results stay in `.local/admission-profile*`. Two later load
samples overlapped a mistaken oracle output argument that temporarily replaced a
facts file. The exact tracked facts were restored; those raw samples have an
`-invalidated` suffix and are excluded. Rerun loads from the frozen checkpoint;
the driver now checks source hashes before/after and fails if they differ.
