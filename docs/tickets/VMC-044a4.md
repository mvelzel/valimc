# VMC-044a4 — Wandering traders

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-044a1](VMC-044a1.md).

Part of [VMC-044a](VMC-044a.md).

- **Wandering traders:** their goals (panic, avoiding zombies and
  illagers, wandering to their target, drinking invisibility at night and
  milk by day) and their despawn delay. They offer no trades (VMC-044).
- **The trader spawner:** its chance and delay saved with the world, a
  trader near a village's bell or a player.

Acceptance:

- The oracle's trader scenes match.
- A group's trader spawner brings a trader near its player.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

**Wandering trader** (`Valimc.AI.Mobs.WanderingTrader`): vanilla's goals at
their priorities — `UseItemGoal` (new, `Valimc.AI.Goals.UseItem`) for a
potion of invisibility when it is dark and it does not show as invisible
and for milk when it is bright and it does, each ending with its sound;
`AvoidEntityGoal` of zombies (every `Zombie`), evokers, vindicators, vexes,
pillagers, illusioners and zoglins at their distances; `PanicGoal`;
`WanderToPositionGoal` (to its wander target, ten blocks at a time when far,
forgetting it on arrival); `MoveTowardsRestrictionGoal` (its home); the
stroll; `InteractGoal` and a look at any mob. The trading goals are
registered and never start. Its despawn delay counts down (`maybeDespawn`)
and it goes at 0. It shows as invisible as its data syncs after the tick
(`updateDataBeforeSync`), so, as in vanilla, it drinks its potion twice at
dusk. Its data (despawn delay, wander target, the shown invisibility) and
its home are saved with it.

**Shared:** a mob's drink (`Valimc.AI.Consume`): the consume sound every
fourth tick after the first fifth of its use, with the draws of
`emitParticlesAndSounds`; when its use is up, the sound again, a potion's
effects or milk's clearing them, and the use remainder in its hand; a
family may play its own consume sound (`consume_sound/2`). A mob's effects
now run down each tick (`tickEffects`, at the end of `baseTick`); their
periodic actions on mobs are not modelled yet. The invisible shared flag
(bit 5) shows.

**Trader spawner** (`Valimc.World.TraderSpawner`, after a group's natural
spawns, overworld, while `spawnWanderingTraders` holds): every 1200 ticks
its delay runs down from 24000; when up, with its chance (25 to 75) and one
time in ten it spawns a trader by the meeting point within 48 blocks of a
random player (else by the player), at the first of ten random spots on the
spawn heightmap where it may stand and has room, outside biomes without
traders; it wanders toward that point, keeps within 16 blocks of it and
goes after 48000 ticks. The spawn kernel answers the spot's height and
whether a trader may stand there (`Native.spawn_position_ok`).

Checks: stage `village` (`VillageOracle`, scenes following the sky): 6039
villager, golem and trader ticks of 14 scenes equal vanilla's, among them a
trader walking to its wander target, drinking invisibility at dusk (twice)
and milk at dawn (equipment, use, the effect's duration, the flag, sounds),
fleeing a zombie and counting its despawn delay down. Stage `ai_facts` (its
goals) and `loot` (its table). `test/natural_spawning_test.exs`: the
spawner brings a trader with its despawn delay, wander target and home.

Not modelled yet: its trades and everything that reads them (VMC-044), its
two trader llamas (llamas are VMC-043's), the spawner's delay and chance
saved with the level (each group counts its own and starts afresh), and
the effects' periodic actions on mobs.

Client scenario (phase-end handoff): survival, `/summon wandering_trader`
by day: it wanders and looks at you; `/time set 13000`: it drinks a potion
(the drinking sound, a bottle in its hand) and turns invisible;
`/time set day`: it drinks milk and shows again; `/summon zombie` next to
it: it backs away.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
