# VMC-004b6 — Item-holding components

Status: Done
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-004b](VMC-004b.md). Depends on [004b4](VMC-004b4.md).

`container` (a shulker box's 27 slots, at most 256), `bundle_contents`,
`use_remainder`, `sulfur_cube_content`, `pot_decorations` and
`charged_projectiles` hold item stack templates, which hold their own patches. Nesting is bounded: a depth
limit of 16 stack levels, the existing 64 KiB per stack on the network and
the data form's NBT budget, all checked before allocation.

Acceptance: a shulker box holding a bundle holding a named, enchanted item
round-trips both formats, and its identity compares its contents. Depth and
size bombs fail without unbounded work. The `item_components` stage covers
nested and empty slots, sparse container slots and the 256-slot bound.
Putting items into a bundle and taking them out is its own slice
([004b9](VMC-004b9.md)).

## Result (October 31, 2026)

`ItemStack.take_template/1` and `encode_template/1` read and write
`ItemStackTemplate` (item, count 1..99, its own patch) without the stack
rules a template need not meet; `ItemData.template/1` and `template_tag/1`
do the data form, the item alone or `{id, count, components}` with the count
left out at 1. A template's patch is kept as written, defaults included, as
vanilla keeps it, and compares that way (`ItemStackTemplate.equals`).

- Templates nest at most 16 deep (`:nesting_too_deep`), counted on the
  caller's process across both formats; every list is still bounded by the
  bytes left, the stack's 64 KiB and the data form's NBT limits.
- A container is a list of slots, `nil` when empty, at most 256; its data
  form lists the filled ones by slot and reads back up to the highest.
- A container's, a bundle's and a crossbow's held stacks must fit their own
  items (`validateContainedItemSizes`); a use remainder need not.
- `charged_projectiles` moved from its hand-written codec to the schema and
  gained its data form, so a loaded crossbow now survives world export.

The `item_components` stage passes 3,873 values of 98 types, among them a
container whose slot 26 holds a bundle holding a shulker box holding a
named, enchanted, damaged pickaxe. A nested patch's entries may arrive in
Mojang's map order, so the network check accepts other orders that decode
to the same value. Tests: depth 16 accepted and 17 refused, an oversized
held stack refused, a remainder's count unconstrained.
