# VMC-032c4 — Copper chests

Status: Planned
Note: started October 31, 2026 and stopped when the lanes halted; no change was kept.
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-032c](VMC-032c.md). Depends on [032c1](VMC-032c1.md).

The eight copper chests (four weathering stages, waxed or not) are chests
with a copper lid sound: single or joined, the same menus and machines.
They weather by random ticks as copper blocks do, an axe scrapes and wax
seals them, and every change keeps the entry and its contents. VMC-034f's
client run found a copper chest invisible after a reconnect because its
chunk carried no block entity; that goes with this slice. Copper golems
are M5.

Acceptance: the chest oracles over copper states; weathering, waxing and
scraping keep contents; a reconnect shows the chest. Client scenario (M4
batch): place and join copper chests, fill them, scrape and wax them.
