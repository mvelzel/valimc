# VMC-050a1 — Explosions and primed TNT

Status: In progress
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043a](VMC-043a.md) (primed TNT bodies),
[VMC-077o](VMC-077o.md) (box edits). Part of [VMC-050a](VMC-050a.md).

A primed TNT falls, slides and explodes when its fuse runs out; the
explosion breaks blocks, drops their loot, primes TNT it reaches, and hurts
and pushes the entities around it, as vanilla's `ServerExplosion` does.

## Design

- **Kernel.** `valimc_core::physics::explosion` casts vanilla's 1,352 rays
  (`calculateExplodedPositions`) over published rows against the fact
  table's explosion resistance, drawing from a `LegacyRandomSource` seed,
  and returns the reached cells with their states; it measures exposure
  (`getSeenPercent`) with a collider ray per sample. The resistance column
  comes from `BlockFactsOracle` (`getBlockExplosionResistance` per state).
  `physics::block_body` ticks a primed TNT (`PrimedTnt.tick` with
  `Entity.move`: gravity, collision, restitution, block speed, drag, the
  ground slow-down) for many bodies in one call, like items.
- **Rules.** `Living.Explosions`: the default calculator's damage, the
  knockback, the entity box and difficulty scaling. The `explosion` damage
  type's message, exhaustion and scaling are in `damage_messages.json`
  (`DamageMessageOracle`); the items an explosion cannot hurt (the nether
  star) in `item_armor_wear.json`. Armor wears by a quarter of the damage,
  at least one point (`Inventory.hurtArmor`), for any amount.
- **Owner.** A section's items phase ticks its primed TNT
  (`World.BlockBodies`); a body crossing into a sibling moves there in the
  group commit. At fuse 0 the body leaves the store and `World.Explosion`
  runs: the reach from the rows as they stand; the section's own entities
  hit now (items and orbs past 4 damage destroyed, mobs hurt through
  `Living.Damage`, everything pushed); siblings' entities through the
  group's sibling effects; the group's players by their sessions
  (`Player.Explosions`: damage scaled by difficulty, knockback in the server
  velocity and the packet, the explode packet to every player within 64
  blocks).
- **Crater.** The crater commits from a job (one per section, later craters
  wait, at most 64) as one `BoxEdit` across every owner it touches, groups
  included. Each part carries its drops and primed TNT as entity records
  the owner adopts in the edit's frame (`BoxEdit` parts gained `entities`).
  Drops are the tool-less mining loot, each item surviving `1 / radius`
  (the TNT drop-decay rule), merged into stacks of at most 16 at the first
  cell of each (`addOrAppendStack`), popped like `Block.popResource`. TNT it
  reaches primes with a 10–29 tick fuse. A cell whose state changed since
  is read again and planned from what stands there.
- **Priming.** Flint and steel or a fire charge on TNT primes it (80 ticks)
  in one box edit with the hiss (`World.Sounds`), and wears or uses the item.
- **Packet.** `Protocol.ExplodePacket`; vanilla's particle, sound and block
  particle bytes come from `ExplosionOracle packet`.

Not here: fire from explosions and entity sources (fireballs, beds, who lit
the TNT) come with their sources; cells holding a block entity stand (their
contents need the container spill); explosion knockback resistance (Blast
Protection) and item health below the 4-damage line are not modelled;
redstone does not prime TNT yet; a TNT does not cross a group line by
moving (groups keep their loaded chunks apart).

## Acceptance

- Craters, exposure and damage equal the oracle's (`explosion` stage), and
  a primed TNT's motion equals vanilla's tick for tick (`tnt_travel`).
- A primed TNT falls, explodes after its fuse, breaks a crater that commits
  as one box edit (also across a group line), drops loot and primes the TNT
  it reaches; players and entities nearby are hurt and pushed.

## Results

- `explosion` stage: 72 craters over six scenes (flat stone, mixed blocks
  with water and bedrock, a pool, the world's bottom and top, walls) at radii
  1, 3, 4 and 6 equal vanilla's cell sets; 81 exposure and damage cases and
  three explode packets equal vanilla's values and bytes.
- `tnt_travel` stage: 8,640 `PrimedTnt.tick` observations over stone, ice,
  slime, honey, slabs and soul sand, with a wall and a ceiling, equal the
  kernel's position, velocity and ground flag.
- `test/explosion_test.exs`: a primed TNT on the line between two separate
  owners blows one crater on both sides, leaves the obsidian under it,
  primes the TNT block it reaches and hurts and pushes a zombie; the plan
  splits by region. `test/explosion_connection_test.exs` (tcp): a survival
  player six blocks away is hurt and gets the explode packet with knockback
  away from the TNT.
