# VMC-077i2 — One menu click engine and one menu access

Status: Done
Milestone: foundations II (between M2 and M3)
Depends on: [VMC-077i](VMC-077i.md).

Before: vanilla's `AbstractContainerMenu.doClick` existed three times, as
`ContainerClick` (chests: pick up, shift-click and swaps only), `CookingClick`
(furnaces: the same three, with fuel and result predicates) and the click part
of `Inventory` (the player's inventory and the crafting table: every gesture),
with `ChestMenu`, `CookingMenu` and `TableMenu` mapping window slots. The grant
check for opening and using a block menu was copied into `ChestAccess`,
`ChestOpening`, `CookingOpening` and `TableAccess` (transfers B4).

Acceptance:

- `Valimc.Menu` describes every menu as slots: which storage a window slot
  addresses, what a slot accepts and how many (`may_place?`, `limit`, vanilla's
  `mayPlace` and `getMaxStackSize`), where a shift-click routes a stack, the
  slots a double click collects from, and the window's type and contents for
  packets.
- `Valimc.MenuClick` is the one engine over those descriptions, for the
  inventory, a crafting table, chests and furnaces: pick up and put down,
  outside-the-window drops, shift-click, number and offhand swaps, creative
  clone, throw, drag and double-click collect, and the crafting result's
  gestures. A slot that takes nothing gives its whole stack to a matching
  cursor (vanilla's `tryRemove`), and outside the slots vanilla's early returns
  are no-ops. A swap past a slot's limit and items with their own click
  behaviour stay refused. Hoppers and droppers (M3) add a description, not an
  engine.
- Chest and furnace clicks still cross to their owner as container requests
  until VMC-077i3, so a request carries only the gestures that move nothing
  into the world and no drag, as before.
- `Valimc.MenuAccess` is the one grant check for chests, furnaces and crafting
  tables (grant shape, freshness, opening use, owned entry, reach, revision)
  and holds the chest's opening obstruction; the chest's block identity facts
  moved to `Valimc.Block.Chest`.
- The vanilla stages compare the engine with Mojang 26.3 unchanged: chest
  clicks, cooking clicks with their refused overflow proposals, 11,990 table
  click traces, the player's crafting gestures, bulk and remainder crafting,
  and the chest, cooking and table menus and access facts.

Deleted: `ContainerClick`, `CookingClick`, `ChestMenu`, `CookingMenu`,
`TableMenu`, the click half of `Inventory` (795 lines to 296), `ChestAccess`,
`ChestOpening`, `CookingOpening` and `TableAccess`, and four tests the stages
cover.

Checks: `mix test.fast`, the full `mix test` (menu clicks run in every
inventory connection test), the changed vanilla stages (93), the audit.
