# VMC-050a2 — Falling blocks

Status: Planned
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-050a1](VMC-050a1.md) (block-like bodies). Part of
[VMC-050a](VMC-050a.md).

Sand, gravel, concrete powder and anvils with nothing under them fall as
`minecraft:falling_block` bodies and land as blocks, as vanilla's
`FallingBlock` and `FallingBlockEntity` do: a scheduled tick two ticks
after a change, the landing rules (replaceable, surviving, waterlogging),
concrete powder hardening in water, a drop where it cannot land, and an
anvil's damage to what it lands on.

Acceptance:

- Sand falls and lands as in vanilla (oracle motion and landing cases).
- A falling anvil hurts and wears as vanilla's.
