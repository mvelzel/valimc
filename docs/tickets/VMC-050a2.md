# VMC-050a2 — Falling blocks

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-050a1](VMC-050a1.md) (block-like bodies). Part of
[VMC-050a](VMC-050a.md).

Sand, red sand, gravel, concrete powder, anvils and the dragon egg with
nothing under them fall as `minecraft:falling_block` bodies and land as
blocks, as vanilla's `FallingBlock` and `FallingBlockEntity` do.

## Design

- **Fall check.** The block rules schedule a falling block's check two
  ticks out when it is placed or a neighbour changes (`FallingBlock.onPlace`
  and `updateShape`, live play only), for every state the fact table flags
  as a `FallingBlock`; concrete powder beside water whose face toward it is
  not sturdy hardens there (`touchesLiquid`). At the tick,
  `Blocks.FallingBlock` falls when the cell below is free (`isFree`: air,
  replaceable or liquid) and inside the world: the cell keeps its fluid and
  a falling block body starts at its bottom centre (`FallingBlockEntity.fall`).
- **Body.** `Entity.Body` models `minecraft:falling_block`: its state (the
  spawn packet's data), time, fall distance, start cell (entity data), and
  whether it hurts, drops and breaks. The physics kernel ticks it with the
  primed TNT's step (gravity, collision, block speed, drag).
- **Landing.** On the ground, or concrete powder in water, it places its
  block where the cell can be replaced and it would not fall on (concrete
  powder hardening where the replaced cell or a side holds water,
  `ConcretePowderBlock.onLand`), in the section's next frame with its
  updates; otherwise it drops its item at the body (`spawnAtLocation`). One
  that falls 100 ticks outside the world or 600 ticks anywhere drops.
- **Anvils.** A landing anvil hurts the living entities in its box by 2 per
  block fallen past the first, at most 40 (`falling_anvil`, the section's
  mobs and the group's players in survival or adventure), and chips, damages
  or breaks with a chance of 5% plus 5% per block; it raises the land or
  break sound (level events 1031 and 1029).

Not here: suspicious sand and gravel (brushable, with their block entity),
pointed dripstone and scaffolding falls, the concrete-powder clip of a very
fast fall into water, block entity data carried by a falling block, and a
fall's dust particles (client side).

## Acceptance

- Sand falls and lands as in vanilla (oracle motion and landing cases).
- A falling anvil hurts and wears as vanilla's.

## Results

- `falling_block` stage: `FallingBlock.isFree` over all 35,723 states (914
  free) and 48 real `FallingBlockEntity` falls (sand, gravel, red concrete
  powder, an anvil; onto stone, a torch, a slab and into water; from three
  heights) equal the kernel's motion tick for tick, and the landing or drop.
- `test/falling_block_test.exs`: sand placed in the air lands on the floor,
  sand falling onto a torch drops one sand item, red concrete powder falling
  into water hardens, and an anvil falling six blocks onto a zombie takes
  10 of its health and lands.
- `mix test --stale` 585/586 (the one failure, a block-query timeout under
  host load in `block_observation_test.exs`, passes alone);
  `check_vanilla.py --changed` 100/100.

## Client scenario

In creative over open ground: `/setblock ~ ~10 ~3 sand`, `/setblock ~2 ~10
~3 gravel` and an anvil the same way: each falls smoothly to the ground and
lands with no flicker; place a torch and let sand fall onto it: the sand
breaks into an item. Pour water and drop red concrete powder into it: it
lands as red concrete. In survival, stand under a falling anvil: hurt.

flicker; sand onto a torch broke into an item; red concrete powder into water
became red concrete; a two-block anvil hurt the survival player by one heart.

`BlockBodies.facing/1`: `Map.take/2` on the property list of
`Blocks.properties/1`) whenever the anvil wore, which is certain from twenty
blocks and about one fall in three from six. The falling body was saved
mid-air, so the owner crashed again on reload and kicked the rejoining
player. Fixed in `cab65365` (the facing map is built from the list); the
anvil test in `test/falling_block_test.exs` passed seven runs in a row
(before, it failed about one in three).
