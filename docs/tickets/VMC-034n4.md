[earned cooking output](VMC-034n.md).

Acceptance:

- Establish native launch randomness, gravity, collision/bounce, drag, water/lava,
  attraction, merge/count/age and expiry rules through focused differential probes.
  Start with pure motion/lifetime kernels and explicit environmental inputs.
- Separate immutable award/adoption receipts from mutable active bodies. Persist
  stable UUIDs, motion, age/count and terminal decisions with bounded versioned
  storage. Recovery must never recreate an expired, merged or transferred body
  from its creation receipt. Preserve existing versioned snapshots and obligations.
- Use region-owned batches and bounded geometry workers, explicit ordering with
  transfers/removals and revision/epoch/deadline fences. Keep I/O and CPU planning
  outside owners. Measure moving/resting populations and preserve cadence without
  a process per orb. Observations never authorize target selection or mutations.
- Define durable moving-owner handoff separately from initial spawn delivery;
  retain one authority through destination backpressure, uncertainty and recovery.
  Do not mutate the accepted initial request or use its replay as a moving handoff.
- Actual client: natural launch/fall/landing, water/lava, attraction, boundary
  movement, merge and near-expiry removal, then owner replacement/full restart.
  Verify one body/count, no resurrection, conserved picked/remaining XP, ordinary
  item behavior and empty-output negatives. Delegate the verdict to the sole tester.
