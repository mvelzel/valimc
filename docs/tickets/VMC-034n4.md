# VMC-034n4 — Authoritative XP orb simulation

Status: Planned. Depends on [initial materialization](VMC-034n3.md); part of
[earned cooking output](VMC-034n.md).

Replace static staging with server-owned launch, movement and lifetime. Player XP,
Mending, recipe grants and earned-output admission remain separate prerequisites.

Acceptance:

- Establish native launch randomness, gravity, collision/bounce, drag, water/lava,
  attraction, merge/count/age and expiry rules through focused differential probes.
  Start with pure motion/lifetime kernels and explicit environmental inputs.
- Separate immutable award/adoption receipts from mutable active bodies. Persist
  stable UUIDs, motion, age/count and terminal decisions with bounded versioned
  storage. Recovery must never recreate an expired, merged or transferred body
  from its creation receipt. Preserve existing versioned snapshots and obligations.
- Use region-owned batches and bounded geometry workers, explicit ordering with
  transfers/removals and revision/epoch/deadline fences. Keep I/O and CPU planning
  outside owners. Measure moving/resting populations and preserve cadence without
  a process per orb. Observations never authorize target selection or mutations.
- Define durable moving-owner handoff separately from initial spawn delivery;
  retain one authority through destination backpressure, uncertainty and recovery.
  Do not mutate the accepted initial request or use its replay as a moving handoff.
- Actual client: natural launch/fall/landing, water/lava, attraction, boundary
  movement, merge and near-expiry removal, then owner replacement/full restart.
  Verify one body/count, no resurrection or player XP gain, ordinary item behavior
  and cooking/output-refusal negatives. Delegate the verdict to the sole tester.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1 `33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`,
`ExperienceOrb` constructors, `tick`, `followNearbyPlayer`, `setUnderwaterMovement`,
`scanForMerges`, `merge`, save/load and generic `Entity.move`/block-effect hooks.
Private bytecode `.local/experience-orb.javap` shows movement before drag/bounce,
age increment before the 6,000-tick discard, and client-side attraction even when
gravity is disabled. Turn these observations into native probes before claiming
physics parity. Pinned upstream references remain in the materialization ticket.

Reuse the proven scheduling/geometry and durable-transfer ideas from ordinary
items where they fit XP behavior. Keep XP authority and accounting distinct;
the ordinary item pickup path must never consume XP. Initial creation records
currently reject consumption or changed poses; they cannot serve as active bodies.

Next: native pure kernels and boundary regressions, then mutable storage/receipts
before live simulation. Each slice needs targeted checks; this subticket never
triggers broad integration by itself. The roadmap may split/reorder this scope.
