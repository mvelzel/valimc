# VMC-043e1 — Endermites and shulkers

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-041b](VMC-041b.md), [VMC-037o](VMC-037o.md), [VMC-042b](VMC-042b.md).

The End's mobs (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- **Endermites** (from ender pearls) chase and bite players, alert each
  other when hit, climb out of powder snow, and one that is not persistent
  is gone after 2400 ticks.
- **Shulkers** (end cities) sit on a block face, peek now and then, open
  fully to shoot bullets at a player within 20 blocks, take 20 more armor
  and no arrows while closed, find another face or teleport when they cannot
  stay, and may teleport when hurt below half health or hit by a bullet
  (which can leave a second shulker). They drop their shells.
- **Shulker bullets** fly through blocks one axis at a time toward their
  target, turning every 10 to 50 ticks, at blocks in the way and when lined
  up with it; what they hit takes 4 and Levitation for ten seconds; a block
  stops them; a hit breaks them.

Acceptance:

- Endermite and shulker behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`) and bullet
  flights (`ShulkerBulletOracle`) equal vanilla's tick for tick.
- Shulker loot, combat facts and entity data match the oracle.
- In an owner a shulker with nothing to hold teleports onto a floor, a
  bullet hurts and lifts its target, and an old endermite leaves.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- **Mob families.** A mob's own code is one family module
  (`Valimc.AI.Mobs.*`, the optional callbacks of `Valimc.AI.Kind`) listed in
  `Valimc.AI.Mobs`, which VMC-043b's zombies share: goals, navigation,
  brain, entity data, dimensions and box, how the kernel moves and pushes
  it (`motion/0`), hooks where vanilla's subclasses override the tick
  (`tick_start`, `before_ai`, `custom_step`, `after_ai`, `after_tick`), its
  look control, ambient sound and hurt reactions, dispatched by
  `Mobs.hook/4` from `AI`, `Look`, `Combat` and `MobMotion`. A family may
  move its mob in the AI (a teleport): `AI.think/2` and `AI.settle/4`
  return the position.
- **Kernel.** Mobs report and read powder snow (`isInPowderSnow`,
  `wasInPowderSnow`, `canEntityWalkOnPowderSnow`: a walker climbs out of
  it); a mob can be fixed (never moves, no motion), not pushable or not
  pushing. The projectile kernel has the bullet's kind (steering, no
  collision, half-way turns).
- **Goals.** `ClimbOnTopOfPowderSnowGoal` joins the shared library;
  `LookAtPlayerGoal` looks level (`onlyHorizontal`), `HurtByTargetGoal`
  ignores types and `NearestAttackableTargetGoal` may skip peaceful.
- **Shulkers.** `Mobs.Shulker` (`Peek`, `Attack`), `AI.Shapes` (collision
  boxes through the AI's world, free boxes, full faces). Bullets are
  projectile bodies (`Entity.ShulkerBullet`, planned in
  `World.Projectiles`); the owner makes them, offspring and the teleport
  trail from the mobs' effects (`RegionData.move_mobs`).
- **Facts.** Entity data fields for every VMC-043e mob (`EntityOracle`),
  the shulker in `combat_facts` and `loot`, its sounds and look axes in
  `ai_facts`.

Client scenario (milestone handoff): survival, normal, in the End. `/summon
shulker ~3 ~ ~`: it opens, a bullet floats toward you around blocks and
lifts you; it closes when you walk away 20 blocks. Hit it with arrows while
closed: they glance off. Hurt it below half: it may teleport. Kill it: a
shell may drop. Throw ender pearls until an endermite appears: it chases
and bites.
