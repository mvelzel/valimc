# VMC-004b9 — Bundle interactions

Status: Planned
Milestone: M4
Parent: [VMC-004b](VMC-004b.md). Depends on [004b6](VMC-004b6.md).

Clicks with a bundle are refused today (`Menus.Click`'s
`inventory_click_override`). Vanilla's `BundleItem.overrideStackedOnOther`
and `overrideOtherStackedOnMe` put a stack into a bundle and take the
selected one out; `ServerboundSelectBundleItemPacket` picks which; using a
bundle drops its contents one by one. The bundle's weight rule
(`BundleContents.weight`: an item weighs 1/max stack size, a nested bundle
1/16 plus its contents, a beehive with bees 1) bounds what fits.

Acceptance: insertion, extraction, selection and the drop-contents use match
the oracle's `BundleItem` traces, as durable menu transactions. Client
scenario (M4 batch): fill a bundle from the inventory and empty it again.
