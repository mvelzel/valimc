loaded End sections share one owner, with no outgoing transfer, and the assigned

dragon. Vanilla's `EnderDragon.checkDespawn` overrides that tick with no action;
its distance, peaceful difficulty, persistence flag and idle counter cannot turn
it into an ordinary monster despawn. Keep this policy in the existing native
removal dispatch, while preserving normal monster despawns and the fight's
existing missing-body recovery. Do not add a persistence flag to hide the issue.

  hostile can still despawn; existing flight/combat and native checks pass.
