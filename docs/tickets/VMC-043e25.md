# VMC-043e25 — Collision-aware dolphin breathing movement

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e5](VMC-043e5.md), [VMC-043e17](VMC-043e17.md).

The breathing goal moves its dolphin before navigation and move controls run.
Those decisions must read the collision-resolved position, including step-up
and neighboring solid entities. Observing the move must not move the ticker
or consume the mob's turn; its actual physics step still performs it once.

Acceptance: position observations match actual native `BreathAirGoal.tick`
against walls, ceilings, slabs, stairs and shulkers. Repeated observations are
identical and leave stored velocity unchanged. Complete low-air mob traces
match native controls, travel and random state. Actual-client tests are deferred.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `BreathAirGoal.tick`,
`LivingEntity.aiStep`, `Entity.move` and `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl.tick`.

Implementation: the mob kernel shares the tick prefix and goal move between
its real tick and a read-only ticker observation. The observation uses current
solid colliders and runs on a dirty scheduler. `MobTicks` supplies the callback
only while that mob thinks and clears it afterwards. The former duplicate
Elixir input-vector prediction is removed; missing geometry supplies no
invented position. The actual tick remains the sole movement authority.

Validation: `DolphinBreathOracle` invokes the actual native goal in 480 cases
over six layouts, five velocities (including the dead zone), four headings,
grounded/airborne and two heights. Positions match exactly; repeated reads
agree and preserve ticker velocity. The former prediction disagrees in 288
cases, including floor collisions in the otherwise free layout. The complete
mob trace now includes a low-air dolphin under a roof: all 18,263 ticks of 55
scenes match native goals, controls, motion, random draws and effects.

All 20 focused owner/socket tests pass. All nine affected native areas pass:
`dolphin_breath`, `mob_ai`, `shulker_mob_collision`, `water_age`, `tcp`,
`hostile_ai`, `nether_mobs`, `animals`, `village`. Core Rust's 124 tests, NIF
crate tests, workspace Clippy and compilation without parity entry points
pass; Clippy retains the six existing type-complexity warnings. Evidence:
`.local/m3-dolphin-breath-{native,before,check,final-check,tests,rust,stages,shared-stages,audit}.log`.

Deferred client scenario: a dolphin low on air swims under an overhang,
finds its way around the obstacle and reaches air without clipping through it.
