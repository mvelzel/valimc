# VMC-044b1 — Trade facts, random sequences and offers

Status: Foundation
Note: Live once VMC-044b2 opens the merchant menu.
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044a1](VMC-044a1.md), [VMC-044a4](VMC-044a4.md).

Part of [VMC-044b](VMC-044b.md).

- **Facts:** the oracle writes every `villager_trade` (its codec's JSON),
  every `trade_set` with its trades in holder-set order, amount,
  `allow_duplicates` and random sequence, and each profession's set per
  level (`VillagerProfession.getTrades`), into `priv/loot/trades.json`;
  the loot evaluator parses the trades' providers, predicates and
  modifiers with its own.
- **Random sequences:** vanilla's `RandomSequences` (one per server, a
  Xoroshiro source per id seeded from the world seed, the salt and the
  id's hash), owned by one process per world. A group leases a sequence
  for one draw of offers and returns it; a second lessee waits for the
  return; a lessee that dies gives the sequence back as it was. Advanced
  sequences are written to the world's `random_sequences.json` at most
  once a second, so a crash may replay a second of draws but never
  touches an item.
- **Offers:** `VillagerTrade.getOffer` (predicate, result, modifiers,
  additional trade cost, double price enchantments, costs clamped to the
  stack size, at least one use) and `addOffersFromTradeSet` with and
  without duplicates, over the trade-set's sequence. A villager's offers
  are made when first asked (`getOffers`): its profession's set at its
  level; a profession change drops them. A wandering trader's are its
  `buying`, `common` and `uncommon` sets. Offers (`MerchantOffer`'s
  fields) are saved with the mob.

Acceptance:

- Stage `trades`: for several world seeds, every profession at every level
  and every villager type, and wandering traders, the offers equal
  vanilla's, drawn in sequence as vanilla draws them (several villagers
  sharing sequences).
- A lessee crashing mid-lease leaves the sequence as before its lease; a
  restart reads the written sequences.
- A villager's and a trader's offers survive their record.

Reference: `VillagerTrade`, `TradeSet`, `TradeCost`, `MerchantOffer`,
`AbstractVillager.addOffersFromTradeSet`, `Villager.updateTrades`,
`WanderingTrader.updateTrades`, `RandomSequences`, `RandomSequence`
(26.3, jar SHA-1 `33680f5f…`).

## Implementation (October 31, 2026)

**Facts** (`TradeOracle facts`, `priv/loot/trades.json`, stage `trades`):
the 391 trades through `VillagerTrade.CODEC`, the 68 sets with their trades
in holder-set order, each profession's set per level, the wandering
trader's sets in its draw order (buying, uncommon, common), the potion tags
and `DyeColor.VALUES`' colours. `Blocks.Loot.registries/0` carries them to
the native registry.

**Draws** (`valimc_core::loot::trades`, `Valimc.Menus.Offers`): `getOffer`
and both of `addOffersFromTradeSet`'s loops over the loot evaluator, which
gained what trades use: the `minecraft:add` provider, `set_random_dyes`
(`DyedItemColor.applyDyes`), `set_random_potion`,
`include_additional_cost_component` on both enchant functions, item filter
predicates on dyed colour and (stored) enchantments, the entity predicate's
`minecraft:predicates` (a merchant's `villager/variant`). A draw that needs
an exploration map's structure without an answer stops before taking
anything and answers `{:locate, tag, radius}`; map ids are not allocated
(maps are VMC-051), so every map takes id 0, as container loot's do.

**Random sequences** (`Valimc.World.RandomSequences`, in the infrastructure
domain): the server's sequences seeded as `RandomSequences.createSequence`
does (world seed ^ salt, the id's MD5, Stafford13 mixing). `with/3` lends
one sequence to one caller at a time (others wait, at most 1024); a lessee
that dies gives it back unchanged; returned states are written to
`random_sequences.json` by a task at most once a second and on a graceful
stop. `Valimc.World.Trades.draw/3` draws a merchant's sets through it.

**Offers on the mob:** villagers and wandering traders carry `offers` (nil
until first asked) in their family data, so their records save them; a
villager that takes or loses a profession drops its offers
(`setVillagerData`).

Checks: stage `trades` (4 world seeds; per seed the 13 trading professions
in all 7 villager types through their five levels, then three wandering
traders, all sharing the sequences; one seed whose map searches find
nothing): 1832 draws, 3531 offers equal vanilla's; `--regen` verified.
Stages `loot`, `block_loot`, `village`, `animals` pass.
`test/random_sequences_test.exs`: the lease, a waiting second caller, a
dead lessee, the written file read by a restarted owner, and a group's draw
(a librarian's sequence advancing, a cartographer's map search stopping the
draw without touching its sequence).
