# VMC-043e7 — Axolotls

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e6](VMC-043e6.md), [VMC-041a2](VMC-041a2.md).

Lush caves' amphibians (part of [VMC-043e](VMC-043e.md)):

- An axolotl thinks with a brain: it strolls by vanilla's widening swim
  search in water and walks on land, heads for water within six blocks when
  dry, looks at players now and then, follows a tempting player or an
  adult, and breeds on tropical fish (fed from a bucket, which leaves the
  water bucket).
- It hunts fish and squid in water (unless it hunted in the last two
  minutes), bites them where vanilla's melee lands (mid-tick: the prey
  ticks hurt, a kill ends the chase at once) and leaves the player who
  killed something nearby Regeneration and no Mining Fatigue.
- Hit in water, one time in three it may play dead for ten seconds:
  still, unseen by enemies and healing under Regeneration.
- It swims with the smooth swimming controls (shared with dolphins), travels
  in water at its own speed, dries out on land (6000 ticks of air, water or
  rain refill it) and comes in its spawn's variant (a blue one in 1200
  births).

Acceptance:

- Axolotl behaviour fixtures (`MobAiOracle cases`, its brain seeded as the
  oracle seeds it) equal vanilla's tick for tick: swimming about, hunting
  a cod to its death, playing dead when hit, heading for water from land.
- Their combat, animal and entity facts match the oracle.

## Implementation (October 23, 2026)

- `Entity.Animals.Axolotl` (an animal: variant, playing dead, from a
  bucket; its controls, air, hurt choice) and `AI.Brains.Axolotl` (core,
  idle, fight and play-dead activities); shared parts: `AI.SmoothSwimming`,
  `Behaviors.swim_stroll/1` (`RandomPos.swim_target/1`), a try-all gate, a
  stroll's own condition; a partner's memories set at once (`{:remote, ...}`).
- Mid-tick melee on mobs: `MobTicks` lands a mob's hit (with its family's
  extras) on a mob of its tick before either moves on
  (`Combat.hit_body/3`; the thinker senses a kill through `World.melee/3`),
  a brain mob's target is its valid attack target, and a hurt mob still to
  tick runs its timers that tick.
- Mobs' effects tick in one place (`MobVitals.base_tick/2`: periodic heal
  and hurt, durations); a family's default travel holds while its AI does
  not choose one (a dead fish's). The oracle reports a mob target by its entity id and removes a
  dead mob after its death animation, as the replay now does.

Checks: stage `mob_ai`: 50 scenes, 17463 ticks equal (four axolotl scenes);
`animal_test.exs`; see the commit for the other stages.

Not modelled yet: picking an axolotl (or a fish) up in a water bucket, a
group's later babies from spawn group data, the bucket's saved variant.

Client scenario (milestone handoff): in a pool, `/summon axolotl` and
`/summon cod`: the axolotl hunts the cod. Hit the axolotl in water: now and
then it plays dead and heals. On land it heads back to the water.
