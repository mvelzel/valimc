# VMC-043e18 — One entity simulation per group tick across sections

Status: Done
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e17](VMC-043e17.md).

An entity that crosses from an earlier section into a later section of the same
group currently can travel again in the destination's entity phase. Fence
simulation and ageing to once per entity per group tick while preserving the existing
whole-group adoption and durability path.

Acceptance:

- A mob with an initial four-block impulse crossing east moves once,
  retaining the same post-move velocity as the corresponding westward or
  same-section mover. Destination section ordering cannot double its travel.
- Later group ticks still simulate the adopted mob normally; reconnect and
  persistence do not introduce a lasting simulation fence.
- No new per-feature transfer or receipt protocol is introduced.
- Actual-client testing remains deferred at the user's request.

Reference: Mojang 26.3 server SHA-1
`33680f5f2ac32864d6d7cf5e56a705fdb3e05f4c`, `EntityTickList.forEach`
iterates each entity id once; `ensureActiveIsNotIterated` preserves that
iteration while the live list changes. Moving across a chunk/section does
not add a second entity tick.

Reproduction from VMC-043e17's live group: a husk beginning at the section
edge minus 1.3 with velocity 4 ends at edge plus 6.34 rather than plus 2.7;
the additional 3.64 is its next friction-adjusted movement. Private evidence:
`.local/m3-group-mob-tick-before.log`. That private run loads the prior
`f003101d` store/owner modules in a separate VM and reproduces the failures
without changing the checkout or the running test server.

Implementation: the entity store owns in-memory ageing/simulation stamps,
shared by mobs, projectiles, TNT/falling blocks, dropped items and XP orbs.
Successful physics sets the simulation stamp without changing revisions or
views. Later sections skip that simulation and its ageing; an XP orb also
skips a second scan. Piston displacement remains an external move and does
not consume the entity's own tick.

Same-owner moves reuse the existing advance/withdraw/adopt path. They carry
the live body through the existing transient body carrier, plus unsaved
idle/lived counters and tick stamps. This preserves AI minds, school links,
combat windows and pending family state instead of reloading a saved body.
Adoption uses one shared store helper. Durable records and cross-owner
handoff ledgers retain their existing codecs; reloaded entries have no
simulation fence.

Validation: the previous modules fail four of the eight group regressions;
the fixed implementation passes all eight. Eastward, westward and same-
section husks now have equal movement and post-tick velocities, keep their
live AI markers, count one lived tick, and reduce hurt cooldowns once. The
next tick progresses normally. TNT, arrows, items and XP cross once with
their correct fuse/age and post-move velocity. Saved records contain none of
the transient state and reload unfenced. The full affected test selection
passes 57 tests; all ten affected native entity, mob, projectile, item/orb,
dragon and TCP stages pass (92 seconds, 13 cache hits). The TNT-travel and XP-orb native stages also pass. Evidence:
`.local/m3-group-{mob-tick-before,entity-tick-tests,entity-final-tests,entity-tick-stages,entity-tick-lifecycle}.log`.

Deferred actual-client scenario: a moving mob, thrown item, arrow and TNT
cross a section line without speeding up; a damaged mob keeps its combat
window while crossing. Repeat in both directions.

Closed with M3 (October 31, 2026): its client observations passed in the [gate run](VMC-078.md#gate-run-on-the-native-mob-core-october-30-2026) and the [VMC-083 run](VMC-083.md#client-run-october-31-release-67a9072e).
