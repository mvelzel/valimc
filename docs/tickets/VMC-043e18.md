# VMC-043e18 — One mob movement per group tick across sections

Status: In progress
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-043e17](VMC-043e17.md).

A mob that crosses from an earlier section into a later section of the same
group currently travels again in the destination's entity phase. Fence
simulation to once per entity per group tick while preserving the existing
whole-group adoption and durability path.

Acceptance:

- A mob with an initial four-block impulse crossing east moves once,
  retaining the same post-move velocity as the corresponding westward or
  same-section mover. Destination section ordering cannot double its travel.
- Later group ticks still simulate the adopted mob normally; reconnect and
  persistence do not introduce a lasting simulation fence.
- No new per-feature transfer or receipt protocol is introduced.
- Actual-client testing remains deferred at the user's request.

Reproduction from VMC-043e17's live group: a husk beginning at the section
edge minus 1.3 with velocity 4 ends at edge plus 6.34 rather than plus 2.7;
the additional 3.64 is its next friction-adjusted movement. Private evidence:
the dedicated group-tick regression will retain its own before/after log.
