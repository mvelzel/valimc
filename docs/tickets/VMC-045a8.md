# VMC-045a8 — Live crystal respawn and recovery

Status: Awaiting client
Milestone: M3
Depends on: [VMC-045a6](VMC-045a6.md), [VMC-045a7](VMC-045a7.md).

Four crystals around the defeated dragon's portal start the native ritual.
The origin owner retains the stage, clock and crystal UUIDs, while existing
group effects change their beams, rebuild the spikes, run explosions and
admit the next dragon. Interrupting or restarting the ritual preserves one
coherent fight and its rewards.

Acceptance:

- The full 604-tick native sequence is live: beams and events, ten cleared
  and regenerated pillars with protected crystals, the final explosions,
  a fresh dragon and reset spike crystals.
- Pillar geometry uses the existing generation kernel. CPU-heavy planning
  runs off the owner, stays bounded and is ready before the ritual needs it;
  owner admission uses existing group fencing and shared frames.
- A participating crystal's destruction aborts, resets spike beams/protection
  and restores the active portal. A persisted ritual resumes without duplicate
  dragons or rewards; an entirely missing crystal list aborts safely.
- Owner and native checks cover completion, abort and restart. The parent's
  client finale verifies the live sequence and repeated-kill rewards.

## Implementation and evidence (October 24, 2026)

The origin's saved fight stage, clock and crystal UUIDs drive the complete
ritual. Real item placement invokes the four cardinal-box admission in its
shared frame, including placements held by siblings. Each ordered arena
effect goes through the existing sibling-effect path; the group applies its
block and entity changes before the next effect. Each pillar therefore
clears its 21-cube, explodes, then regenerates, with no stale sibling read
between those operations. Empty explosion reaches start no crater worker.
Final blasts retain every participant position before earlier blasts can
remove later crystals.

Pillar decoration and regeneration visit one Rust geometry function. One
bounded origin job leases the world's existing generation context through
the generation pool and prepares ten immutable binary plans. The normal
604 accepted ritual ticks have ample preparation time; a late or restarted
worker can delay a pillar admission while other simulation continues.
Plans are transient, move with the section and rebuild after restart. Job
routing, deadlines, retries, block fencing and durable group frames reuse
the existing primitives. No separate transaction or gameplay process exists.

A participant's destruction aborts in its removal frame, resets protected
spike crystals and activates the portal. As native does, abort resets spike
beams, while surviving portal participants retain their last beam. A restored
list resolving to no participants also aborts; partial resolution remains
allowed. Final completion resets protection, consumes the participants and
admits the next dragon, preserving the previous-kill reward flag.

The expanded native arena check passes all 604 ticks, ten pillars' ordered
block writes, seven spike/gateway seeds and 54 boss packets. Two additional
owner scenarios pass the ten live pillars, restart after the first pillar,
a fresh UUID and exactly 500 repeated-kill XP; and a sibling participant's
abort plus an entirely missing restored list. Fixtures use flat End columns,
a defeated fight and a disclosed audience row; placement, effects, workers,
clock, shared frames and reload use the actual owner paths. Entity phases
can wait behind existing item plans, so the repeated-death scenario bounds
group ticks while the native actor retains its exact 200-tick death clock.
