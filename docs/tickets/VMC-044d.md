# VMC-044d — Illagers and patrols

Status: In progress
Milestone: M4
Depends on: [VMC-044c](VMC-044c.md).

Outposts and mansions place pillagers, vindicators and evokers, but with no
kind they stand inert, and no patrol ever spawns. After this ticket the
illagers fight as vanilla's do: vindicators with axes, pillagers with
crossbows, evokers with fangs, vexes and wololo, ravagers with their roar;
patrols find players outside villages, captains carry the ominous banner
and drop ominous bottles, and a totem of undying saves whoever holds it.

Slices:

- [VMC-044d1](VMC-044d1.md) — the raider base (patrol data and goals, the
  captain's banner, illager allies, patrol despawning) with the witch on it,
  and the vindicator.
- [VMC-044d2](VMC-044d2.md) — the pillager and the mob crossbow (charge,
  shot, multishot), its spawn crossbow and loot; outposts come alive.
- [VMC-044d3](VMC-044d3.md) — the evoker: the spellcaster base, fangs and
  wololo, the fang entity and its loot.
- [VMC-044d4](VMC-044d4.md) — vexes: no-physics flight, their goals,
  limited life, and the evoker's summon.
- [VMC-044d5](VMC-044d5.md) — the ravager: its attack, stun and roar,
  leaf breaking, and a shield's stun.
- [VMC-044d6](VMC-044d6.md) — patrols (`PatrolSpawner`), the ominous
  bottle and the totem of undying.

Acceptance (the backlog row): pillagers, vindicators, evokers with vexes
and fangs, ravagers and the totem; outposts and patrols; ominous bottles,
matching the oracle.

Out of scope (VMC-044e): raids, raid buffs, banner pickup, ravager riders,
door breaking, moving through the village and celebrating (their goals stay
idle), and Bad Omen turning into Raid Omen. The illusioner (no natural
spawn) is left out.
