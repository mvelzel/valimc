# VMC-081g2 — Resident natural spawning

Status: In progress
Milestone: M3, at the user's request
Parent: [VMC-081](VMC-081.md).
Depends on: [081d](VMC-081d.md), [081i](VMC-081i.md).

The spawning half of 081g ([081g1](VMC-081g1.md) holds the block ticks). After 081i natural spawning cost about 2.0 ms of
the `mobs` scenario's tick (probe at a75a314c, ms per tick): the kernel
1.31 (rows of all ~695 loaded mobs read from their sections 0.31,
`SpawnState` and the caps rebuilt 0.41, the chunk attempts over 441 chunks
0.57, despawn checks 0.02), the dirty-scheduler hop with the request's
decode and the reply's encode 0.20 (0.96 while exploring, when generation
holds the dirty schedulers), and the Elixir inputs 0.48 (`SpawnInputs.prepare`
0.13, spawner blocks 0.04, natives 0.04, kept starts 0.02 and the glue
around them). Every tick rebuilt what had not changed.

## Design

- **Resident inputs.** The group's `MobWorld` (`mob_tick.rs`) keeps a
  `spawn_world::Resident`: per section its Region resource and native mob
  section, its simulated and absent chunks as 64-bit masks, the rows and
  boxes of its Elixir mobs and objects, its spawner blocks (saved
  configuration, a stamp of the compound, the running countdown) and its
  kept starts of structures that override spawns.
  `World.SpawnInputs.sync/3` pushes a section's part
  (`mob_world_spawn_set/3`) only when its source term changed; a section
  none of whose sources changed costs one tuple comparison. A `seen` of
  another world, or none, pushes every section whole and drops the rest.
- **A census per mob section.** `MobSection` notes every mob on any
  mutable access (`get_mut`, `peek_mut`, `put`, `insert`, `remove`, a
  chunk change, `mobs_mut`), once until the next refresh. The spawn tick
  refreshes only the noted mobs (`valimc_core::spawning::census`): each
  mob's type (kept, since a slot's type never changes), its category
  unless persistent or from a bucket, its position's chunk, and per chunk
  the counts by category. Past `2 × mobs + 256` noted ids the census
  forgets them and rebuilds whole at its next refresh, so a section whose
  spawning does not run holds no growing list.
- **`createState` from counts.** The categories' counts sum the censuses'
  chunks and the Elixir rows; each player's local counts add a chunk's
  counts to the players near it (`getPlayersCloseForSpawning`, without
  allocating). The potential charges are gathered by a closure the
  `SpawnState` calls when a spawn's biome first has a cost for its type
  (`canSpawn`): only types with a spawn cost somewhere carry charges, so
  outside the Nether's cost biomes nothing is gathered.
- **Reads.** Blocks, light, heightmaps and biomes go through each
  section's store, locked when the tick first reads it, with the last
  chunk's column and the last biome query kept at hand (a chunk's
  categories read the same column, a pack's positions repeat their biome).
  A column keeps the WORLD_SURFACE heights read so far and forgets one on a
  write to that column, so the starts stop rescanning the same columns;
  `light_at` no longer allocates a list per read.
  The living bodies a new mob must not overlap are the players and the
  tick's spawns, plus a query of the resident ones near the box (the
  sections' Elixir boxes and the native mobs in the chunks the box can
  reach). Kept starts are looked up in the chunk's own section.
- **One normal call.** `mob_world_spawn` runs on a normal scheduler; its
  dirty twin only past about 500 simulated chunks (`@twins`, 2 µs a
  chunk). It returns the spawns, the despawned and idle-reset mobs with
  their sections, the randoms and the spawner blocks' spawns.

Vanilla's order of draws is unchanged: the chunks' `Util.shuffle`, each
chunk's categories, starts, packs, rules and yaws, the tropical fish's
schools, then `checkDespawn` and the spawner blocks. The despawn checks
now run section by section in key order (Elixir rows first, then native
mobs in id order); the shared despawn random (vanilla's is each mob's own)
draws in that order.

Two behaviours move closer to vanilla's `createState`, which counts every
loaded entity: the caps now count the mobs of every section of the group,
where they counted only the sections within one of a simulated one, and a
far mob's charge reads its own section's biome.

## Acceptance

- The census equals a rebuild over every mob after moves, persistence
  changes, bookkeeping changes, removals and an overflow
  (`mobs::section` tests, `spawning::census` tests).
- `natural_spawning_test`: the caps of one player, of two players in one
  group and of separate groups, despawning, spawner blocks (their
  countdown read back from the world) and structure overrides, over the
  resident path; `dragon_live_test`'s despawn of an ordinary hostile.
- The vanilla stages `natural_spawning`, `spawn_facts`, `hostile_spawns`,
  `aquatic_packs`, `bucket_mobs`, `mob_ai`, `animals` and `nether_mobs`
  pass.
- The `mobs` scenario's spawning call stays on a normal scheduler and its
  kernel and inputs stop scaling with the loaded mobs.

## Measurements

The `mobs` scenario (`bench/tick_budget.exs`, two pinned cores of the
development Mac's Linux VM, 60 s, about 160 active mobs), with the 081x
probe's spans (ms per tick). Before: a75a314c with the probe
(`probe081g-p3-before-1`); after: b6397ad3 with the probe
(`probe081g-p3-after-1`), run right after it. The host carried four other
sessions' benchmarks and vanilla runs (load 12 to 16), so both are slower
than the quiet probe at a75a314c (spawning 1.92 and 2.08 there); only the
pair compares.

| | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| Spawning phase (`g.spawn_mobs`) | 2.58 | 1.11 |
| of it: the NIF call with its boundary | 1.84 | 0.74 |
| of it: the kernel | 1.47 | 0.71 |
| rows of every mob / census refresh | 0.36 | 0.07 |
| `SpawnState` and caps | 0.39 | 0.06 |
| chunk attempts | 0.68 | 0.50 |
| despawn checks and spawner blocks | 0.03 | 0.07 |
| dirty hop, decode, encode | 0.36 | 0.03 |
| calls on a dirty scheduler | 1,198 of 1,198 | 0 of 1,194 |
| Elixir inputs (prepare, natives, spawners, kept / sync) | 0.34 | 0.22 |
| rest of the plan and the glue around it | 0.37 | 0.12 |
| TPS (mean / lowest second) | 19.92 / 17.96 | 19.84 / 15.97 |

The whole tick of either run says nothing under that load (the after
image also holds 081g1 and 081c2). An earlier after image without the kept
surface heights (`probe081g-v1-1`) gave spawning 1.15, chunk attempts 0.56,
census 0.14 and sync 0.17. Raw results in
`.local/tick-budget/results/probe081g-*.json`; the coordinator measures
the landed slices together on a quiet host.

Checks: `cargo test -p valimc_core` (census, section, column, spawning);
clippy without new warnings; `mix compile --warnings-as-errors`;
`natural_spawning_test`, `spawn_inputs_test`, `dragon_live_test`,
`phantom_spawner_test` and the native mob section, record, level, living,
animal and water tests; the vanilla stages `natural_spawning`,
`spawn_facts`, `hostile_spawns`, `aquatic_packs`, `bucket_mobs`, `mob_ai`,
`animals` and `nether_mobs`.

Left: the chunk attempts (0.5 ms for 441 chunks) are now the largest part;
a sampling profile of the real workload should say whether block reads,
light or the packs' rules dominate. The sync still walks a changed
section's entries for its Elixir rows (0.2 ms): once the Elixir mob path
is gone only objects remain, which a store index could push on change. The
despawn checks walk the simulated sections' slots.
